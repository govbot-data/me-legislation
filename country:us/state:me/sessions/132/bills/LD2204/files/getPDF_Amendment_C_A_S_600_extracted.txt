1 Sec. 1. Department of Education to develop guidance. Resolved: That the
2 Department of Education, referred to in this resolve as "the department," shall develop and
3 disseminate guidance for school administrative units and school leaders regarding the
4 implementation of supportive measures for students up to 11 years of age who exhibit or
5 are affected in school settings by challenging, disruptive or unsafe student behaviors,
6 including aggressive or sexualized behaviors.
7 Sec. 2. Scope of guidance. Resolved: That the guidance developed pursuant to
8 section 1 must include, but is not limited to, the following:
9 1. Developmentally appropriate responses to student behaviors for students in
10 preschool through grade 5;
11 2. Trauma-informed and restorative practices that emphasize prevention, de-escalation
12 and relationship building;
13 3. Supportive interventions designed to assist students exhibiting challenging,
14 disruptive or unsafe behaviors while maintaining the safety and well-being of other
15 students and school staff;
16 4. Strategies to support students affected by challenging, disruptive or unsafe
17 behaviors, including aggressive or sexualized behaviors, to ensure classroom supports and
18 communication with families;
19 5. Guidance on collaboration with school counselors, psychologists, social workers,
20 special education staff and community-based behavioral health providers;
21 6. Recommendations for professional development for educators and school
22 administrators related to early childhood behavioral development and positive behavioral
23 supports; and
24 7. Strategies to reduce exclusionary discipline for young students while maintaining
25 safe and orderly learning environments.
26 Sec. 3. Stakeholder consultation. Resolved: That, in developing guidance
27 pursuant to section 1, the department shall consult with stakeholders, including, but not
28 limited to, the following:
29 1. School administrators and educators from elementary schools;
30 2. School psychologists, counselors and social workers from elementary schools;
31 3. Experts in child development and behavioral health;
32 4. Parents and guardians of students up to 11 years of age;
33 5. Organizations representing educators and school personnel, including, but not
34 limited to, the Maine School Management Association and the Maine Education
35 Association; and
36 6. Representatives of child advocacy and mental health organizations.
37 Sec. 4. Dissemination and technical assistance. Resolved: That the
38 department shall disseminate the guidance developed pursuant to section 1 to school
39 administrative units no later than the beginning of the 2026-2027 school year. The
40 department shall make the guidance publicly available and provide technical assistance to
41 school administrative units to support implementation of the recommended practices.

23 SUMMARY
2244 This amendment replaces the bill with a resolve. The amendment directs the
2255 Department of Education to develop guidance related to supportive measures for young
2266 students who exhibit or are affected by challenging, disruptive or unsafe behaviors in
2277 school settings. The amendment provides that the guidance must include, but is not limited
2288 to, developmentally appropriate behavioral responses for young students; trauma-informed
2299 and restorative practices; supportive interventions; strategies to support students affected
3300 by challenging, disruptive or unsafe behaviors, including aggressive or sexualized
3311 behaviors; guidance on collaboration with appropriate school personnel and mental health
3322 professionals; recommendations for professional development; and strategies to reduce
3333 exclusionary discipline for young students. The amendment requires the department to
3344 develop this guidance in consultation with stakeholders. The department must disseminate
3355 the guidance to schools no later than the beginning of the 2026-2027 school year and
3366 provide technical assistance to support implementation of recommended practices. The
3377 department must also submit a report to the joint standing committee of the Legislature
3388 having jurisdiction over education matters by January 15, 2027 summarizing the guidance.
