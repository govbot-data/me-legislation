18 RETIREMENT IMPROVEMENT FUND
19 §17446. Retirement Improvement Fund
20 1. Establishment of fund. The Retirement Improvement Fund, referred to in this
21 section as "the fund," is established within and administered by the retirement system as an
22 interest-bearing, nonlapsing fund for the payment of retirement benefit improvements.
23 2. Content of fund. The fund consists of all money transferred to the fund under this
24 section and other money made available to the fund, which may include but is not limited
25 to legislative appropriations from the General Fund or any other fund, the unappropriated
26 surplus of the General Fund or any other source or fund identified or established through
27 law.
28 3. Use of fund. Money in the fund must be used to pay for retirement benefit
29 improvements. Whenever the fund has sufficient capital to pay for an increase in the
30 amount of at least $1,000 on the benefit base on which the annual cost-of-living adjustment
31 is applied pursuant to section 17806, subsection 1, paragraph A, for all beneficiaries in the
32 retirement system, that increase must be applied but only until the benefit base reaches
33 $40,000.

13 state employees, teachers and their beneficiaries, to the greatest extent possible within
14 existing state resources, including the future use of current state appropriations dedicated
15 to the payment of the unfunded actuarial liability when those funds are no longer needed
16 for payment of the existing liability.
