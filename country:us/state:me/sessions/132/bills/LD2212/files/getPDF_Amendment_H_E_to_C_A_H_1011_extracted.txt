20 §26001. Short title
21 This chapter may be known and cited as "the Public Safety and Protection Act."
22 §26002. Definitions
23 As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise indicates, the following terms
24 have the following meanings.
25 1. Federal immigration agency. "Federal immigration agency" means the United
26 States Department of Justice, the United States Department of Homeland Security or any
27 division within either of those departments, including but not limited to United States
28 Immigration and Customs Enforcement, United States Customs and Border Protection or
29 any successor agency or any other federal agency charged with the enforcement or
30 administration of immigration or border control.

4 §26003. Prohibited practices regarding immigration enforcement
5 A state government entity, local government entity or law enforcement agency may not
6 adopt or maintain a law, ordinance, resolution, rule, regulation, policy, directive, order,
7 practice or procedure, formal or informal, written or unwritten, that prohibits or materially
8 restricts the state government entity, local government entity or law enforcement agency
9 from complying with or assisting in the enforcement of immigration law, including, but not
10 limited to, prohibiting or materially restricting the state government entity, local
11 government entity or law enforcement agency from prohibiting or otherwise materially
12 restricting any state government entity, local government entity or law enforcement agency
13 from assisting in the enforcement of immigration law. This section applies to prohibitions
14 or restrictions on:
15 1. Inquiries into status. Inquiries into the immigration status of any person;
16 2. Information relating to status. Transmitting, requesting or receiving information
17 relating to immigration status, lawful or unlawful, of any person to or from any federal
18 immigration agency;
19 3. Maintaining information. Maintaining, archiving or otherwise storing for
20 subsequent use information relating to a person's immigration status;
21 4. Exchanging information. Exchanging information relating to immigration status
22 with another local government entity or state government entity or a federal immigration
23 agency;
24 5. Complying with detainer. Complying with an immigration detainer, including,
25 but not limited to, refusing to cooperate or comply with a lawfully issued immigration
26 detainer in the absence of a warrant or other order directing compliance with or
27 enforcement of such an immigration detainer;
28 6. Complying with request to notify. Complying with a request from a federal
29 immigration agency to notify the agency before the release of an inmate;
30 7. Providing incarceration status. Providing a federal immigration agency with an
31 inmate's incarceration status or release date;
32 8. Assisting federal immigration agency. Assisting or cooperating with a federal
33 immigration agency, including by providing enforcement assistance;
34 9. Participating in program. Participating in any program or agreement authorized
35 under the federal Immigration and Nationality Act, 8 United States Code, Section 1357(g);
36 and
37 10. Permitting officer to enter jail. Permitting a federal immigration officer to enter
38 and conduct enforcement activities at a municipal jail, county jail, state prison or other
39 correctional facility involving or related to the enforcement of federal immigration laws.

5 2. Attorney General investigation. After receiving a complaint or request under

8 a violation of section 26003 has occurred, the Attorney General shall issue an opinion that

11 3. Review of Attorney General findings. If the Attorney General issues an opinion
12 pursuant to subsection 2 that a government entity has violated section 26003, the
13 government entity has 30 days from the date the opinion was issued to contest the finding
14 in Superior Court.
15 A. If the Superior Court affirms the Attorney General's findings, the court shall
16 immediately enjoin the policy or practice in violation of section 26003. A government
17 entity that fails to comply with an injunction is subject to a fine of $500 for each day
18 the policy or practice of the government entity remains in effect.
19 B. If the Superior Court overturns the Attorney General's findings, the Attorney
20 General shall immediately certify that the government entity is in compliance with
21 section 26003.
22 §26005. Duty to report
23 A state government entity official, local government entity official, law enforcement
24 agency official or person holding public office shall report a violation of section 26003 by
25 submitting a complaint to the Attorney General pursuant to section 26004, subsection 1. A
26 person who reports a violation under this section is protected under the federal
27 Whistleblower Protection Act of 1989.
28 §26006. Implementation
29 This chapter must be implemented in a manner consistent with federal laws regulating
30 immigration, protecting the civil rights of all persons and respecting the privileges and
31 immunities of citizens of the United States. In complying with the requirements of this
32 chapter, a government entity may not consider a person's race, color or national origin,
33 except to the extent permitted by the United States Constitution and the Constitution of
34 Maine.
