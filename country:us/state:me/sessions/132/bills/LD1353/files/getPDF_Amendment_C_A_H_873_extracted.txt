13 SUMMARY
1144 This amendment replaces the bill. It authorizes the Commissioner of Marine Resources
1155 to issue a 2026 resident commercial menhaden fishing license or a 2026 nonresident
1166 commercial menhaden fishing license to an individual if that individual reported legal
1177 landings of menhaden of 20,000 pounds or more under a commercial menhaden fishing
1188 license in at least one of the following years, 2019, 2020 or 2021. The individual must also
1199 have possessed a license to fish commercially for menhaden in at least one of the following
2200 years, 2019, 2020 or 2021, and may not have qualified for or received a license for any
2211 period in 2023. The amendment establishes that no more than 350 commercial menhaden
2222 licenses are to be issued and the fishery has an exit ratio of one to one. Finally, the
2233 amendment directs the Department of Marine Resources to identify groups that should be
2244 prioritized for the first available licenses and develop an entry plan to provide the first
2255 opportunity for licenses to individuals in the identified groups and report findings to the
2266 joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over marine resources
2277 matters by January 1, 2027.
