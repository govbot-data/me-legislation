Resolve, to Rename a Bridge in West Forks Plantation and The Forks
Plantation the Gordon Clifton Berry, Sr. Bridge
Sec. 1. Bridge in West Forks Plantation and The Forks Plantation
renamed. Resolved: That the Department of Transportation shall designate Bridge
2841 on Route 201, which crosses the Kennebec River between West Forks Plantation and
The Forks Plantation and is currently known as the Forks Bridge, the Gordon Clifton Berry,
Sr. Bridge.
