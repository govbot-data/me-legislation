17 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
18 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
19 Whereas, certain individuals who receive services under the MaineCare waiver
20 program serving individuals with intellectual disabilities or autism spectrum disorder and
21 have resided in 2-person group homes for 5 years or longer face eviction because suitable
22 housemates have not been identified to fill vacancies as required by current rule; and
23 Whereas, immediate enactment of this resolve is necessary to update rules related to
24 filling residential vacancies in 2-person group homes to prevent certain individuals from
25 being evicted; and
26 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
27 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
28 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
29 therefore, be it

31 Benefits Manual; residential vacancies in 2-person group homes. Resolved:
32 That the Department of Health and Human Services, referred to in this resolve as "the
33 department," shall amend its rules in Chapter 101: MaineCare Benefits Manual, Chapter
34 II, Section 21, Home and Community Benefits for Members with Intellectual Disabilities
35 or Autism Spectrum Disorder pertaining to filling a residential vacancy in a 2-person group

11 SUMMARY
1122 This amendment does the following.
13 1. It removes from the resolve provisions that relate to vacancies in 2-person group
14 homes for persons with intellectual disabilities or autism spectrum disorder related to
15 hospitalizations.
16 2. It narrows the exception to those residents who have lived at the current address for
17 at least 5 years at the time of enactment of the resolve.
18 3. It requires the Department of Health and Human Services to seek federal approval
19 for the requirements of the resolve. If approval is not received, the requirements of the
20 resolve do not take effect.
21 4. It adds an emergency preamble and emergency clause to the resolve.
