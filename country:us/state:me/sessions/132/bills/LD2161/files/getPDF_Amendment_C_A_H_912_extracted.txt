27 SUMMARY
2288 This amendment clarifies that if a probation officer withdraws a motion to revoke
2299 probation, the probation officer must include in the request to withdraw the position of the
3300 prosecuting attorney regarding that request. The amendment also clarifies that if a motion
3311 to transfer a probation revocation hearing to another court is filed by a probation officer,
3322 the motion must state the position of the prosecuting attorney at the time of the motion and
3333 the prosecuting attorney receiving the transfer.
