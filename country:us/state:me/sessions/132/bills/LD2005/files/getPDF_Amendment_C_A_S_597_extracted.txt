9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 821, L.D. 2005, “An Act Regarding Mail

11 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
12 following:
13 'Sec. 1. 24-A MRSA §4349, sub-§7 is enacted to read:
14 7. Covered persons using mail order pharmacy; access to prescription drugs. If
15 a covered person is using a mail order pharmacy, the pharmacy benefits manager shall
16 allow for the dispensing of the covered person's prescription drug at a network pharmacy
17 under the following circumstances to ensure that the covered person has access to
18 prescription drugs.
19 A. If the prescription is delayed by more than one day after the expected delivery date
20 provided by the mail order pharmacy, the network pharmacy shall provide the covered
21 person up to a 7-day supply or the smallest prepackaged unit supply of the prescription
22 drug.
23 B. If the prescription drug arrives in an unusable condition, the network pharmacy
24 shall provide the prescription drug in accordance with the covered person’s
25 prescription.
26 A covered person that receives a prescription drug pursuant to paragraph A or B may not
27 be subject to any payment that exceeds the total of one copayment, coinsurance payment
28 or other out-of-pocket payment for the dispensing of that prescription drug.'
29 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
30 number to read consecutively.
