11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'Resolve, to Assess the Total Cost to the State of Greenhouse Gas Emissions'
13 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
14 'Sec. 1. Department of Environmental Protection; assessment of cost to
15 State of greenhouse gas emissions; report. Resolved: That the Department of
16 Environmental Protection shall conduct an assessment of the costs to the State of the
17 emission of greenhouse gases for the period that began on January 1, 1995 and ended on
18 December 31, 2024. The department may contract with a consultant to conduct the
19 assessment required by this section. The assessment must include:
20 1. A summary of the various cost-driving effects of greenhouse gas emissions on the
21 State, including effects on public health, natural resources, biodiversity, agriculture,
22 economic development, flood preparedness and safety, and any other effect that the
23 department determines is relevant;
24 2. A categorized calculation of the costs that have been incurred and are projected to
25 be incurred by the State of each of the effects identified under subsection 1; and
26 3. A categorized calculation of the costs that have been incurred and are projected to
27 be incurred by the State to abate the effects of greenhouse gas emissions from between
28 January 1, 1995 and December 31, 2024 on the State and residents of the State.
29 On or before January 1, 2028, the department shall submit a report containing the
30 results of the assessment conducted pursuant to this section to the joint standing committees
31 of the Legislature having jurisdiction over environment and natural resources matters, labor
32 matters, judiciary matters and energy, utilities and technology matters. After reviewing the
33 report, the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over environment
34 and natural resources matters may report out a bill relating to the report to the Second
35 Regular Session of the 133rd Legislature.
