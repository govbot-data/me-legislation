27 SUMMARY
2288 This amendment strikes from the bill the repeal of the academic standing requirement
2299 in determining student eligibility for secondary school students receiving a state subsidy
3300 for postsecondary courses and instead requires a student to maintain a minimum secondary
3311 school grade point average of at least 2.0, rather than 3.0 as in current law, on a scale of
3322 4.0 unless the student is granted a waiver by the relevant public postsecondary education
3333 institution in the State. The amendment repeals this provision July 31, 2028.
34 The bill amends current law regarding the required recommendation for such a state
35 subsidy to provide that a student is required to receive a recommendation from the student's
36 school to take a postsecondary course at such a public postsecondary education institution.
37 The amendment clarifies that the student may also receive a recommendation from a school
38 official with knowledge of the student's academic performance.
39 The amendment eliminates on July 31, 2028 the lifetime credit caps proposed in the
40 bill for secondary school students and career and technical education students in a
41 designated early college career pathway.
