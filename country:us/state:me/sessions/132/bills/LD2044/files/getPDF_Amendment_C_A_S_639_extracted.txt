1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment replaces the bill, changes the title and amends the tax laws by making
33 the biofuel commercial production and commercial use tax credit and the renewable
44 chemicals tax credit refundable for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2027. The
55 amendment limits a person's eligibility to generate a credit under the Maine Revised
66 Statutes, Title 36, sections 5219-X and 5219-XX to a period not to exceed 10 consecutive
77 taxable years as of the first tax year beginning on or after January 1, 2021 for which the
88 credit is allowed. The amendment requires the Department of Economic and Community
99 Development to report to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having
1100 jurisdiction over taxation matters 5 years after the credits become refundable and authorizes
1111 the committee to report out legislation related to the credits allowed under Title 36, sections
1122 5219-X and 5219-XX after receiving the report.
