10 SUMMARY
1111 This amendment amends Committee Amendment "A" to:
12 1. Exempt from the definition of "collect" activities between a controller and a
13 processor and between a processor and its subcontractors;
14 2. Remove from the definition of "process" the exclusion of the collection of personal
15 data;
16 3. Add to the definition of "processor" the collection of personal data;
17 4. Remove from the definition of "publicly available information" the exclusion of
18 information used to create a consumer profile that is publicly available, information
19 available for sale and inferences related to such information;
20 5. Add as an exempt entity a political organization;
21 6. Delay the effective date of the Maine Online Data Privacy Act from September 1,
22 2026, as proposed in Committee Amendment "A," to September 1, 2027 and make other
23 date changes to accommodate that delay, including:
24 A. The date by which a consumer must be allowed to opt out of the collection or
25 processing of data, September 1, 2027;
26 B. The cutoff date for a violation that the controller or processor may cure without
27 enforcement by the Attorney General, April 1, 2028; and
28 C. The date the Attorney General is first required to submit a report to the joint standing
29 committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over judiciary matters, February 1,
30 2028. Subsequent reports are due annually by January 5th; and
31 7. Replace the appropriation and allocation section in Committee Amendment "A" to
32 change the source of the funding.
