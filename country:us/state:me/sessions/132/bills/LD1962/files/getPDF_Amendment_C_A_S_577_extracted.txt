16 'Sec. 1. 34-A MRSA §3001-A, sub-§1, as amended by PL 2023, c. 383, §2, is
17 further amended to read:
18 1. Appointment. The Except as provided in paragraph H, the Governor shall appoint
19 a board of 7 visitors for each correctional facility under the department, as authorized by
20 Title 5, section 12004‑I, subsection 5.
21 A. The terms of the members of the boards of visitors are for 3 years.
22 B. Members of the boards of visitors are eligible for reappointment at the expiration
23 of their terms.
24 C. A member of the Legislature or an employee of the department may not serve on
25 any board of visitors.
26 D. At least one member of each board must be a person licensed by this State to provide
27 mental health services.
28 E. Each member of the boards of visitors must be compensated according to the
29 provisions of Title 5, chapter 379.
30 F. The Governor shall appoint a chair from the membership.
31 G. At least one member of each board of visitors must have been incarcerated in a
32 correctional facility.

1 H. If a vacancy occurs within a board of visitors, the commissioner shall provide notice
2 to the Governor, the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of
3 Representatives within 5 days of the vacancy. A vacancy on a board of visitors under
4 this section must be filled by the Governor within 90 days of the notice of vacancy. If
5 the Governor fails to appoint a member within 90 days of the notice of vacancy, the
6 President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives shall jointly
7 appoint a member to fill the vacancy pursuant to this subsection.
8 Sec. 2. 34-A MRSA §3001-B is enacted to read:
9 §3001-B. Legislator access to correctional facilities and detention facilities
10 Each Legislator assigned to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having
11 jurisdiction over corrections matters must be provided open access to all physical areas of
12 a correctional facility and detention facility, including access to areas housing clients. A
13 Legislator must be provided the opportunity to speak to clients and facility staff. A
14 Legislator shall adhere to all federal and state laws regarding confidentiality. The chief
15 administrative officer of a facility may only prohibit a Legislator's access to a facility if an
16 emergency situation is certified pursuant to section 3005.'
17 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
18 number to read consecutively.
19 SUMMARY
2200 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, changes the title and
2211 replaces the bill. The amendment requires the Commissioner of Corrections to provide
2222 notice to the Governor, the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of
2233 Representatives within 5 days of a vacancy on a board of visitors. If the vacancy is not
2244 filled by the Governor within 90 days, the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the
2255 House of Representatives must jointly appoint a member to fill the vacancy. The
2266 amendment also permits a sitting Legislator of the joint standing committee of the
2277 Legislature having jurisdiction over corrections matters to access all physical areas of
2288 correctional facilities and the Long Creek Youth Development Center except when an
2299 emergency situation has been certified.
