10 SUMMARY
1111 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, changes the title of
1122 and replaces the bill and makes the following changes to the beverage container redemption
1133 laws.
14 1. Like the bill, it directs a portion of unclaimed beverage container deposits toward
15 investment in the Lake Water Quality Restoration and Protection Fund and the Maine
16 Working Farmland Access and Protection Program beginning October 1, 2026. The
17 amendment does not include the provision in the bill directing a portion of unclaimed
18 beverage container deposits toward the Maine Commission on Public Defense Services to
19 support regional public defender office staffing.
20 2. It further amends the provisions of law regulating unclaimed beverage container
21 deposits to provide that if the total amount of unclaimed deposit expenditures required by
22 law to be made in any calendar year exceeds the total amount of unclaimed deposits that
23 are available for expenditure in that same calendar year, the cooperative may reduce the
24 amount of the expenditures required to be directed to the Lake Water Quality Restoration
25 and Protection Fund and the Maine Working Farmland Access and Protection Program by
26 the amount of that excess.
27 3. It clarifies under the provisions of law regulating unclaimed beverage container
28 deposits that unclaimed deposits for beverage containers managed by the Department of
29 Administrative and Financial Services, Bureau of Alcoholic Beverages and Lottery
30 Operations are the property of the bureau and are not subject to the requirements of the law
31 concerning unclaimed deposits and that the disposition and use of those deposits are to be
32 determined by the bureau. The amendment stipulates that the bureau remains responsible
33 for its apportioned share of the costs incurred by the commingling cooperative in managing
34 the single commingling program as determined pursuant to the approved program plan.
35 4. It amends the provision of law requiring annual reporting by initiators of deposit
36 regarding deposit transactions to provide that an initiator that fails to submit that annual
37 report to the department commits a violation of the beverage container redemption laws, is
38 subject to applicable penalties under those laws and, as long as the violation exists, is
39 prohibited from selling or distributing in the State any beverage container subject to the
40 requirements of the beverage container redemption laws. A distributor or dealer may not
