6 SUMMARY
77 This amendment replaces the bill. The amendment makes the following changes to the
88 laws governing recruitment and retention adjustments for state employees in the classified
99 service.
10 1. It requires a state agency or bargaining agent to file a request for a recruitment and
11 retention adjustment with the Department of Administrative and Financial Services, Bureau
12 of Human Resources on forms prescribed by the bureau.
13 2. It establishes that a recruitment and retention adjustment may be authorized by the
14 State Human Resources Officer only when justified by prescribed conditions. It also,
15 subject to certain exceptions, requires a committee to be established to evaluate each
16 request from a state agency or bargaining agent for a recruitment and retention adjustment.
17 3. It establishes a preliminary review process to be completed by the State Human
18 Resources Officer when a request for a recruitment and retention adjustment is filed. The
19 purpose of the preliminary review is to determine the extent to which vacancy rates,
20 turnover rates or other workforce indicators identified in the request are attributable to
21 certain conditions that would not require a committee to be formed to evaluate the request.
22 This preliminary review process must be completed within 30 days following the receipt
23 of the request.
24 4. It requires a recruitment and retention adjustment to be reviewed at least every 4
25 years in alignment with the market pay study required pursuant to the Maine Revised
26 Statutes, Title 5, section 7061, subsection 4, paragraph B and adjusted to changes in the
27 labor market or the overall relation of the standard pay policy to the specialized labor
28 market.
29 5. It establishes an appeals process for decisions of the State Human Resources Officer.
30 6. It establishes that if resources within a department or agency budget do not exist to
31 fund an approved adjustment, the State Human Resources Officer must submit the cost
32 items for inclusion in the Governor's next operating budget. If the Legislature does not
33 appropriate the requested funds, the department or agency is not obligated to make the
34 adjustment.
