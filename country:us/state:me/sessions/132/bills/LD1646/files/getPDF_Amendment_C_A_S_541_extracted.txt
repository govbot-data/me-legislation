11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:

15 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the

17 'Sec. 1. 25 MRSA §2804-C, sub-§2-H is enacted to read:
18 2-H. Training regarding procedures for responding to call for assistance for
19 suspected drug-related overdose. Beginning January 1, 2028, the board shall include in
20 the basic law enforcement training program a block of instruction on appropriate
21 procedures for responding to a call for assistance for a suspected drug-related overdose.
22 The training must include information regarding identifying symptoms of a drug-related
23 overdose and a protected person's immunity from arrest for a violation of law pursuant to
24 Title 17-A, section 1111-B. The training must be developed and provided within existing
25 resources and through collaboration between a statewide advocacy organization
26 specializing in substance use disorder and a tier 1 distributor of naloxone hydrochloride
27 under the Maine naloxone distribution initiative or successor organization. For the purposes
28 of this subsection, "protected person" has the same meaning as in Title 17-A, section
29 1111-B, subsection 1, paragraph B.

31 repealed and the following enacted in its place:
32 2. Role of board. The board shall establish in-service recertification training
33 requirements, consistent with subsection 1, and coordinate delivery of in-service training.
