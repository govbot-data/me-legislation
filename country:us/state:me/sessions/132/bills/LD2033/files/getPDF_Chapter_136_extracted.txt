Resolve, to Designate Route 3 in Trenton as the Deputy Luke Gross
Memorial Highway
Sec. 1. Designate Route 3 in Trenton the Deputy Luke Gross Memorial
Highway. Resolved: That the Department of Transportation shall designate Route 3 in
the Town of Trenton the Deputy Luke Gross Memorial Highway and shall erect appropriate
signs to identify Route 3 as the Deputy Luke Gross Memorial Highway.
