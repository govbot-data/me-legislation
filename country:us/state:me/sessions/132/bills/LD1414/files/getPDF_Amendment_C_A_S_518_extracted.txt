19 SUMMARY
2200 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill, which
2211 is a concept draft, with a resolve.
22 The amendment requires the Department of Health and Human Services to increase
23 reimbursement rates under the Maine Child Care Affordability Program no later than
24 January 1, 2027 so that reimbursement rates paid to child care centers and family child care
25 providers for the care of infants and toddlers are 20% higher than reimbursement rates for
26 the care of children 36 months of age or older.
27 The amendment also requires the Department of Health and Human Services to
28 establish a grant program to facilitate the availability of child care from 6:00 p.m. to 6:00
29 a.m. The department must establish the grant program so that directors or owners of child
30 care centers or family child care providers may begin applying no later than January 1,
31 2027 for funding awards for use in fiscal year 2027-28. The grants must be used by child
32 care providers to hire experts or consultants or dedicated staff to develop a plan for how a
33 provider would provide child care services for any portion of time from 6:00 p.m. to 6:00
34 a.m. A grant may not exceed $10,000 and is available to an applicant on a one-time basis.
35 The department is required to develop the grant program, including the criteria and process
36 for applications, awards and oversight and any other relevant requirements. As long as
37 sufficient funding has been appropriated for the purpose, the total amount for grants
38 awarded in fiscal year 2027-28 is up to a total of $500,000, but if the funding is expended
39 before June 30, 2028, no more grants may be awarded.
