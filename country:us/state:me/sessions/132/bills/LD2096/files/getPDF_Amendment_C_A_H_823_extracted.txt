1 In determining distribution under this subsection, an allowance may not be made on
2 premiums offered and paid by a licensee at any competition or event held other than during
3 the period in which its annual fair is held and at any competition or event judged up to 48
4 hours prior to the licensed fair dates, except that an allowance may not be made on
5 premiums offered and paid by a licensee at any competition or event under paragraphs A,
6 F-1, I, J and K judged up to 48 hours prior to the licensed fair dates. Allowance may not
7 be made or consideration given for lump sums, payments or premiums previously arranged
8 and agreed upon for the presentation and display of any animals or products without regard
9 to competition or event. The department may provide an allowance to licensees for
10 payments made for educational agricultural talks, workshops and demonstrations pursuant

18 SUMMARY
1199 This amendment clarifies that an agricultural fair licensee holding an event within one
2200 week of a fair's assigned fair dates is considered to be operating a fair outside of the fair
2211 dates assigned by the Commissioner of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry.
22 The amendment authorizes a fair licensee to host an exhibition up to 48 hours, rather
23 than 24 hours as proposed in the bill, prior to the assigned fair dates for the purposes of
24 displaying and awarding premiums.
25 The amendment amends the law governing the distribution of funds from the Stipend
26 Fund to agricultural fair licensees by allowing prizes paid by a licensee at any competition
27 or event judged up to 48 hours, rather than 24 hours as proposed in the bill, prior to the
28 licensed fair dates to be considered in calculating the distribution. The amendment also
29 provides that this provision does not apply to livestock and poultry competitions or events,
30 youth livestock and pulling competitions or events, pulling competitions or events or
31 educational agricultural talks, workshops and demonstrations.
32 The amendment also authorizes the Department of Agriculture, Conservation and
33 Forestry to use funds from the Stipend Fund to provide allowances to licensees for
34 payments made for educational agricultural talks, workshops and demonstrations without
35 regard to premiums paid for competitions or events in this category.
