15 SUMMARY
1166 This amendment makes the following changes to the bill.
17 1. It replaces a cross-reference in the bill with the definition of "advanced
18 communications technology infrastructure" in the laws governing connectivity
19 infrastructure.
20 2. It replaces references struck in the bill in the sections of the bill related to
21 development districts and related to municipal access to poles with references to definitions
22 established by the Maine Connectivity Authority. The amendment also adds a provision
23 requiring the Maine Connectivity Authority to establish criteria to define unserved and
24 underserved areas with respect to broadband service.
25 3. It replaces a reference to the state broadband policy with a reference to the state
26 connectivity goals.
27 4. It requires that notice of proposed facilities located underground and in excess of
28 500 feet in length be provided to the Maine Connectivity Authority instead of the
29 ConnectMaine Authority.
30 5. It adds an effective date of June 30, 2027.
31 6. It adds an appropriations and allocations section.
