33 SUMMARY
3344 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. The amendment requires
3355 anyone applying for or renewing a registry identification card or registration certificate
3366 under the Maine Medical Use of Cannabis Act, except for assistants, to submit to a criminal
3377 history record check. If a person applying for or renewing a registry identification card or
3388 registration certificate is a business entity, every officer or director of the business entity is
3399 also required to submit to a criminal history record check.
