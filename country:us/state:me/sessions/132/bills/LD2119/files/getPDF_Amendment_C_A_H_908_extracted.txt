9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1434, L.D. 2119, “An Act to Expand
10 Reimbursement for Treatment in Place, Community Paramedicine and Alternate
11 Destination Transport”
12 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
13 'Resolve, to Review Reimbursement for Certain Emergency Medical Services'
14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
15 'Sec. 1. Department of Health and Human Services to conduct review.
16 Resolved: That the Department of Health and Human Services shall review MaineCare
17 reimbursement for basic life support and advanced life support treatment provided by
18 ambulance service providers that does not result in transport of a patient. The department
19 shall submit its findings by January 1, 2027 to the joint standing committee of the
20 Legislature having jurisdiction over health and human services matters. The committee is
21 authorized to report out legislation related to the report to the 133rd Legislature in 2027.
22 Sec. 2. Department of Professional and Financial Regulation, Bureau of
23 Insurance to conduct review. Resolved: That the Department of Professional and
24 Financial Regulation, Bureau of Insurance shall conduct a review and evaluation of a
25 coverage requirement for alternate destination transport in accordance with the Maine
26 Revised Statutes, Title 24-A, section 2752, subsection 3. For the purposes of this resolve,
27 "alternate destination transport" means transportation of a patient to a facility other than a
28 hospital emergency department that is clinically appropriate for the patient's condition,
29 including, but not limited to, an urgent care center, federally qualified health center,
30 primary care practice, behavioral health crisis center, substance use treatment facility or
31 other clinically appropriate site. The bureau shall submit its findings by January 1, 2027
32 to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over health and
33 human services matters and to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having
34 jurisdiction over health coverage, insurance and financial services matters. Both
35 committees are authorized to report out legislation related to the report to the 133rd
36 Legislature in 2027.'
