3 SUMMARY
44 This amendment replaces the bill, changes the title and does the following.
5 1. Currently, the annual snowmobile registration fee for a resident is $55. The
6 amendment increases the registration fee for a registration issued December 16th of each
7 year to June 30th of the following year to $70.
8 2. Currently, the seasonal registration fee for a nonresident is $119. The amendment
9 increases the registration fee for a registration issued December 16th of each year to June
10 30th of the following year to $150.
11 3. The amendment also increases the nonresident 3-consecutive-day registration fee
12 from $74 to $94 and the nonresident 10-consecutive-day registration fee from $99 to $119.
13 4. The amendment also requires that snowmobile registration fees collected December
14 16th of each year to June 30th of the following year must be transferred to the Department
15 of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry. The amendment specifies how the funds must
16 be allocated, with a portion of the funds allocated to the Snowmobile Trail Fund of the
17 Department of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry, Bureau of Parks and Lands and a
18 portion of the funds allocated to a special fund administered by the Off-road Recreational
19 Vehicle Office of the bureau.
20 5. The amendment provides an effective date of July 1, 2027 for these changes.
