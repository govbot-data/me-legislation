An Act to Confirm and Finalize the Boundary Between the Town of Kittery
and the Town of York
Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
Whereas, the communities of Kittery and York have experienced a history of border
disputes and disagreement over the municipal boundary line between the 2 towns; and
Whereas, the 2 towns have reached a mutually agreed-upon description of their
common boundary in an amicable manner; and
Whereas, it is in the public interest to immediately acknowledge the amicable
settlement of the boundary line dispute between the 2 towns as mutually agreed upon; and
Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
therefore,
Be it enacted by the People of the State of Maine as follows:
Sec. 1. Boundary line. Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, the
boundary line between the Town of Kittery and the Town of York is as described:
