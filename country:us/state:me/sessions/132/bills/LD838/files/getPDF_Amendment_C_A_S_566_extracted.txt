20 1. Review any reports commissioned by the Department of Energy Resources related

22 SUMMARY
2233 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft, with a resolve. The
2244 amendment creates the Commission to Study Options for Public Financing of Transmission
2255 and Distribution Infrastructure, Generation Projects and Energy Storage Projects. The
2266 commission is charged with examining various aspects of public financing of energy
2277 projects, including approaches to public financing used by other states. By December 2,
2288 2026, the commission must submit a report to the joint standing committee of the
2299 Legislature having jurisdiction over energy matters that includes study findings and
3300 recommendations for public financing of transmission and distribution infrastructure,
3311 generation projects and energy storage projects. The committee may report out legislation
3322 related to the report to the 133rd Legislature in 2027.
