6 SUMMARY
77 This amendment requires that the State Controller transfer 14% of cigarette and
88 tobacco products tax revenue received in the fiscal year ending June 30, 2028 to the Fund
99 for a Healthy Maine. For each following year, the percentage of revenue that the State
1100 Controller must transfer to the Fund for a Healthy Maine is increased by one percentage
1111 point until the fiscal year ending June 30, 2034 when 20% of revenue received must be
1122 transferred to the Fund for a Healthy Maine. In subsequent fiscal years, the 20% transfer is
1133 maintained.
