13 Amend the bill by striking out all of the emergency preamble.
14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
15 following:
16 'Sec. 1. 30-A MRSA §951, sub-§1, as amended by PL 2003, c. 178, §2 and PL
17 2013, c. 16, §10, is further amended to read:
18 1. Annual audit. Every county shall have an audit made of its accounts annually
19 covering the last complete fiscal year by the Office of the State Auditor or by a certified
20 public accountant selected by the county commissioners. The audit must be performed in
21 accordance with generally accepted auditing standards and procedures pertaining to
22 governmental accounting. The auditor shall produce an audit report that includes the items
23 required in section 952‑A. When an audit is conducted by a certified public accountant,
24 the audit, upon completion, must be forwarded to the Office of the State Auditor within 12
25 months of the date of the close of the fiscal year. The Office of the State Auditor shall
26 review the audit for completeness. The audit, including the management letter, is a public
27 document and must be posted on the Office of the State Auditor's publicly accessible
28 website no later than 30 days after receipt.
29 The State Auditor is authorized to grant an exception to the requirement for a county to
30 forward a completed annual audit to the Office of the State Auditor within 12 months of
31 the date of the close of the fiscal year under extenuating circumstances as determined by
32 the State Auditor. The Office of the State Auditor shall make public any requests for an
33 exception along with the State Auditor's decision within 60 days of the receipt of a request
34 for an exception.'
