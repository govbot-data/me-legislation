15 SUMMARY
1166 This amendment makes the following changes to the bill.
17 1. It clarifies that the parties' spousal support income is calculated using the definition
18 of "gross income" under the child support statutes, regardless of whether there are any
19 children of the marriage. If there are children of the marriage, the spousal support income
20 of the party obligated to pay child support must be reduced based on the amount that party
21 is obligated to pay directly in support of the children of the marriage as reflected on the
22 child support worksheet and the spousal support income of the party receiving child support
23 must be reduced by the amount that party is determined to spend directly in support of
24 children of the marriage as reflected on the child support worksheet.
25 2. It increases the income disparity between the spouses required to trigger a
26 presumptive entitlement to general spousal support by providing that there is a rebuttable
27 presumption that a spouse is entitled to general spousal support if the parties have been
28 married for at least 10 years and that spouse's spousal support income is an amount equal
29 to or less than 65% of the other spouse's spousal support income.
30 3. It clarifies that, after a court calculates a presumptive spousal support award in
31 appropriate cases using the formula set forth in the amendment, the court must consider the
32 factors set forth in the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 19-A, section 951-A, subsection 5 and
33 any other fact the court determines relevant in deciding whether that presumptive spousal
34 support amount would be equitable or unjust. It also amends the list of factors to require a
35 court to consider whether the incomes of the parties are sufficiently low or sufficiently high
36 that application of the presumptive general spousal support calculation is inequitable or
37 unjust.
38 4. It clarifies that the court must make written findings supporting any decision that
39 any of the general spousal support rebuttable presumptions have been rebutted because its
40 application would be inequitable or unjust.
41 5. It clarifies that the court may enter a separate spousal support award that will take
42 effect upon expiration of a child support obligation that is expected to expire within 3 years
