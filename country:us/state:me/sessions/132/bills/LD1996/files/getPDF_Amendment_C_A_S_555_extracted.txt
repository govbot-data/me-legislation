18 SUMMARY
1199 This amendment replaces the bill. It shifts from July 1, 2025 to July 1, 2028 the
2200 compliance date for the Department of Health and Human Services to create an Internet-
2211 based, real-time database containing the information necessary to properly determine an
2222 applicant's eligibility for general assistance. It requires the department to report to the joint
2233 standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over health and human services
2244 matters by February 1, 2027 on its progress toward building a statewide general assistance
2255 database. It also adds an appropriations and allocations section providing $2,800,000 in
2266 one-time funding to the department to build the database and $450,000 in ongoing funding
2277 to maintain the database.
