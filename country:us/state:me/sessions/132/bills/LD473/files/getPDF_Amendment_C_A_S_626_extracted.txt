14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
15 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
16 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
17 Whereas, the potato industry is a cornerstone of Maine's agricultural heritage,
18 contributing over $1,300,000,000 annually to the State's economy, supporting more than
19 6,500 local jobs and driving agricultural innovation; and
20 Whereas, an uncontrolled outbreak of dangerous plant disease or invasive insect pests
21 would result in irreparable economic loss, jeopardize food security and undermine the
22 stability of our rural communities, and it is necessary that this resolve take effect
23 immediately to safeguard Maine's agricultural future; and
24 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
25 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
26 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
27 therefore, be it

30 of Colorado until the department has finally adopted an amended version of rule Chapter
31 30: Quarantine (Potato), a routine technical rule of the Department of Agriculture,
32 Conservation and Forestry, in accordance with section 2.
33 Sec. 2. Department of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry to amend
34 rules. Resolved: That, on or before May 31, 2026, the Department of Agriculture,

24 SUMMARY
2255 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft, with a resolve that directs
2266 the Department of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry to temporarily prohibit the
2277 importation of seed potatoes from the State of Colorado until the department has finally
2288 adopted an amended version of rule Chapter 30: Quarantine (Potato), a routine technical
2299 rule of the Department of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry. The amendment directs
3300 the department to initiate rulemaking on or before May 31, 2026. The amendment also
3311 directs the department to continue to collaborate with representatives of the potato industry
3322 to prevent, to the extent practicable, the introduction of harmful potato pests that may affect
3333 the health, yield and marketability of the State's potato crops.
34 The amendment requires the department to submit a report, no later than February 2,
35 2027, to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over
36 agriculture, conservation and forestry matters on current standards for certification of seed
37 potatoes in the State, including, but not limited to, importation requirements, testing
38 requirements and existing quarantines prohibiting or restricting the transportation of seed
39 potatoes capable of carrying plant disease or insect infestation. The amendment authorizes
40 the joint standing committee to submit legislation to the 133rd Legislature in 2027 based
41 on the subject matter of the report.
