33 SUMMARY
3344 This amendment changes the title of and replaces the bill.
35 1. It removes hospice services from the bill. Physicians who are licensed out of state
36 issuing orders in this State are limited to home health care services.
37 2. It provides that home health care providers who provide services to patients that
38 have orders from a physician licensed in another state must be certified by the United States
39 Department of Health and Human Services, Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services,
40 in addition to being licensed by the Department of Health and Human Services.

1 3. It removes the requirement from the bill that, for a patient in this State that has a
2 primary care provider, the physician licensed in another state must contact that primary
3 care provider.
4 4. It provides that an order for home health care services from a physician licensed in
5 another state is limited to 90 days.
6 5. It clarifies that the physician licensed in another state may provide telehealth
7 services only to the patient for which the order has been issued.
8 6. It clarifies that the physician licensed in another state may order services only from
9 a home health care provider that is certified by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid
10 Services and may only order services while the patient is receiving those services from
11 such a provider.
12 7. It clarifies that a physician licensed in another state is exempt from licensure by the
13 Maine Board of Licensure in Medicine or the Maine Board of Osteopathic Licensure for
14 this limited purpose. A physician who violates this limited exemption may lose the
15 exemption.
