Resolve, to Name the New East Main Street Bridge in Dover-Foxcroft in
Honor of Private Willard Merrill and Private Barton Merrill, Jr.
Sec. 1. Bridge in Dover-Foxcroft named. Resolved: That the Department of
Transportation shall designate Bridge 2293 on East Main Street in the Town of Dover-
Foxcroft, formerly known as the Foxcroft West Bridge, the Private Willard D. Merrill and
Private Barton G. Merrill, Jr. Bridge.
