COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 816, L.D. 2002
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment, which is the unanimous report of the committee, changes the repeal
33 date for the Maine Lobster Marketing Collaborative from December 31, 2031, as in the
44 bill, to December 31, 2028. It also establishes that collaborative members serve staggered
55 terms and directs the Department of Marine Resources to submit a report to the joint
66 standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over marine resources matters no
77 later than January 1, 2028 with an update on the collaborative and work accomplished by
88 the collaborative in 2026 and 2027 and authorizes the committee to report out a bill based
99 on that report to the Second Regular Session of the 133rd Legislature.
