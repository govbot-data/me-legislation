14 §513-B. Veterans' Housing Provider Incentive Program; establishment; report
15 The Veterans' Housing Provider Incentive Program is established to provide grants to
16 organizations to support permanent housing for veteran populations.
17 1. Fund established. The Veterans' Housing Provider Incentive Fund, referred to in
18 this section as "the fund," is established under the bureau for the purpose of receiving funds
19 from state, federal and other sources, including donations from private citizens,
20 corporations and entities for the purposes of this section.
21 2. Grants to support permanent housing. The bureau shall use the fund to award
22 grants to organizations that have been determined by the United States Internal Revenue
23 Service to be exempt from taxation under the United States Internal Revenue Code of 1986,
24 Section 501(c)(3) and have as a core program supporting permanent housing for veteran
25 populations. An organization awarded a grant under this section may use the funding for
26 housing provider incentives, risk mitigation payments and past rental debt payments for
27 veterans experiencing homelessness.
28 3. Rules. The bureau may adopt rules necessary to implement this section, including
29 requirements for organizations to report on the use of grant funding received under this
30 section. Rules adopted under this subsection are routine technical rules as defined in Title
31 5, chapter 375, subchapter 2-A.
32 4. Report. The bureau shall submit a report by February 15, 2027, and by February
33 15th in any year in which a grant is issued, regarding grants awarded to organizations under
34 this section, including information from the organizations on their use of that funding, to
35 the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over veterans affairs.
