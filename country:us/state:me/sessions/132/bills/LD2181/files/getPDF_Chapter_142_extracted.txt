Resolve, to Rename a Bridge in Roxbury the Roxbury-Frye Veterans
Memorial Bridge
Sec. 1. Bridge in Roxbury renamed. Resolved: That the Department of
Transportation shall designate Bridge 2300 on Frye Crossover Road in the Town of
Roxbury, currently known as the Frye Bridge, the Roxbury-Frye Veterans Memorial
Bridge.
