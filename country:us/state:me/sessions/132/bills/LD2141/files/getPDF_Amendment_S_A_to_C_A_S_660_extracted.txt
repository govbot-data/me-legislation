2200 This amendment changes the title and amends Committee Amendment "A" by striking
2211 all of its provisions except for the provision that provides the Department of Environmental
2222 Protection with authority to enforce the requirement for annual reporting data regarding
2233 unclaimed deposits for beverage containers. This provision provides that an initiator of
2244 deposit that fails to submit an annual report regarding deposit transactions commits a
2255 violation of the beverage container redemption laws, is subject to applicable penalties under
2266 those laws and, as long as the violation exists, is prohibited from selling or distributing in
2277 the State any beverage container subject to the requirements of the beverage container
2288 redemption laws. A distributor or dealer may not sell or distribute in the State any
2299 containers of the initiator, and the department may remove from sale any such containers
3300 of the initiator.
