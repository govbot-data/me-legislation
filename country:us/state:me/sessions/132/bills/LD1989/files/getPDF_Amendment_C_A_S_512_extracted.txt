18 SUMMARY
1199 This amendment replaces the bill and adds an emergency preamble and emergency
2200 clause. It changes eligibility for reimbursement from the Progressive Treatment Program
2211 Fund by replacing the term "private entities" with a list of individuals eligible to receive
2222 reimbursements, including the superintendent or chief administrative officer of a private
2233 psychiatric hospital, the director of an ACT team, a private medical practitioner, a law
2244 enforcement officer or the legal guardian of the patient who is the subject of an application
2255 to a progressive treatment program. It also allows reimbursement for legal costs incurred
2266 to extend an existing progressive treatment program. It adds language stating that a private
2277 attorney representing an individual eligible to receive funds may also be reimbursed. It
2288 changes the cap for reimbursement from the fund from $800 to an annual cap of $3,500. It
2299 adds language stating that reimbursement is subject to available funds.
