13 SUMMARY
1144 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. The amendment requires the
1155 Director of the Office of Program Evaluation and Government Accountability to prepare
1166 and present a biennial briefing to the Government Oversight Committee on the
1177 confidentiality and nondisclosure policies, standards and procedures that the office adheres
1188 to when conducting program evaluations. The amendment also permits the director to share
1199 a confidential copy of a program evaluation report with the committee prior to its public
2200 release but not before the review and response period of the evaluated agency or other
2211 entity is completed. The amendment also removes the emergency preamble and emergency
2222 clause.
