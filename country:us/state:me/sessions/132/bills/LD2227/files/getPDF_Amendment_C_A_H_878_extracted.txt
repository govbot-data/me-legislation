15 §1310-Y. Financial assurance
16 An owner or operator of a solid waste processing facility that processes plastic waste
17 through chemical plastic processing that is licensed under section 1310‑N or of a solid
18 waste disposal facility that is licensed under section 1310‑N shall provide the department
19 assurance of its financial ability to satisfy the estimated cost of corrective action for known
20 releases from the facility and its financial capacity to satisfy the estimated cost of closure
21 and postclosure care and maintenance at the facility for a period of at least 30 years after
22 closure. The board may adopt rules that increase or decrease that postclosure care period,
23 as long as those rules are consistent with applicable federal rules. The department may
24 consider the use of more than one acceptable form of financial assurance per facility to
25 satisfy the financial assurance requirement of this section. This section applies to all
26 privately owned solid waste disposal facilities licensed by the department, including
27 facilities licensed by the department before June 16, 1993, and to all solid waste processing
28 facilities that process plastic waste through chemical plastic processing. This section does
29 not apply to a municipally owned or operated solid waste disposal facility that accepts
30 exclusively special waste, construction and demolition debris, land-clearing debris or any
31 combination of those types of waste or to a municipally owned or operated solid waste
32 disposal facility licensed before June 16, 1993.

34 are under this section must include at least one of the following:
35 A. A letter of credit;
36 B. A surety bond;

26 update its financial assurance mechanisms under this section. The department may require
27 an applicant or licensee subject to the requirements of this section to conduct and submit
28 to the department an independent 3rd-party financial review of any proposed form of
29 financial assurance.
30 2. Report. An owner or operator of a solid waste processing facility that processes
31 plastic waste through chemical plastic processing or of a solid waste disposal facility shall
32 annually prepare a report containing a sworn statement providing the year-end balance or
33 value of any escrow, trust or reserve account form of financial assurance established under
34 this section. That report must be submitted to the commissioner by March 31st of each
35 year or such other date as the commissioner may designate.'
