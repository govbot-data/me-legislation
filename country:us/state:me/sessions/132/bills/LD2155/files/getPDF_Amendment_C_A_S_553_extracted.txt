9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 874, L.D. 2155, “An Act to Clarify
10 Certain Laws Governing Licenses for the Sale of Liquor by Manufacturers ”
11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'An Act to Direct the Bureau of Alcoholic Beverages and Lottery Operations to
13 Temporarily Suspend Enforcement of Public Law 2025, Chapter 87'
14 Amend the bill in the emergency preamble by striking out all of the 3rd and 4th
15 indented paragraphs (page 1, lines 6 to 15 in L.D.) and inserting the following:
16 'Whereas, several persons that hold a majority ownership interest in liquor
17 manufacturers that were licensed prior to the passage of the legislation limiting the number
18 of on-premises retail liquor licenses in which a person may hold no less than a majority
19 ownership interest are now in violation of the law; and
20 Whereas, this legislation needs to take effect as soon as possible to provide a
21 reasonable and clear time frame for these persons to come into compliance; and'
22 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and before the
23 emergency clause and inserting the following:
24 'Sec. 1. Temporary suspension of enforcement of limits on number of on-
25 premises retail liquor licenses liquor manufacturers may hold. Notwithstanding
26 any provision of law to the contrary, for a period of 24 months beginning on the effective
27 date of this Act, the Director of the Bureau of Alcoholic Beverages and Lottery Operations
28 within the Department of Administrative and Financial Services may not suspend, revoke
29 or refuse to renew a license issued under the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 28-A, section
30 1355-A or Title 28-A, chapter 43 that was initially issued prior to May 23, 2025 solely on
31 the basis that a person holding a majority ownership interest in the licensed manufacturing
32 facility was determined by the bureau after the license was initially issued to hold a majority
33 ownership interest in more than the allowable number, under Title 28-A, section 1355-A,
34 subsection 2, paragraph I, of on-premises retail licenses issued under Title 28-A, chapter
35 43.'

3 SUMMARY
44 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. The amendment provides that
55 the Director of the Bureau of Alcoholic Beverages and Lottery Operations within the
66 Department of Administrative and Financial Services may not, for a period of 24 months
77 beginning on the effective date of this legislation, suspend, revoke or refuse to renew a
88 manufacturing facility license or an on-premises retail license issued to a manufacturing
99 licensee that was issued prior to May 23, 2025 solely on the basis that a person holding a
1100 majority ownership interest in the licensed manufacturing facility was determined by the
1111 bureau after the license was initially issued to hold a majority ownership interest in more
1122 than the allowable number of on-premises retail licenses that may be issued per licensed
1133 manufacturing facility.
