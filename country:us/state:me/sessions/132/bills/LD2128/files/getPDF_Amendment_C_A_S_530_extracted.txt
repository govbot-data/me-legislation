22 SUMMARY
2233 This amendment clarifies that one member of the Emergency Medical Services' Board
2244 must represent a municipal fire department administration instead of a municipal
2255 administration as in the bill. The amendment also requires that the board's annual report
2266 required by the bill be submitted to an additional legislative committee, the joint standing
2277 committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over emergency medical services
2288 personnel licensing matters, and provides authority for that committee to report out
2299 legislation related to the recommendation of the board in its annual report.
