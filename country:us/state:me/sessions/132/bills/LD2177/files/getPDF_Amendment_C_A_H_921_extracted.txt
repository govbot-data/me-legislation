26 SUMMARY
2277 This amendment does the following.
28 1. It removes references in the requirement to increase reimbursement rates, to apply
29 cost-of-living increases or in response to a rate review, to the requirement's being subject
30 to available funds.
31 2. It requires the Department of Health and Human Services, when developing the
32 annual schedule of rate determination, to include the date of the most recent rate review
33 and any discussion of issues related to MaineCare member access.
34 3. It corrects the cross-reference for rules that are adopted by reference.
35 4. It removes the changes in the bill to the index of MaineCare rates by service code
36 that is maintained on the department's publicly accessible website.
