13 SUMMARY
1144 This amendment makes the following changes to the bill.
15 1. It removes language from the powers and duties provision of the proposed
16 Independent Office of Tax Appeals and instead adds that language to the qualifications
17 provision to make clear that the Commissioner of Administrative and Financial Services
18 may assign other duties to the hearing officers, including assisting the State Board of
19 Property Tax Review.
20 2. It removes a listed duty of the Chief Hearing Officer regarding the assignment of
21 duties to an administrative staff person and adds a listed duty to provide and assign part-
22 time duties to a hearing officer to provide legal services to the State Board of Property Tax
23 Review in consultation with the Office of the Attorney General.
24 3. It changes the proposed date of transition from the existing Department of
25 Administrative and Financial Services, Maine Board of Tax Appeals to the new
26 Independent Office of Tax Appeals from September 15, 2026 to January 1, 2027.
27 4. It removes provisions that propose changes to the composition, jurisdiction and
28 duties of the State Board of Property Tax Review.
29 5. It removes the provisions that change the jurisdictional threshold amounts and
30 appeals process for nonresidential property.
31 6. It removes the provisions that require the Department of Administrative and
32 Financial Services, Bureau of Revenue Services, Office of Tax Policy to study further
33 simplification and modernization of the administrative tax appeals process and report
34 findings and recommendations to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having
35 jurisdiction over taxation matters.
36 7. It adds an appropriations and allocations section.
