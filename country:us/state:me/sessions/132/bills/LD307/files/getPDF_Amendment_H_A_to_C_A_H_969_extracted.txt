13 'Sec. 3. Exemption for certain data centers. Notwithstanding any provision of
14 section 1 to the contrary, a proposed data center, as defined in section 1 and referred to in
15 this section as "the data center," is exempt from the provisions of section 1 if it meets the
16 requirements of this section.
17 1. Site restrictions. The exemption applies if, prior to the effective date of this Act:
18 A. The data center developer has entered into a binding lease or purchase agreement
19 and has demonstrated site control for the property on which the data center is planned
20 to be constructed or operated;
21 B. The property on which the data center is to be constructed or operated is a former
22 industrial site with existing electrical transmission and distribution functionality that
23 supported industrial or electric generation use and for which the data center developer
24 provides the results of a system impact analysis provided by the transmission and
25 distribution utility that served the site or, if applicable, ISO-New England, as well as
26 agreements with the transmission and distribution utility and, if applicable, ISO-New
27 England. As used in this paragraph, "ISO-New England" means the independent
28 system operator of the New England bulk power system; and
29 C. The data center developer has identified a maximum electric utility demand of 82
30 megawatts for the data center, supported by documentation provided to the Public
31 Utilities Commission, and the demand does not exceed the level evaluated by the
32 Public Utilities Commission pursuant to subsection 2 and may not be increased except
33 as provided in this section.
34 2. Determination by Public Utilities Commission. Upon the receipt of a filing by a
35 data center developer, the Public Utilities Commission shall conduct a proceeding and may
36 approve the data center only upon a finding, based on substantial evidence in the record,
37 including verification of compliance with subsection 1, that:

21 SUMMARY
2222 This amendment exempts from the provisions of Committee Amendment "A" a data
2233 center that, prior to the effective date of the legislation, meets specified criteria, such as:
24 1. The developer entered into a lease or purchase agreement for property on which the
25 data center is planned to be constructed or operated;

27 industrial site with existing electrical transmission and distribution functionality that
28 supported industrial or electric generation use;
29 3. Following the receipt of a filing by a data center developer, the Public Utilities
30 Commission finds, after a proceeding, that:
