HOUSE AMENDMENT “ ” to COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “A” to S.P. 874, L.D. 2155
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment changes the title and replaces the provision of Committee
33 Amendment "A" that temporarily suspended the ability of the Bureau of Alcoholic
44 Beverages and Lottery Operations, within the Department of Administrative and Financial
55 Services, to suspend, revoke or refuse to renew a manufacturing facility license or an on-
66 premises retail license issued to a manufacturing facility licensee that was issued prior to
77 May 23, 2025 solely on the basis that the person or persons holding a majority ownership
88 interest in more than the allowable number of on-premises retail licenses that may be issued
99 per licensed manufacturing facility as long as the on-premises retail licenses were also
1100 initially issued prior to May 23, 2025. Instead, this amendment grandfathers those licenses
1111 permanently and prohibits the sale, transfer or other conveyance of those licenses.
