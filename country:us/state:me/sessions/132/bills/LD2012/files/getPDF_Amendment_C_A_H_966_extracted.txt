COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1342, L.D. 2012
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment does the following.
3 1. It limits the current requirements regarding the power source for carbon monoxide
4 detectors for educational facilities to those constructed prior to January 1, 2027.
5 2. It requires educational facilities constructed on or after January 1, 2027 to have
6 carbon monoxide detectors installed in accordance with the Life Safety Code of the
7 National Fire Protection Association but does not specify the power requirements.
8 3. It removes enforcement of the laws regarding carbon monoxide detectors by state,
9 county or municipal law enforcement officers as proposed in the bill.
10 4. It adds a mandate preamble.
