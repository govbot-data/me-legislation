COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 444, L.D. 705
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft. The amendment makes
33 corrections and clarifying changes to Public Law 2025, chapter 650 as follows.
4 It clarifies that the standard deduction for tax year 2025 is $15,000.
5 It clarifies that the application date for the pass-through entity tax applies only to the
6 Part of Public Law 2025, chapter 650 that enacts the pass-through entity tax.
7 It includes reimbursement rates subject to rule Chapter 101: MaineCare Benefits
8 Manual, Chapter III, Section 28, Section 97 Appendix B and Section 97 Appendix D in the
9 3.07% cost-of-living adjustment for essential support workers.
10 It provides a one-time transfer from the MaineCare Stabilization Fund Other Special
11 Revenue Funds account within the Department of Health and Human Services to the
12 General Fund to fund the General Fund portion of the cost-of-living adjustments for fiscal
13 year 2026-27.
14 It provides that the funds provided in Public Law 2025, chapter 650 to address unmet
15 needs of older adults through community-based services and programs must be distributed
16 to area agencies based on priorities identified by local advisory councils to meet regional
17 needs through the intrastate funding formula to develop programs and deliver services to
18 older adults.
19 It provides appropriations and allocations. It changes the funding provided for grants
20 in the early childhood nutrition grant program in Public Law 2025, chapter 650 from one-
21 time to ongoing. It also changes the funding provided for low-income electric ratepayer
22 assistance in Public Law 2025, chapter 650 from one-time to ongoing. It also includes the
23 appropriations and allocations necessary for the cost-of-living adjustments.
