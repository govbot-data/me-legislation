33 SUMMARY
3344 This amendment does the following.
35 1. It increases by $2 certain hunting license fees for residents and by $4 certain hunting
36 license fees for nonresidents.
37 2. It requires the Commissioner of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife to suspend an
38 individual's hunting license for one year if the individual fails to register a harvested deer.

1 3. It directs the Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife to submit a report to the
2 joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over inland fisheries and
3 wildlife matters by January 1, 2029 regarding its findings and recommendations related to
4 the electronic registration of deer. The committee is authorized to report out a bill based
5 on the report.
6 4. It renames the Pheasant Fund as the Resource Management Fund and expands the
7 funding sources and purposes of the fund. It requires $2 to be transferred from the Resource
8 Management Fund to the Maine Deer Management Fund for each registered deer.
