1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment specifies that the Maine Education Policy Research Institute must
33 survey, in addition to school administrative units, schools in the unorganized territory and
44 private schools approved for the receipt of public funds that enroll 60% or more publicly
55 funded students. The amendment specifies that guidance collected by the institute must
66 include, but is not limited to, a review of neuropsychological and developmental studies,
77 comparative academic performance data and behavioral and environmental observations
88 that provide insight on the impact of digital consumption on cognitive development. The
99 institute must also share this guidance with its steering committee. The amendment also
1100 stipulates that funding for the requirements of the resolve must be incorporated into the
1111 institute's fiscal year 2026-27 cooperative project agreement.
