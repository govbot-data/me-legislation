11 Amend the bill by inserting after the title and before the enacting clause the following:
12 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
13 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
14 Whereas, shared appreciation mortgage loans are a new form of equity-based
15 financing; and
16 Whereas, this legislation enacts statutory provisions to regulate shared appreciation
17 mortgage loans as mortgage loans and directs the Department of Professional and Financial
18 Regulation, Bureau of Consumer Credit Protection to enforce those provisions; and
19 Whereas, immediate enactment of this legislation is necessary for the protection of
20 consumers; and
21 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
22 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
23 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
24 therefore,'

8 SUMMARY
99 This amendment replaces the bill, adds an emergency preamble and emergency clause

11 Part A of the amendment defines "shared appreciation mortgage loan" and imposes on
12 such a loan similar requirements and limitations to those imposed on other mortgage loans
13 issued in the State. This Part also requires the Department of Professional and Financial
14 Regulation, Bureau of Consumer Credit Protection to report on compliance with these
15 provisions to the 134th Legislature and authorizes the submission of legislation in response
16 to the report.
17 Part B of the amendment enacts into law the advisory ruling of the Superintendent of
18 Consumer Credit Protection, which took effect October 29, 2025, stating that a shared
19 appreciation mortgage loan is a "consumer credit transaction" and providers of these
20 products are "supervised lenders" under the Maine Consumer Credit Code and provides
21 that any shared appreciation mortgage loan entered into with a consumer that is secured by
22 a dwelling or residential real estate in this State between October 29, 2025 and the effective
23 date of this legislation that does not comply with the provisions of that ruling is void and
24 unenforceable.
