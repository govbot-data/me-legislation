S.P. 491 - L.D. 1202
Resolve, Requiring the Department of Education to Develop Professional
Opportunities for Educators in African American Studies
Sec. 1. Professional development in African American studies. Resolved:
That the Department of Education shall develop professional development opportunities
for educators in African American studies.
Sec. 2. Appropriations and allocations. Resolved: That the following
appropriations and allocations are made.
EDUCATION, DEPARTMENT OF
Office of Workforce Development and Innovative Pathways Z334
Initiative: Provides one-time funds for professional development opportunities for
educators in African American studies.
