9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 32, L.D. 19, “An Act to Change the
10 Definition of "Oversized ATV" in the Laws Governing the Registration of All-terrain
11 Vehicles”
12 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
13 'An Act to Allow Oversized All-terrain Vehicle Registration for Individuals with a
14 Disability Registration Plate'
15 Amend the bill by striking out all of the emergency preamble.
16 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
17 following:
18 'Sec. 1. 12 MRSA §13155, sub-§5-B, as amended by PL 2025, c. 267, §4, is further
19 amended to read:
20 5-B. Oversized ATV; exception. A person may not register an oversized ATV except
21 as provided in this subsection. A resident who has registered an oversized ATV prior to
22 January 1, 2022 may continue to register that oversized ATV upon payment of the fee
23 specified in subsection 5, paragraph A, and upon a transfer of ownership of that registered
24 oversized ATV, the new owner may also register that oversized ATV upon payment of the
25 fee specified in subsection 5, paragraph A. Registration is not required for an oversized
26 ATV operated by a person on the land of another if written permission is received from the
27 landowner, lessee of the land or landowner's representative and the person is engaged solely
28 in a business activity, other than a business activity involving recreational use of the
29 oversized ATV. A person with a disability who has been issued a disability registration
30 plate for a motor vehicle pursuant to Title 29-A, section 521, subsection 2, paragraph A
31 may register an oversized ATV upon payment of the fee specified in subsection 5,
32 paragraph A. For purposes of this subsection, "oversized ATV" means an ATV that is
33 wider than 65 inches or weighs more than 2,000 pounds according to the manufacturer's
34 specifications.'
35 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
36 number to read consecutively.
