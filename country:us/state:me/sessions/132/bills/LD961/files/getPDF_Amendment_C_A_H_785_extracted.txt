21 registered nurses; and

23 current licensing requirements by establishing practice standards including the length of

26 practitioner within the clinic or hospital serving in a mentorship role to a certified nurse
27 practitioner; and

30 patients; and

10 SUMMARY
1111 This amendment retains the emergency status of the bill but replaces the substantive
1122 provisions in the bill. The amendment provides that, once rules are adopted by the State
1133 Board of Nursing to establish practice standards, a certified nurse practitioner who qualifies
1144 as an advanced practice registered nurse is no longer required to practice for at least 24
1155 months under the supervision of a licensed physician or supervising nurse practitioner or
1166 be employed by a clinic or hospital that has a medical director who is a licensed physician.
1177 The amendment directs the board to establish practice standards by rule for a certified
1188 nurse practitioner related to the length of time a certified nurse practitioner must practice
1199 as an advanced practice registered nurse; the minimum amount of time a certified nurse
2200 practitioner must practice with a supervising nurse practitioner as a mentor; the practice
2211 setting for a certified nurse practitioner, including any requirements for employment by a
2222 clinic or hospital that has a supervising nurse practitioner within the clinic or hospital
2233 serving in a mentorship role to a certified nurse practitioner; and any other factors
2244 determined necessary by the board. Rules adopted by the board are designated as routine
2255 technical rules.
2266 The amendment directs the board to submit the proposed practice standards for
2277 certified nurse practitioners to the Joint Standing Committee on Health Coverage,
2288 Insurance and Financial Services for review no later than March 15, 2026 and authorizes
2299 the committee to report out a bill to the Second Regular Session of the 132nd Legislature
3300 relating to the practice standards for certified nurse practitioners. The amendment prohibits
3311 the board from adopting rules relating to the practice standards for certified nurse
3322 practitioners before May 1, 2026.
