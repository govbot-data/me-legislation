27 SUMMARY
2288 This amendment makes the following changes to the bill.
29 1. It reduces the threshold amounts for financial transactions that require candidates for
30 State Representative and municipal office to file reports during the last 13 days before an
31 election from $2,000, as in the bill, to $1,500.
32 2. It removes the extended deadline for certain reports of contributions or expenditures
33 for party committees and reports of independent expenditures but retains the extended
34 deadline for candidates.
35 3. It removes the direction to the Commission on Governmental Ethics and Election
36 Practices to amend its rules to provide that independent expenditures made during the 60
37 days before an election must be disclosed in a report within 2 calendar days of the
38 expenditure.
39 4. It adds a section that clarifies what party committees must disclose in their campaign
40 finance reports, including for each expenditure: the candidate or ballot measure; for a
41 candidate, the office sought and the candidate's district; the date and amount of each
