9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 563, L.D. 877, “An Act to Require

11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'Resolve, to Establish the Commission to Study the Employment Practices and
13 Operations of Transportation Network Companies Operating in the State'
14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
15 'Sec. 1. Commission established. Resolved: That the Commission to Study the
16 Employment Practices and Operations of Transportation Network Companies Operating in
17 the State, referred to in this resolve as "the commission," is established.
18 Sec. 2. Commission membership. Resolved: That, notwithstanding Joint Rule
19 353, the commission consists of 13 members as follows:
20 1. Two members of the Senate appointed by the President of the Senate, including
21 members from each of the 2 parties holding the largest number of seats in the Legislature;
22 2. Two members of the House of Representatives appointed by the Speaker of the
23 House, including members from each of the 2 parties holding the largest number of seats
24 in the Legislature;
25 3. One member representing the Maine Center for Economic Policy, appointed by the
26 President of the Senate;
27 4. Four members who are transportation network company drivers in the State, 2 of
28 whom are appointed by the President of the Senate and 2 of whom are appointed by the
29 Speaker of the House;
30 5. Two members representing a transportation network company that operates in the
31 State, one of whom is appointed by the President of the Senate and one of whom is
32 appointed by the Speaker of the House;
33 6. One member representing a statewide union appointed by the Speaker of the House;

1 7. The Commissioner of Labor or the commissioner's designee.
2 Sec. 3. Chairs. Resolved: That the first-named Senate member is the Senate chair
3 and the first-named House of Representatives member is the House chair of the
4 commission.
5 Sec. 4. Appointments; convening of commission. Resolved: That all
6 appointments must be made no later than 30 days following the effective date of this
7 resolve. The appointing authorities shall notify the Executive Director of the Legislative
8 Council once all appointments have been completed. After appointment of all members,
9 the chairs shall call and convene the first meeting of the commission. If 30 days or more
10 after the effective date of this resolve a majority of but not all appointments have been
11 made, the chairs may request authority and the Legislative Council may grant authority for
12 the commission to meet and conduct its business.
13 Sec. 5. Duties; meetings. Resolved: That the commission shall:
14 1. Collect and review aggregate data and information regarding the employment
15 practices and operation processes of transportation network companies that operate in the
16 State, including, but not limited to:
17 A. The compensation of transportation network company drivers;
18 B. The percentage of each passenger fare kept by a transportation network company,
19 or the so-called "take rate" of a transportation network company;
20 C. The deactivation process, including any investigation processes, of transportation
21 network drivers utilized by transportation network companies when a policy violation
22 occurs;
23 D. The supply and demand for transportation network companies in the State; and
24 E. The contractual agreements between a transportation network company and a
25 transportation network company driver, including the employment classification;
26 2. Examine rules, regulations and laws adopted by other states governing
27 transportation network company operations, including for transportation network company
28 riders and transportation network company drivers; and
29 3. Develop recommendations for legislation relating to the findings of the study,
30 including legislation relating to the compensation of transportation network company
31 drivers and the deactivation process.
32 For the purposes of this resolve, "transportation network company," "transportation
33 network company driver" and "transportation network company rider" have the same
34 meanings as in the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 24-A, section 7302.
35 Sec. 6. Staff assistance. Resolved: That the Legislative Council shall provide
36 necessary staffing services to the commission, except that Legislative Council staff support
37 is not authorized when the Legislature is in regular or special session.
38 Sec. 7. Report. Resolved: That, no later than November 4, 2026, the commission
39 shall submit a report that includes its findings and recommendations for presentation to the
40 joint standing committee having jurisdiction over labor matters. The joint standing
41 committee may submit legislation based on the findings and recommendations in the report
42 to the 133rd Legislature in 2027.'

1100 the report to the 133rd Legislature in 2027.
