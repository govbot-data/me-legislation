9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1102, L.D. 1661, “An Act to Establish
10 a Comprehensive and Interagency Approach to Invasive Species Management”
11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'Resolve, Requiring a Review and Assessment of the State's Invasive Species
13 Management Efforts'
14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
15 'Sec. 1. Review and assessment of State's invasive species management
16 efforts. Resolved: That, within existing budgeted resources, the Department of Inland
17 Fisheries and Wildlife and the Department of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry,
18 referred to in this resolve as "the departments," shall conduct a review and assessment of
19 the State's invasive species management efforts. The departments shall collaborate with
20 the Department of Environmental Protection, the Department of Marine Resources, other
21 state agencies and local units of government and nongovernmental organizations in
22 conducting the review and assessment. The review and assessment must include a
23 summary of existing interagency working groups and other collaborative efforts focused
24 on invasive species, including, but not limited to, invasive aquatic species and invasive
25 terrestrial plants. In conducting the review and assessment, the departments shall explore
26 opportunities for increased collaboration and efficiency and funding opportunities for
27 projects related to the control and management of invasive species, education and outreach
28 efforts and projects aimed at early detection and prevention of invasive species.
29 Sec. 2. Report. Resolved: That, by January 31, 2027, the departments shall submit
30 a report detailing the results of the review and assessment under section 1, including any
31 findings and recommendations, to the joint standing committees of the Legislature having
32 jurisdiction over agriculture, conservation and forestry matters; inland fisheries and
33 wildlife matters; marine resources matters; and environment and natural resources matters.
34 Each joint standing committee may submit legislation to the 133rd Legislature in 2027.'
35 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
36 number to read consecutively.
