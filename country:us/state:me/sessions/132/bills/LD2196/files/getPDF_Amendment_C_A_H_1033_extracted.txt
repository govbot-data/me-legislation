6 SUMMARY
77 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, makes the following
88 changes to the bill.
9 1. It updates the price growth ceilings to apply only to individual health plans, small
10 group health plans and the state employee health plan.
11 2. It adds an exception to price growth ceilings for acute care hospitals with
12 standardized commercial inpatient prices below the national first quartile and provides the
13 circumstances under which those hospitals may enter into a contract with an individual or
14 small group market insurer.
15 3. It adds language providing that, upon the agreement of a carrier and hospital,
16 individual service level prices may exceed the price caps as long as certain criteria are met.
17 4. It removes provisions establishing maximum prices for inpatient and outpatient
18 hospital services.
19 5. It removes the requirement that the Office of Affordable Health Care determine
20 whether a hospital is financially distressed.
21 6. It removes provisions regarding limitations on prior authorizations for treatment of
22 chronic conditions.
23 7. It clarifies that the minimum negotiated rate requirements for in-network primary
24 care or behavioral health care evaluation and management apply only to individual health
25 plans, small group health plans and the state employee health plan.
26 8. It adds a graduated implementation period for minimum negotiated rate
27 requirements for in-network primary care or behavioral health care evaluation and
28 management.
29 9. It adds language requiring the Office of Affordable Health Care to, beginning
30 January 15, 2027, report on the impact of growth caps to the joint standing committee of
31 the Legislature having jurisdiction over health and human services matters and to the joint
32 standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over health coverage, insurance
33 and financial services matters. The report must summarize the status of the implementation
34 of growth caps, their impact on the quality and accessibility of hospital care in the State
35 and their impact on premiums paid by individuals insured by the state employee health
36 plan, individual health plans and small group health plans.
