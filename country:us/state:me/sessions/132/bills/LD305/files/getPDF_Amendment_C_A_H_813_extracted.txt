14 Amend the bill by inserting after the title and before the enacting clause the following:
15 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
16 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
17 Whereas, Public Law 2025, chapter 152 was enacted to prevent the misuse of the
18 motor vehicle excise tax exemption for certain disabled veterans; and
19 Whereas, through the process of implementing Public Law 2025, chapter 152, it was
20 uncovered that a significant group of disabled veterans lease, rather than own, their vehicles
21 and was excluded from the excise tax exemption, creating an inequity that was not
22 contemplated during legislative debate and runs counter to the spirit of the law; and
23 Whereas, leasing may be the most practical and affordable option for veterans living
24 on fixed incomes or disability compensation; and
25 Whereas, this legislation must go into effect as soon as possible to address this matter;
26 and

28 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as

30 therefore,'

1 'Sec. 1. 36 MRSA §1483, sub-§12, as amended by PL 2025, c. 152, §2, is further
2 amended to read:
3 12. Certain Automobiles owned by certain veterans. Automobiles owned by
4 veterans who are granted free registration of those vehicles by the Secretary of State under
5 Title 29‑A, section 523, subsection 1 or 2 or who are disabled by injury or disease incurred
6 or aggravated during active military service in the line of duty and are receiving any form
7 of pension or compensation from the United States Government for total, service-
8 connected disability. A maximum of 3 automobiles owned by a veteran are exempt from
9 the excise tax. In order for an automobile to qualify for an exemption from the excise tax
10 under this subsection, the name of the qualifying veteran must appear on the automobile's
11 certificate of title. If there are multiple owners of an automobile, at least one of the owners
12 whose name appears on the certificate of title must meet the requirements of this subsection
13 in order for the automobile to qualify for the exemption;
14 Sec. 2. 36 MRSA §1483, sub-§12-A is enacted to read:
15 12-A. Automobiles leased by certain veterans. Automobiles leased by veterans who
16 are disabled by injury or disease incurred or aggravated during active military service in
17 the line of duty and are receiving any form of pension or compensation from the United
18 States Government for total, service-connected disability. A maximum of 3 automobiles
19 leased by a veteran are exempt from the excise tax. In order for an automobile to qualify
20 for an exemption from the excise tax under this subsection, the name of the qualifying
21 veteran must appear on the automobile's lease agreement. If there are multiple lessees of
22 an automobile, at least one of the lessees whose name appears on the automobile's lease

30 SUMMARY
3311 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft, and changes the title. It also
3322 adds an emergency preamble and emergency clause and provides an effective date of July
3333 1, 2026.
34 The amendment exempts from excise tax automobiles leased by veterans who meet
35 certain eligibility requirements. The amendment provides that in order for an automobile
36 to qualify for the exemption, the name of an eligible veteran must appear on the
37 automobile's lease agreement. It also requires that if there are multiple lessees of an
38 automobile, at least one of the lessees whose name appears on the lease agreement must be
39 a veteran who is eligible for the exemption.
