COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 344, L.D. 785
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment, which is a minority report of the committee, replaces the bill and
33 changes the title. The amendment provides that, beginning with the First Regular Session
44 of the 133rd Legislature, the Mi’kmaq Nation may, like the other Wabanaki Nations, elect
55 a representative to the House of Representatives of the Maine Legislature. The
66 representative of the Mi’kmaq Nation has, to the same extent as other members of the
77 Legislature, the right to receive a salary for each regular session of the Legislature, an
88 allowance for constituent services, allowances for travel-related expenses and per diem
99 payments for the duration of any special session of the Legislature.
