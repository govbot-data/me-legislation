17 'Sec. 1. 22 MRSA §3198 is enacted to read:
18 §3198. Health Care Premium Stabilization Fund
19 1. Fund established. The Health Care Premium Stabilization Fund, referred to in this
20 section as "the fund," is established as a dedicated, nonlapsing fund administered by the
21 department to maintain coverage for residents of the State through the Maine Health
22 Insurance Marketplace, established in chapter 1479.
23 2. Sources of fund. The fund consists of amounts appropriated or allocated by the
24 State. The department may receive money from any gift, grant, contribution, legislative
25 appropriation, allocation or transfer or bond proceeds.
26 3. Uses of fund. The department shall use the fund to establish a state subsidy to offset
27 the financial impact of the expiration of the enhanced premium tax credits under the
28 American Rescue Plan Act of 2021, Public Law 117-2. The subsidy must be used to
29 increase the amount of assistance available to those previously eligible for subsidies under
30 the federal Affordable Care Act, Public Law 111-148 and to expand assistance to those
31 with an income at or above 400% of the federal poverty level if the recipient's monthly
32 premium cost of the second lowest cost silver health plan, as described in the United States
33 Internal Revenue Code of 1986, Section 36B(b)(3)(B), would, prior to the receipt of a
34 subsidy under this section, exceed 8.5% of the recipient's monthly household income.
