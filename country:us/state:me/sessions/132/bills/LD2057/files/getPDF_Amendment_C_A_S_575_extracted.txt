20 SUMMARY
2211 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill and
2222 changes the title. It removes the requirement in statute that the Maine State Housing
2233 Authority only make construction loans if a financial institution in the State agrees to
2244 participate in the loan, at least to the extent of acting as an escrow agent. It allows the
2255 Maine State Housing Authority to make construction loans to support programs authorized
2266 under the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 30-A, chapter 201. It also repeals the provision of
2277 law setting different bonding requirements and limitations to bonds issued by the Maine
2288 State Housing Authority for construction loans.
