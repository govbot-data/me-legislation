22 1. Definitions. For the purposes of this section, unless the context otherwise indicates,

24 A. "Diagnostic and therapeutic pharmaceutical agent" means any prescription or
25 nonprescription drug delivered by any route of administration, which may be used or

27 conditions and diseases of the visual system or the human eye and its adnexa, including

33 scalpel, cryoprobe, laser or electric cautery, or by the use of ionizing radiation.

17 SUMMARY
1188 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill.
19 Part A of the amendment, which takes effect January 1, 2028, makes changes to the
20 provisions of the law governing optometrists by providing a more detailed explanation of
21 what constitutes the practice of optometry. The definition of "practice of optometry" in
22 current law does not include surgical procedures.
23 Under the amendment, certain types of ophthalmic surgeries and laser procedures are
24 included in the practice of optometry and certain ophthalmic surgical procedures are
25 specifically excluded. An optometrist may only perform ophthalmic surgery or laser
26 procedures if the optometrist meets minimum credentialing requirements established in law
27 and any additional requirements established by the State Board of Optometry in rule. The
28 amendment repeals these provisions of the amendment January 1, 2033 and requires the
29 board to recommend to the Legislature whether to continue, modify or repeal these
30 provisions no later than January 15, 2032. The joint standing committee of the Legislature
31 having jurisdiction over optometry licensing matters may report out a bill based on the
32 recommendation to the Second Regular Session of the 135th Legislature.
33 Part A also broadens the authority of an optometrist to dispense and administer certain
34 medications independently and removes language that prohibits an optometrist from
35 administering drugs intravenously or by injection. The amendment directs the board to
36 adopt rules to further define the scope of practice of optometry and establish credentialing
37 requirements for ophthalmic surgery procedures. The rules are designated major
38 substantive and are subject to legislative approval before final adoption. Finally, Part A
39 requires an optometrist to obtain malpractice insurance in an amount established by the
40 board and also makes minor changes to the provisions relating to telehealth services and
41 filling contact lenses and spectacle prescriptions.
