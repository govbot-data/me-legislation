17 SUMMARY
1188 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, increases the amount
1199 in the bill that is required to be annually appropriated to the County Jail Operations Fund
2200 from $28,342,104 to $30,000,000. It removes a provision requiring that this amount is
2211 increased annually by 4% and replaces it with a provision that requires that the amount be
2222 adjusted annually by any percentage change in the Consumer Price Index from July 1st to
2233 June 30th. It removes a provision of the bill requiring an appropriation of an additional
2244 $5,000,000 for community corrections and pretrial release programs. It amends current law
2255 to reduce the amount county jails must spend on community corrections and pretrial release
2266 programs from 25% of funds appropriated by the State to 20%. It also adds an
2277 appropriations and allocations section.
