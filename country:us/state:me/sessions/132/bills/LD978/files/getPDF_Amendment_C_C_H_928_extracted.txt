15 1. Departmental reimbursement. When a municipality incurs net general assistance
16 costs in any fiscal year prior to July 1, 2015 in excess of .0003 of that municipality's most
17 recent state valuation relative to the state fiscal year for which reimbursement is being
18 issued, as determined by the State Tax Assessor in the statement filed as provided in Title
19 36, section 381, the Department of Health and Human Services shall reimburse the
20 municipality for 90% of the amount in excess of these expenditures when the department
21 finds that the municipality has been in compliance with all requirements of this chapter. If
22 a municipality elects to determine need without consideration of funds distributed from any
23 municipally-controlled municipally controlled trust fund that must otherwise be considered
24 for purposes of this chapter, the department shall reimburse the municipality for 66 2/3%
25 of the amount in excess of such expenditures when the department finds that the
26 municipality has otherwise been in compliance with all requirements of this chapter.
27 The department shall reimburse each municipality and each Indian tribe 70% of the direct
28 costs incurred by that municipality or tribe on or after July 1, 2015 and before July 1, 2026
29 for the general assistance program granted by that municipality or tribe. For the purposes
30 of this subsection, "Indian tribe" has the same meaning as in section 411, subsection 8‑A.
31 Beginning July 1, 2026, the department shall reimburse each municipality and each Indian
32 tribe 75% of the direct costs incurred by that municipality or tribe for costs incurred on or
33 after July 1, 2026.
34 For the purposes of this subsection, "Indian tribe" has the same meaning as in section 411,
35 subsection 8-A.
