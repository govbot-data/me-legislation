31 SUMMARY
3322 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. The amendment makes the
3333 following changes to the cannabis sales tax and excise tax laws, effective January 1, 2027.
34 1. It imposes a sales tax of 14% on the value of a potentially intoxicating hemp product.
35 2. It defines "prerolled cannabis cigarette" and provides that a cultivation facility
36 licensee must pay an excise tax of $143 per pound or fraction thereof of the combined
37 weight of the cannabis flower and cannabis trim contained in a prerolled cannabis cigarette
38 sold to another cannabis establishment licensee in the State.
