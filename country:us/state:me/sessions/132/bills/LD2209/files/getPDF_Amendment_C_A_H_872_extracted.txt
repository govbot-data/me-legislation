COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1488, L.D. 2209
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment amends the resolve to require that the Department of Health and
33 Human Services explore options to expand education and training programs for dental
44 therapists in the State in addition to the other duties required in the resolve. The amendment
55 also specifies that the department invite the participation of the Maine Oral Health Centers
66 Alliance and dentists working in private practice who serve the oral health care needs of
77 underserved and high-need populations as additional stakeholders. Finally, the amendment
88 adds language to clarify that the Department of Professional and Financial Regulation,
99 Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation must provide technical assistance to
1100 the Department of Health and Human Services on behalf of the Board of Dental Practice
1111 in place of language in the resolve requiring a representative of the board to participate as
1122 a stakeholder.
