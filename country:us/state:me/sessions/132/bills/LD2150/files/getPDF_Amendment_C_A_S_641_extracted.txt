9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 871, L.D. 2150, “An Act to Establish
10 Procedures for Restricting Access to State Property, Access to State Services and
11 Communication with or Through State Entities”
12 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
13 'An Act to Establish Procedures for Restricting Access to State Property, Access to
14 State Services and Communication with or Through State Entities and to Provide for
15 a Review of Those Restrictions'
16 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and before the
17 emergency clause and inserting the following:
18 'Sec. 1. 5 MRSA §60 is enacted to read:
19 §60. Restriction of access to state property or services; restriction of communication
20 with or through entities of the State
21 1. Entity of the State; definition. As used in this section, "entity of the State" means
22 any office, department, agency, authority, commission, board, institution, hospital or other
23 instrumentality of the State, except that "entity of the State" does not include a public
24 institution of higher education.
25 2. Notice or communication of restriction. An entity of the State that issues a notice
26 or communication, or a local law enforcement agency that issues a notice or communication
27 on behalf of an entity of the State, that restricts a person from accessing state property or
28 services provided by the State or from communicating with or through the entity to which
29 an otherwise unrestricted person would have access must issue the notice or
30 communication in writing to the restricted person. The written notice or communication
31 must include the factual basis for the entity of the State's decision to restrict the person's
32 access or communication as well as information describing the procedure to challenge the
33 notice or communication. The issuance of a court order does not satisfy the requirements
34 of this subsection.

2 constitutes final agency action for the purposes of review under chapter 375.

12 resources, submit a report regarding the implementation of the Maine Revised Statutes,

15 1. The number of notices or communications issued by or on behalf of an entity of the

17 2. The number of notices or communications issued by or on behalf of an entity of the
18 State that were subsequently violated by the person being restricted;
19 3. A list of the entities of the State that issued notices or communications and the
20 number of notices or communications issued on behalf of each entity;
21 4. The geographic scope of the impact that the notice or communication issued to the
22 restricted person has on the entity of the State, including whether the notice or
23 communication was issued for a regional office or offices across the State;
24 5. The final duration of each notice or communication; and
25 6. The number of notices or communications that were challenged pursuant to Title 5,
26 chapter 375.
