2244 This amendment amends Committee Amendment "A" to require the Commissioner of

2266 commercial menhaden fishing license system that provides preference, once the number of

2288 individuals who meet the eligibility standards established in Committee Amendment "A"
2299 and who, based on municipality of residence, reside the farthest east in Maine. If more
3300 than one eligible individual resides in the same municipality, the commissioner is required
3311 to determine randomly which individual is given first preference. The commissioner is
3322 required to assign licenses under the preference system as soon as practicable following the
3333 effective date of this legislation.
