24 SUMMARY
2255 This amendment amends the bill in the following ways.
26 1. It clarifies that a school bus is prohibited from being in motion when a school bus
27 door is open and a passenger is on board.
28 2. It removes the provision making a school bus operator's failure to use the school bus
29 crossing arm a Class E crime and instead states that a violation of any laws related to school
30 buses or rules governing their construction, equipment, operation and identification or the
31 transportation of public preschool children is a traffic infraction unless otherwise
32 prescribed by law.
33 3. It requires the operator of a school bus equipped with a school bus crossing arm to
34 not stop unnecessarily where there is a physical barrier that prevents the school bus crossing
35 arm from extending safely.
36 4. It prohibits a school bus operator from disabling the school bus crossing arm, except
37 when there is a physical barrier that prevents the school bus crossing arm from extending
38 safely.
39 5. It establishes the Maine School Transportation Safety Commission, which is
40 required to report by February 1st annually to the joint standing committee of the
41 Legislature having jurisdiction over education and cultural affairs and the joint standing
