22 SUMMARY
2233 This amendment, which is the minority report of the committee, changes the title and
2244 replaces the bill. The amendment requires certain business entities to include, on the annual
2255 report required to be filed with the Secretary of State, the business entities' industry code
2266 under the North American Industry Classification System of the United States Department
2277 of Commerce, United States Census Bureau. This new requirement applies to for-profit
2288 and nonprofit corporations, limited liability partnerships, limited partnerships and limited
2299 liability companies, all whether foreign or domestic. The amendment requires that
3300 additional information be provided with the annual report if the business entity's industry
3311 code indicates the business is engaged in renting or leasing residential buildings or
3322 residential dwellings in the State. The amendment requires the Secretary of State to provide
3333 a form or other method for the submission of the additional information by January 1, 2028.
3344 The amendment includes an appropriations and allocations section.
