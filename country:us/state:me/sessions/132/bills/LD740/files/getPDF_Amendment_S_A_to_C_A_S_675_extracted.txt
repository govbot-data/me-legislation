8 SENATE AMENDMENT “ ” to COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “A” to H.P. 482,
9 L.D. 740, “An Act to Establish a Comprehensive Program to Divert Youth from the
10 Criminal Justice System and Address Their Needs”
11 Amend the amendment by striking out all of section 3 and inserting the following:
12 'Sec. 3. Duties. Resolved: That the task force shall:
13 1. Identify, describe and collect data on diversion of youth from Department of
14 Corrections supervision prior to formal court involvement, including no further action
15 determinations and informal adjustments;
16 2. Identify obstacles and improvements to diversions that will help ensure the
17 Department of Corrections is able to divert youths from the formal juvenile court process
18 when appropriate;
19 3. To the extent possible, establish at least one pilot site to design and implement
20 methods for diverting youths from the juvenile justice system, with a particular focus on
21 increasing diversion from Department of Corrections supervision and court involvement;
22 and
23 4. To the extent possible and appropriate, develop and implement a family-engaged
24 case planning process for all youth under Department of Corrections supervision.'
25 Amend the amendment by striking out all of section 4 and inserting the following:
26 'Sec. 4. Update. Resolved: That, no later than January 1, 2028, the Commissioner
27 of Corrections shall provide an update to the joint standing committee of the Legislature
28 having jurisdiction over criminal justice and public safety matters on the work of the task
29 force. The joint standing committee may report out legislation based on the update to the
30 Second Regular Session of the 133rd Legislature.'
31 Amend the amendment by striking out all of section 5.
32 Amend the amendment by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or
33 section number to read consecutively.
