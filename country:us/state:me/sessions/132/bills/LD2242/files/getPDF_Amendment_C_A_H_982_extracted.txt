30 agents;
31 6. Specify in Section 1(H) that medications for expedited partner therapy must be
32 provided in accordance with the requirements and limitations in Title 22, section 1242;
33 7. Remove all of Section 1(I) referring to all topical preparations, including topical
34 antineoplastics;
