11 Amend the amendment by striking out the substitute title and inserting the following:
12 'An Act to Establish an Excise Tax on Cannabis Pre-roll and to Change the Tax on
13 Potentially Intoxicating Hemp Products'
14 Amend the amendment by striking out all of sections 4 and 5 and inserting the
15 following:
16 'Sec. 4. 36 MRSA §4921, sub-§7-A is enacted to read:
17 7-A. Cannabis pre-roll. "Cannabis pre-roll" means cannabis flower, cannabis trim
18 or a combination of cannabis flower and cannabis trim that is rolled in rolling papers,
19 cigarette papers or wraps, with or without a cardboard filter, and intended for smoking. A
20 cannabis pre-roll may be infused with cannabis concentrate. For the purposes of this
21 subsection, "cannabis concentrate" has the same meaning as in Title 28-B, section 102-A,
22 subsection 9.
23 Sec. 5. 36 MRSA §4923, sub-§2-A is enacted to read:
24 2-A. Excise tax on cannabis pre-roll. Beginning January 1, 2027, a cultivation
25 facility licensee shall pay an excise tax pursuant to subsection 1 and subsection 2, as
26 applicable based upon the amount per pound or fraction thereof of cannabis flower and
27 cannabis trim contained within a cannabis pre-roll.'
