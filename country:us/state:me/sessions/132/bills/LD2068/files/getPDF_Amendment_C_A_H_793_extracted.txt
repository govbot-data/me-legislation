44 This amendment amends the bill to clarify that reflective triangles used by trucks and
55 truck tractors must be bidirectional emergency reflective triangles to conform with federal
66 law. The amendment also clarifies that either flares or bidirectional emergency reflective
77 triangles, rather than both, are required equipment on a truck or truck tractor.
