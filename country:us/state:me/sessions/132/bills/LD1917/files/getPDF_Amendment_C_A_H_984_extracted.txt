10 SUMMARY
1111 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill,
1122 changes the title and clarifies the list of entities to which sealed criminal history record
1133 information may be disseminated, the basis for such dissemination and the permissible use
1144 of disseminated sealed criminal history record information by the Department of
1155 Professional and Financial Regulation, its agencies and boards and certain other entities.
1166 The amendment clarifies a license applicant’s obligation to disclose sealed criminal history
1177 record information to these entities. The amendment also clarifies that for the conduct to
1188 be an eligible criminal conviction, the conduct must no longer be a crime.
