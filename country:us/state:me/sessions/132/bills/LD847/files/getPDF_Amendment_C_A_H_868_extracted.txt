22 SUMMARY
2233 This amendment replaces the bill with a resolve that establishes the Commission to
2244 Improve Tenant-Landlord Relationships and Increase the Use of Housing Vouchers. The
2255 commission is tasked with examining issues relating to housing discrimination based on
2266 the use of income-based rental assistance and incentivizing landlords to rent to tenants who
2277 rely on income-based rental assistance programs such as so-called "Section 8" housing
2288 vouchers and municipal general assistance. The commission is directed to submit a report
2299 to the joint standing or joint select committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over
3300 housing matters and the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction
3311 over judiciary matters. The committees are authorized to submit legislation to the 133rd
3322 Legislature in 2027 based on the recommendations in the report.
