6 SUMMARY
77 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, does the following.
8 1. It makes technical changes to the bill.
9 2. It amends the definition of "personally identifiable information," which is defined in
10 the bill for the laws governing the Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife's records
11 and databases related to licenses, permits, registrations, harvest records and applications.
12 3. It establishes that the Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife may in certain
13 situations disseminate names and mailing addresses.
14 4. It establishes violations related to all-terrain vehicle accident report data.
