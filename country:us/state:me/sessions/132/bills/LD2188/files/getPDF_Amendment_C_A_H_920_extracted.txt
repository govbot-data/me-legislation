27 SUMMARY
2288 This amendment makes the following changes to the bill:
29 1. It amends the definition of "notice" to increase from 3 to 7 the number of days after
30 which a notice by first-class mail is deemed to be received;
31 2. It removes the changes proposed to the computation of taxable income of resident
32 individuals for the purposes of reductions to federal adjusted gross income;
33 3. It includes crutches and wheelchairs in the definition of "mobility-enhancing
34 equipment" to ensure that they are exempt from sales tax under the Maine Revised Statutes,
35 Title 36, section 1760, subsection 117;
36 4. It updates provisions regarding the procedure for documenting that a seller of real
37 property has paid the seller's pro rata share of taxes owed on property that is still the subject
38 of a lien reflecting taxes due from someone else, adds language regarding proof of payment
