15 §6603-A. Early childhood nutrition grant program
16 The department shall create and administer, in consultation with the Department of
17 Health and Human Services, Office of Child and Family Services, a grant program to
18 provide grants to school administrative units to enter into agreements with providers of off-
19 site public preschool programs to provide students in those programs with breakfast, lunch
20 and a snack in accordance with this section.
21 1. Definition. For the purposes of this section, "off-site public preschool program"
22 means a public preschool program offered by a public school in the State in partnership
23 with a community provider that is not located on the site of a public school and includes,
24 but is not limited to, a public preschool program offered at a private child care facility.
25 2. Eligibility. After consulting with the Department of Health and Human Services,
26 Office of Child and Family Services, the department shall adopt rules to establish eligibility
27 standards and requirements for participation in the grant program. Eligibility standards and
28 requirements must align with rules adopted by the Department of Health and Human
29 Services for the licensing of child care facilities, including, but not limited to, child care
30 centers, nursery schools and small child care facilities. Eligibility standards and
31 requirements must include, but are not limited to:
32 A. A requirement that the off-site public preschool program meet the basic approval
33 standards for public preschools adopted by the department;
34 B. A requirement that the off-site public preschool program provide food that meets
35 the nutritional standards developed for child care centers as provided in section 6602

10 SUMMARY
1111 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill. The
1122 amendment provides that the Department of Education must administer the early childhood
1133 nutrition grant program and clarifies the types of public preschool programs that are eligible
1144 for a grant. The amendment also requires that an eligible public preschool program must
1155 meet certain nutritional standards developed for child care centers and that the eligibility
1166 standards developed by the department must align with Department of Health and Human
1177 Services standards for child care centers. The amendment also requires that eligibility
1188 standards for the grant program must include eligibility for school administrative units
1199 partnering with licensed child care programs for the delivery of special education services
2200 for children with disabilities.
