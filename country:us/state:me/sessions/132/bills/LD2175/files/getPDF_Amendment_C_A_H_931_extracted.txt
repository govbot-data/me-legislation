27 SUMMARY
2288 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, provides that the rule
2299 may be adopted only if a Mathematics Specialist Certificate is established, grade level
3300 specifications for a teacher of children with disabilities are established and existing
3311 certificates or endorsements established for grades 7 through 12 are amended to grades 6
3322 through 12. The amendment also removes the emergency preamble and emergency clause.
