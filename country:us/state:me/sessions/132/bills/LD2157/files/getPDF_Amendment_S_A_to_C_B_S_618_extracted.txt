11 Amend the amendment by striking out all of subsection 1 (page 1, lines 14 to 23 in
12 amendment) and inserting the following:
13 '1. A person is guilty of unauthorized use of a drone on or over the premises of a
14 correctional facility or jail if the person:
15 A. Intentionally or knowingly flies a drone over the premises of a correctional facility
16 or jail at or below a height of 400 feet above the ground, lands a drone on the premises
17 or flies a drone from the premises. Violation of this paragraph is a Class E crime;
18 B. Violates paragraph A and at the time of the offense has one prior conviction for
19 violating paragraph A or one prior conviction for engaging in conduct substantially
20 similar to that prohibited in paragraph A in another jurisdiction. Violation of this
21 paragraph is a Class D crime; or
22 C. Violates paragraph A and at the time of the offense has more than one prior
23 conviction for violating paragraph A or more than one prior conviction for engaging in
24 conduct substantially similar to that prohibited in paragraph A in another jurisdiction.
25 Violation of this paragraph is a Class C crime.
26 Section 9-A governs the use of prior convictions when determining a sentence.'
27 Amend the amendment by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or
28 section number to read consecutively.
