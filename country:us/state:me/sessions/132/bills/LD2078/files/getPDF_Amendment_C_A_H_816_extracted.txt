13 Amend the bill by inserting after the title and before the enacting clause the following:
14 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
15 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
16 Whereas, current law provides a sales tax exemption for the sale and delivery of only
17 some residential electricity, and this legislation expands that exemption to all residential
18 electricity; and
19 Whereas, immediate relief from the high cost of residential electricity is necessary
20 for customers in Maine; and
21 Whereas, to make the expanded exemption available for the sale and delivery of all
22 residential electricity as early as possible in 2026, this legislation must take effect prior to
23 the expiration of the 90-day period after adjournment; and
24 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
25 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
26 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
27 therefore,'
