HOUSE AMENDMENT

HOUSE AMENDMENT “ ” to COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “A” to H.P. 1220, L.D. 1822
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment amends Committee Amendment "A" to:
3 1. Exempt from the definition of "collect" activities between a controller and a
4 processor and between a processor and its subcontractors;
5 2. Remove from the definition of "process" the exclusion of the collection of personal
6 data;
7 3. Add to the definition of "processor" the collection of personal data;
8 4. Remove from the definition of "publicly available information" the exclusion of
9 information used to create a consumer profile that is publicly available, information
10 available for sale and inferences related to such information;
11 5. Add to the exemptions from the Maine Online Data Privacy Act an affiliate of a
12 supervised financial organization or service corporation;
13 6. Delay the effective date of the Maine Online Data Privacy Act from September 1,
14 2026, as proposed in Committee Amendment "A," to September 1, 2027 and make other
15 date changes to accommodate that delay, including:
16 A. The date by which a consumer must be allowed to opt out of the collection or
17 processing of data, September 1, 2027;
18 B. The cutoff date for a violation that the controller or processor may cure without
19 enforcement by the Attorney General, April 1, 2028; and
20 C. The date the Attorney General is first required to submit a report to the joint standing
21 committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over judiciary matters, February 1,
22 2028. Subsequent reports are due annually by January 5th; and
23 7. Replace the appropriation and allocation section in Committee Amendment "A" to
24 change the source of the funding.
