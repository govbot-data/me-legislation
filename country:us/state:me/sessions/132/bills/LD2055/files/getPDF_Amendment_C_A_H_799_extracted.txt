9 SUMMARY
1100 This amendment clarifies language in the bill that prohibits a transferee who transfers
1111 a moose permit to a disabled veteran from obtaining another moose permit until the 4th
1122 calendar year after the issuance of the last permit. It also replaces the terms "fish eggs" and
1133 "fish spawn" with "gametes" in certain provisions of the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 12
1144 to clarify that the provisions apply to fish eggs and fish milt.
