COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1279, L.D. 1918
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, changes the title and
33 clarifies the list of entities to which sealed criminal history record information may be
44 disseminated; the basis for such dissemination; and the permissible use of the disseminated
55 sealed criminal history record information by the Department of Professional and Financial
66 Regulation, its agencies and boards and certain other entities. The amendment clarifies a
77 license applicant's obligation to disclose sealed criminal history record information to these
88 entities. The amendment also provides an effective date of April 1, 2027 for the provisions
99 that clarify the Criminal History Record Information Act with respect to criminal charges
1100 dismissed as the result of a plea agreement.
