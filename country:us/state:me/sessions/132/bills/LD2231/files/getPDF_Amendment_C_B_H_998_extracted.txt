13 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
14 following:
15 'Sec. 1. 10 MRSA §9093-B, sub-§4, as enacted by PL 2025, c. 399, §2, is amended
16 to read:
17 4. Mediation. Within 15 days of the date of the notice under subsection 3, paragraph
18 B, the parties shall mutually select an independent 3rd-party mediator to facilitate the
19 discussion of the proposed lot rent or fee increase. Within 30 days of the date the request
20 for mediation was mailed under subsection 3, the parties shall meet with the mediator for
21 the purpose of attempting to resolve the dispute. The owner of the manufactured housing
22 community shall pay all costs for the mediator and the mediation. If the owner of the
23 manufactured housing community acts in bad faith with respect to the request for mediation
24 or the mediation process, the lot rent or fee increase may not take effect for at least 6 months
25 from the date the request for mediation is mailed under subsection 3. Failure of the owner
26 of the manufactured housing community, or the owner's authorized representative, to
27 participate in mediation, is an example of bad faith. It is bad faith for the owner of the
28 manufactured housing community, or the owner's authorized representative, to require,
29 request or obtain a nondisclosure agreement or similar agreement from a manufactured
30 home owner as part of the mediation process.'
31 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
32 number to read consecutively.

COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1506, L.D. 2231
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment, which is the minority report of the committee, replaces the bill and
33 changes the title. The amendment clarifies that if an owner of a manufactured housing
44 community requires, requests or obtains a nondisclosure agreement or similar agreement
55 from a manufactured home owner in a manufactured housing community as part of the
66 mediation process, the community owner is acting in bad faith.
