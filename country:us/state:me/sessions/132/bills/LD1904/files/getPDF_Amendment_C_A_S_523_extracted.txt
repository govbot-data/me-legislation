13 'Sec. 1. 38 MRSA §450 is enacted to read:
14 §450. Municipal Shoreline Protection Legal Fund
15 1. Fund established. The Municipal Shoreline Protection Legal Fund, referred to in
16 this section as "the fund," is established within the department to provide financial
17 assistance to municipalities that have incurred or expect to incur legal costs in pursuing
18 shoreland zoning violations. The fund consists of money received from any public or
19 private source, including, but not limited to, any monetary penalties deposited in the fund
20 in accordance with section 480-R, subsection 1. The fund must be held separate and apart
21 from all other money, funds and accounts. Interest earned by the fund must be credited to
22 the fund. Any unexpended balances remaining in the fund at the end of any fiscal year do
23 not lapse and must be carried forward to the next fiscal year.
24 2. Administration; use of money in fund. The department shall administer the fund.
25 In accordance with rules adopted by the department pursuant to subsection 4, the
26 department shall use money in the fund to provide financial assistance to municipalities
27 that have incurred or expect to incur legal costs in pursuing shoreland zoning violations.
28 The department may use money in the fund to cover the department's actual costs incurred
29 in administering the fund.
30 3. Reimbursement. In accordance with rules adopted by the department pursuant to
31 subsection 4, a municipality that receives financial assistance from the fund under
32 subsection 2 may be required to reimburse the fund following the settlement or final
33 adjudication of the legal claim for which the financial assistance was received.
34 4. Rules. The department shall adopt rules necessary for the implementation and
35 administration of the fund, including, but not limited to, rules establishing standards for the
36 provision of financial assistance from the fund to a municipality pursuant to subsection 2

9 SUMMARY
1100 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill. It
1111 establishes the Municipal Shoreline Protection Legal Fund within the Department of
1122 Environmental Protection to provide financial assistance to municipalities that have
1133 incurred or expect to incur legal costs in pursuing shoreland zoning violations. In
1144 accordance with rules adopted by the department, a municipality that receives financial
1155 assistance from the fund may be required to reimburse the fund following the settlement or
1166 final adjudication of the legal claim for which the financial assistance was received.
17 The amendment also amends the Natural Resources Protection Act to direct that, of the
18 total penalties paid by all persons found to have violated that Act in any fiscal year, the
19 Commissioner of Environmental Protection shall ensure that $100,000 of that amount, or
20 the total amount of penalties paid in the fiscal year if that amount is less than $100,000, is
21 deposited in the Municipal Shoreline Protection Legal Fund. It also directs the State
22 Controller, on or before September 1, 2026, to transfer $100,000 from the unappropriated
23 surplus of the General Fund to the Department of Environmental Protection, Municipal
24 Shoreline Protection Legal Fund, Other Special Revenue Funds account and provides a
25 corresponding allocation.
