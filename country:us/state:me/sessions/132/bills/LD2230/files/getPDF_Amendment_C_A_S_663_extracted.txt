COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 912, L.D. 2230
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, does the following.
3 1. It removes reference to workforce housing from the current law governing the
4 Housing Opportunity Program.
5 2. It changes the type of information and the audience for information provided by the
6 Maine Office of Community Affairs under the Housing Opportunity Program.
7 3. It removes from the bill the requirement that the Maine Office of Community Affairs
8 adopt rules defining "low income," "moderate income" and "middle income."
9 4. It removes from the bill the requirement that the Maine Office of Community Affairs
10 adopt rules by January 1, 2027.
11 5. It limits the proposed Industrialized Housing Incentive Program to grants and not
12 loans and specifies the grant amounts available by type of housing unit.
13 6. It adds an organization representing building trade workers to the group of entities
14 the Department of Economic and Community Development must consult in designing the
15 construction extension partnership program required by the bill.
