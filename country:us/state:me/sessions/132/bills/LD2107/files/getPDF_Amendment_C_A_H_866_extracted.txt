13 'Sec. 1. 37-B MRSA §748 is enacted to read:
14 §748. Maine Nonprofit Security Grant Program
15 1. Definitions. As used in this section, unless the context otherwise indicates, the
16 following terms have the following meanings.
17 A. "Hate crime" means a crime in which the perpetrator selected the victim or the
18 property that was damaged or otherwise affected by the crime because of the race,
19 color, religion, sex, ancestry, national origin, physical or mental disability, sexual
20 orientation, gender identity or homelessness of the victim or of the owner or occupant
21 of that property.
22 B. "Program" means the Maine Nonprofit Security Grant Program established in
23 subsection 2.
24 C. "Serious bodily injury" has the same meaning as in Title 17-A, section 2, subsection
25 23.
26 D. "Terror attack" means an attack that causes serious bodily injury or death to multiple
27 persons or causes substantial damage to a structure and is made for the purpose of
28 intimidating or coercing a civilian population or to affect the conduct of government.
29 2. Program established. The Maine Nonprofit Security Grant Program is established
30 in the agency to provide grants to assist nonprofit organizations to improve security at
31 property owned or occupied by those nonprofit organizations to protect against hate crimes
32 and terror attacks.
33 3. Eligibility. To be eligible for a grant from the program, a nonprofit organization
34 must:
