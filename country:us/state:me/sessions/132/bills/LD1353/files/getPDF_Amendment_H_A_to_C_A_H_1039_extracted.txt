3 SUMMARY
44 This amendment amends Committee Amendment "A" to require the Commissioner of
55 Marine Resources to adopt rules by December 31, 2027 to establish a limited entry
66 commercial menhaden fishing license system that provides preference to individuals who
77 meet the eligibility standards established in Committee Amendment "A" and, based on
88 geographical location, reside closest to the eastern coast of Maine.
9 Until the commissioner establishes the limited entry system, the eligibility standards
10 must be used. The amendment also removes the provision of Committee Amendment "A"
11 that directs the Department of Marine Resources to identify groups that should be
12 prioritized for the first available licenses and develop an entry plan to provide the first
13 opportunity for licenses to individuals in the identified groups.
