COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 683, L.D. 1054
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. The amendment changes the
33 definitions of "conservation easement" and "holder" in the provisions of law governing
44 property and the conveyance of real estate.
5 Current law provides that "conservation easement" means a nonpossessory interest of
6 a holder in real property imposing limitations or affirmative obligations for certain
7 purposes. The amendment adds preserving the historical, architectural, archaeological or
8 cultural aspects of real property as one of those purposes. The amendment also clarifies
9 that "holder" includes a federally recognized Indian nation, tribe or band in the State.
