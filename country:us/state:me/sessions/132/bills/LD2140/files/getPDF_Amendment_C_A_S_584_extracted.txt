9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 858, L.D. 2140, “An Act to Reduce
10 Household Energy Costs in Response to Federal Cuts”
11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'Resolve, to Establish a Demand Response Program to Lower Electric Bills and
13 Improve Grid Reliability'
14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
15 'Sec. 1. Demand response program; established. Resolved: That the
16 Efficiency Maine Trust, referred to in this resolve as "the trust," currently administering
17 behind-the-meter demand management programs in the State, shall establish the demand
18 response program, referred to in this resolve as "the program," to encourage electricity
19 customers to reduce their consumption of electricity during periods of peak demand to help
20 lower their electric bills and improve grid reliability. The trust shall administer the program
21 through the end of fiscal year 2027-28. The trust may extend the program beyond that date,
22 in its discretion, if such an extension is proposed and approved in the trust's next triennial
23 plan pursuant to the Maine Revised Statutes, Tile 35-A, section 10104, subsection 4.
24 Sec. 2. Savings and reliability through reduction of peak demand.
25 Resolved: That, as a part of the program, the trust shall develop an outreach and education
26 campaign to increase participation in the State's transmission and distribution utilities opt-
27 in time-of-use rates to encourage shifting electricity consumption to off-peak periods. The
28 trust may not require an electricity customer to participate in the program. The program
29 must be designed to focus on savings opportunities most appropriate for the State's
30 electrical loads, grid planning and climate.
31 Sec. 3. Accessibility. Resolved: That the program must be designed to provide
32 outreach and education to renters, low-income electricity customers and customers having
33 the greatest potential for peak-demand electricity usage reductions, as determined by the
34 trust, and the trust shall prioritize outreach and education for low-income electricity
35 customers.

1 Sec. 4. Collaboration. Resolved: That the trust shall collaborate with the State's

9 the program.
10 Sec. 5. Report. Resolved: That the trust shall assess the efficacy and cost-
11 effectiveness of the program and, by February 28, 2028, submit a report to the joint standing
12 committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over energy matters regarding its
13 assessment. The trust shall include in the report any challenges it encountered in
14 administering the program and any opportunities the trust identifies for improving and
15 expanding efforts to shift electricity consumption to off-peak periods and to help lower

19 SUMMARY
2200 This amendment replaces the bill with a resolve. It requires the Efficiency Maine Trust
2211 to establish the demand response program to encourage electricity customers to reduce their
2222 consumption of electricity during periods of peak demand to help lower their electric bills
2233 and improve grid reliability. The program must be designed to provide outreach and
2244 education to renters, low-income electricity customers and customers having the greatest
2255 potential for peak-demand electricity usage reductions, and the trust is required to prioritize
2266 outreach and education for low-income electricity customers. The trust must assess the
2277 efficacy and cost-effectiveness of the program and, by February 28, 2028, submit a report
2288 to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over energy matters
2299 regarding its assessment.
