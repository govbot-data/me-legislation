6 ARTICLE 10
7 RETIREMENT BENEFIT IMPROVEMENT FUND
8 §17446. Retirement Benefit Improvement Fund
9 1. Establishment of fund. The Retirement Benefit Improvement Fund, referred to in
10 this section as "the fund," is established as an interest-bearing, nonlapsing fund for the
11 purpose of providing the resources necessary to increase the portion of the retirement
12 benefit in which cost-of-living adjustments are applied pursuant to section 17806,
13 subsection 1, paragraph A.
14 2. Content of fund. The fund consists of all resources transferred to the fund under
15 section 1536, subsection 1 and other resources made available to the fund.
16 3. Use of fund. Money in the fund must be used to pay for the cost-of-living
17 adjustment described by section 17806, subsection 1, paragraph A. The State Controller
18 shall notify the retirement system of the balance in the fund no later than August 1st of
19 each year. If the retirement system determines that the balance in the fund is sufficient to
20 increase by at least $500 the portion of the retirement benefit in which cost-of-living
21 adjustments are applied, the retirement system shall notify the State Controller. Upon such
22 notification, the State Controller shall transfer the funds in the fund to the retirement system
23 and the chief executive officer of the retirement system shall increase the portion of the
24 retirement benefits in which cost-of-living adjustments are applied beginning in September
25 of that year. If the balance in the reserve account is not sufficient to increase the portion of
26 the retirement benefits in which cost-of-living adjustments are applied by at least $500,
27 then the funds remain in the fund.
28 4. Report. The chief executive officer of the retirement system shall report at least
29 annually on or before the 2nd Friday in January to the joint standing committee of the
30 Legislature having jurisdiction over appropriations and financial affairs and the joint
31 standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over retirement matters on the
32 status of and activity in the fund.
