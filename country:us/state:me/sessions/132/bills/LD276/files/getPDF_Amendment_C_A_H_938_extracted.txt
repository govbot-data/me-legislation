1 1-A. Dealer to provide registration information. A dealer may not sell a new or
2 used oversized ATV without providing to the purchaser a written form developed and

4 ATV under section 13155, subsection 5‑B and the provisions of section 13157‑A regarding
5 the use of oversized ATVs on designated state-approved ATV trails. The department shall
6 develop a form for use by dealers under this section, which must include a check box, to
7 be filled out by the dealer, indicating whether the ATV is oversized or not, and a place for
8 a purchaser to sign acknowledging receipt of the form. The dealer shall provide the
9 purchaser and the department with a copy of the signed form after the sale. For purposes
10 of this subsection, "oversized ATV" has the same meaning as defined in section 13155,
11 subsection 5‑B.'

14 SUMMARY
1155 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft, and changes the title. The
1166 amendment changes all-terrain vehicle sale and registration processes in the following
1177 ways.
18 1. It requires all-terrain vehicle dealers to supply all-terrain vehicle purchasers with a
19 form with a check box indicating whether the all-terrain vehicle is oversized or not.
20 2. It directs all-terrain vehicle dealers to provide a copy of the signed form to the
21 purchaser of the all-terrain vehicle and to the Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife.
22 3. It establishes that an all-terrain vehicle owner must supply a copy of the signed form
23 in order to register an all-terrain vehicle.
