8 SENATE AMENDMENT “ ” to COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “A” to S.P. 598,
9 L.D. 1457, “Resolve, to Allow the Maine Turnpike Authority to Conduct a Pilot Program
10 to Implement Automated Speed Control Systems in Highway Work Zones”
11 Amend the amendment by inserting after the title the following:
12 'Amend the resolve by striking out the title and substituting the following:
13 'An Act to Increase the Fine for Speeding in a Construction Zone' '
14 Amend the amendment by striking out everything after the first instructional paragraph
15 and inserting the following:
16 'Be it enacted by the People of the State of Maine as follows:
17 Sec. 1. 29-A MRSA §2075, sub-§2, as amended by PL 1995, c. 151, §1, is further
18 amended to read:
19 2. Public ways under construction; fine. The Commissioner of Transportation may
20 restrict the speed limit on a public way under construction or during maintenance and the
21 Executive Director of the Maine Turnpike Authority may restrict the speed limit on any
22 portion of the turnpike under construction or during maintenance when a lower rate of
23 speed would minimize the danger of accident. A person may not operate a motor vehicle
24 in excess of these speeds, as long as notice of the maximum speed and the fine for a
25 violation of the maximum speed has been given by standard black and white speed limit
26 signs on the way. Signs erected pursuant to this subsection must be covered or removed
27 during hours when the speed limit is not restricted.
28 The penalty for a violation of this subsection is a fine equal to twice the amount of the fine
29 designated in accordance with Title 4, section 164, subsection 12 for a similar speeding
30 violation under section 2073, plus $500.'
31 Amend the amendment by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or
32 section number to read consecutively.
