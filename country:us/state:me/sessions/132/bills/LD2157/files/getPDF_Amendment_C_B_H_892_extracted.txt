30 SUMMARY
3311 This amendment, which is the minority report of the committee, does the following.
32 1. It removes a provision from the bill regarding the use of a drone to convey or attempt
33 to convey prison contraband in correctional facilities or jails.

COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1446, L.D. 2157
1 2. It removes a provision from the bill that establishes a Class C crime for unauthorized
2 use of a drone on or over the premises of a correctional facility or jail.
3 3. It creates a mens rea requirement that a person must intentionally or knowingly
4 operate a drone on or over the premises of a correctional facility or jail to be found guilty
5 of the unauthorized use of a drone. A violation of this provision is a Class D crime.
6 4. It adds a provision that if a person has previously been convicted for unauthorized
7 use of a drone on or over the premises of a correctional facility or jail or has been convicted
8 for substantially similar conduct in another jurisdiction, and the person subsequently uses
9 a drone on or over the premises of a correctional facility or jail without authorization, the
10 person is guilty of a Class C crime.
