8 SUMMARY
99 This amendment is the majority report of the committee. The amendment reallocates a
1100 provision of the bill and removes language in the bill referring to the Northeast Public
1111 Health Collaborative or its successor organization and recommendations related to
1122 vaccines made by the collaborative. Instead, the amendment refers to the recommendations
1133 related to vaccines made by the American Academy of Pediatrics, the American Academy
1144 of Family Physicians or the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists or their
1155 successor organizations. The amendment requires the Maine Vaccine Board to consider
1166 vaccines that are recommended by those organizations when making its annual
1177 determination of vaccines that must be made available through the Universal Immunization
1188 Program. If federal funding is not available for certain vaccines under the United States
1199 Department of Health and Human Services, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention,
2200 Vaccines for Children Program, the amendment also authorizes the Maine Vaccine Board
2211 to request state funding to cover the costs of vaccines for children who qualify for vaccines
2222 under the Vaccines for Children Program for those vaccines that are recommended by the
2233 Department of Health and Human Services or those organizations.
24 The amendment provides a pharmacist licensed in this State with immunity from
25 liability for negligence for administering a vaccine that is outside the guidelines
26 recommended by the United States Department of Health and Human Services, Centers for
27 Disease Control and Prevention Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices, or its
28 successor organization, as long as the administration of the vaccine is in accordance with
29 guidelines recommended by the State or those organizations.
