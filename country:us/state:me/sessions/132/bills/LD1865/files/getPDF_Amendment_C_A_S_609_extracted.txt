5 SUMMARY
66 This amendment is the majority report of the committee. The amendment directs the
77 Department of Economic and Community Development, instead of the Department of
88 Labor, as in the resolve, to establish and administer the Four-day Workweek Pilot Project
99 and provides the department authorization to work with the Department of Labor, the
1100 Finance Authority of Maine and institutions for higher education in the State for the
1111 purposes of promoting and evaluating the pilot project.
12 The amendment provides that the pilot project conclude no later than December 31,
13 2029.
14 The amendment replaces section 2 of the resolve, which directs the Department of
15 Labor and State Tax Assessor to develop a tax credit for the pilot project, with a provision
16 that authorizes, for the duration of the pilot project, a participating employer to be eligible
17 for a wellness program credit as established in the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 36, section
18 5219-FF.
