12 'Resolve, to Study Food Insecurity Measures in Maine'
13 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
14 'Sec. 1. Assessment and evaluation of food insecurity measures in State.
15 Resolved: That, in view of the fact that the United States Department of Agriculture
16 terminated its annual Household Food Security report, the Commissioner of Agriculture,
17 Conservation and Forestry shall study opportunities to assess and evaluate food insecurity
18 measures in the State and the State’s progress in its strategic plan to end hunger by 2030
19 under the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 7, section 2, subsection 6. The commissioner shall
20 seek input from the advisory committee established in Title 7, section 2, subsection 6,
21 outside organizations and other state agencies as necessary in conducting the study. The
22 commissioner may contract with external agencies to study feasible methods to measure
23 and track food insecurity in the State over time, if sufficient funding has been provided for
24 this purpose. This study may include reviewing existing measurements of disparities of
25 food insecurity in target populations, including, but not limited to, those defined by age,
26 geography, income, race and veteran status, as defined by federal and state policy. The
27 study may include a gap analysis to identify missing or incomplete data sets. Results of
28 the study must be published and made available on a publicly accessible website.
29 Sec. 2. Acceptance of funds for study. Resolved: That the Commissioner of
30 Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry may accept funds from any public or private source
31 for the purposes of the study under section 1. Funds must be transferred into a separate,
32 nonlapsing account, administered by the Department of Agriculture, Conservation and
33 Forestry, for the purposes of this resolve and may not be used for any other purpose.
34 Sec. 3. Report. Resolved: That, no later than January 15, 2027, the Commissioner
35 of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry shall submit a report to the joint standing
36 committees of the Legislature having jurisdiction over agriculture, conservation and
