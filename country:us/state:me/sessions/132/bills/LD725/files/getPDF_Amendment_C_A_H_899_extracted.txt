11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'Resolve, Directing the Efficiency Maine Trust to Study the Quality of Jobs It Creates
13 in Consultation with an Advisory Group'
14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
15 'Sec. 1. Efficiency Maine Trust study. Resolved: That the Efficiency Maine
16 Trust, established in the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 35-A, section 10103 and referred to
17 in this resolve as "the trust," shall study, within existing resources and in consultation with
18 the advisory group under section 2, the quality of jobs created through projects funded by
19 the trust pursuant to Title 35-A, section 10104. As part of the study, the trust, in consultation
20 with the advisory group, shall prepare a survey to study the quality of jobs created through
21 projects funded by the trust and shall distribute that survey to qualified partners that employ
22 3 or more craft workers. The findings of the survey must be used to identify workforce
23 development needs and any existing plans to address these needs and to develop the
24 findings and recommendations of the study.
25 As used in this resolve, "qualified partner" means an installer that has completed an
26 application and received acceptance by the trust to serve as an Efficiency Maine Qualified
27 Partner; and "craft worker" has the same meaning as in Title 26, section 1304, subsection
28 3-A.
29 Sec. 2. Advisory group; members and duties. Resolved: That the trust shall
30 convene an advisory group to consult with the trust regarding the study described in section
31 1, including regarding the development of the survey described in section 1 and the analysis
32 of the data collected through that survey. The director of the trust or the director's designee
33 shall participate in and oversee the advisory group and shall invite at least one
34 representative from the Maine Labor Climate Council and any additional members
35 considered necessary by the director to ensure comprehensive stakeholder representation.
36 The advisory group shall assist the trust in:

19 SUMMARY
2200 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill, which
2211 is a concept draft, with a resolve that directs the Efficiency Maine Trust to study, within
2222 existing resources, the quality of jobs created due to projects funded by the trust. The
2233 amendment directs the trust to convene an advisory group to inform and consult on the
2244 development of a survey and to assist in the analysis of the survey results. The findings of
2255 the survey must be used to develop the findings and recommendations of the study. The
2266 amendment requires the director of the trust to submit a report to the joint standing
2277 committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over labor matters setting forth the
2288 findings and recommendations of the study and authorizes the committee to report out
2299 legislation related to the report to the 133rd Legislature in 2027.
