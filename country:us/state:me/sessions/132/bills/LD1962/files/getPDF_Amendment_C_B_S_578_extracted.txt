19 1. Appointment. The Except as provided in paragraph H, the Governor shall appoint
20 a board of 7 visitors for each correctional facility under the department, as authorized by
21 Title 5, section 12004‑I, subsection 5.
22 A. The terms of the members of the boards of visitors are for 3 years.
23 B. Members of the boards of visitors are eligible for reappointment at the expiration
24 of their terms.
25 C. A member of the Legislature or an employee of the department may not serve on
26 any board of visitors.
27 D. At least one member of each board must be a person licensed by this State to provide
28 mental health services.
29 E. Each member of the boards of visitors must be compensated according to the
30 provisions of Title 5, chapter 379.
31 F. The Governor shall appoint a chair from the membership.
32 G. At least one member of each board of visitors must have been incarcerated in a
33 correctional facility.

8 Sec. 2. 34-A MRSA §3001-B is enacted to read:
9 §3001-B. Legislator access to correctional facilities and detention facilities
10 A Legislator may access correctional facilities and detention facilities in accordance
11 with rules adopted by the commissioner. In accordance with section 1402, subsection 3,
12 the commissioner shall adopt rules governing a Legislator's access to correctional facilities
13 and detention facilities. Rules adopted pursuant to this subsection are major substantive
14 rules as defined in Title 5, chapter 375, subchapter 2-A.
15 Sec. 3. Rules regarding Legislator access. The Commissioner of Corrections
16 shall submit major substantive rules to the Legislature pursuant to the Maine Revised
17 Statutes, Title 34-A, section 3001-B by January 31, 2027. The rules must include provisions
18 regarding:
19 1. A Legislator's access to physical areas of correctional facilities and detention
20 facilities;
21 2. A Legislator's access to clients and staff; and
22 3. The circumstances in which a chief administrative officer may deny a Legislator
23 access to correctional facilities and detention facilities.'
24 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
25 number to read consecutively.
26 SUMMARY
2277 This amendment, which is a minority report of the committee, changes the title and
2288 replaces the bill. The amendment requires the Commissioner of Corrections to provide
2299 notice to the Governor, the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of
3300 Representatives within 5 days of a vacancy on a board of visitors. If the vacancy is not
3311 filled by the Governor within 90 days, the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the
3322 House of Representatives must jointly appoint a member to fill the vacancy. The
3333 amendment also requires the commissioner to adopt major substantive rules governing a
3344 Legislator's access to correctional facilities and detention facilities. The rules must be
3355 submitted to the Legislature by January 31, 2027.
