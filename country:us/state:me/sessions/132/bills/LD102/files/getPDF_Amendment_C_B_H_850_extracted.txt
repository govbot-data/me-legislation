11 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
12 following:
13 'Sec. 1. 15 MRSA §3308-A, sub-§4-A is enacted to read:
14 4-A. Limited dissemination; escaped juveniles. Notwithstanding any provision of
15 this Part or in Title 34-A, section 1216 to the contrary, when a juvenile has committed a
16 juvenile crime that would constitute escape under Title 17-A, section 755, subsection 1,
17 1-C or 1-D if the juvenile were an adult and there is probable cause to believe the juvenile
18 presents a serious risk to the juvenile's own safety or to public safety, the head of a criminal
19 justice agency, or that person's designee, may publicly disclose the following information
20 for the purpose of identifying and apprehending the juvenile:
21 A. The juvenile’s physical description;
22 B. A photograph of the juvenile;
23 C. Any recent location of the juvenile;
24 D. Any likely location of the juvenile;
25 E. The fact that the criminal justice agency is searching for the juvenile; and
26 F. Instructions for the public not to approach the juvenile, but to report any sightings
27 or relevant information to a law enforcement agency.'
28 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
29 number to read consecutively.
