11 SUMMARY
1122 This amendment, which is a minority report of the committee, replaces the bill, which
1133 is a concept draft, and changes the title. The amendment provides that a member of the
1144 Legislature, or an immediate family member of a member of the Legislature, who is issued
1155 a permit to carry a concealed handgun must be reimbursed for the cost of completing a
1166 handgun safety course if that member or that member's immediate family member was
1177 required to take the handgun safety course to be eligible for the permit. "Immediate family
1188 member" is defined to mean a member's spouse, child, sibling, parent, grandparent or
1199 grandchild who is at least 18 years of age and who resides in the same place as the member.
2200 It directs an issuing authority to waive the permit fee for a permit issued to a member of
2211 the Legislature. The amendment also requires that rules adopted by certain state agencies
2222 governing security at the state capitol area must allow a member of the Legislature to carry
2233 a handgun, including a concealed handgun, in the capitol area as long as the member of the
2244 Legislature has received a concealed handgun permit.
