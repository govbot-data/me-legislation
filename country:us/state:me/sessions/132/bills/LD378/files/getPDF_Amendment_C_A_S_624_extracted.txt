11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'An Act to Clarify That Health Insurers Must Comply with Plan Sponsors' Statutory
13 Rights to Audit Claims and Data Requests Related to Those Audits'
14 Amend the bill by inserting after the title and before the enacting clause the following:
15 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
16 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
17 Whereas, current law requires 3rd-party administrators of health plans to grant plan
18 sponsors certain audit rights and to provide the requisite medical and pharmacy data to
19 conduct those audits; and
20 Whereas, this legislation clarifies that law to ensure that health insurers administering
21 health plans on behalf of plan sponsors are considered administrators and must comply
22 with a plan sponsor's statutory right to audit claims and data requests related to those audits;
23 and
24 Whereas, this legislation needs to take effect without delay so that plan sponsors can
25 include these audit rights in contracts with administrators that are being renewed, amended
26 or entered into prior to the 2027 plan year; and
27 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
28 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
29 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
30 therefore,'

29 SUMMARY
3300 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft, changes the title and adds
3311 an emergency preamble and an emergency clause. It clarifies current law to ensure that
3322 health insurers administering health plans on behalf of plan sponsors are considered
3333 administrators and must comply with a plan sponsor's statutory right to audit claims and
3344 data requests related to those audits. The amendment also clarifies the responsibilities of
3355 plan sponsors related to the security of data shared by an administrator for the purposes of
3366 an audit. The requirements take effect July 1, 2026.
