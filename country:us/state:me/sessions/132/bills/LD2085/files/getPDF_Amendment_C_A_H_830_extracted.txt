1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. The amendment provides that
33 a person in the employment of the Department of Public Safety on August 1, 2026 or hired
44 thereafter who has the job classification of emergency communications director is eligible
55 for retirement benefits under the 1998 Special Plan.
66 The amendment also includes an allocation of $2,467 from the Consolidated
77 Emergency Communications Fund to the department in fiscal year 2026-27 to add the
88 position to the 1998 Special Plan.
