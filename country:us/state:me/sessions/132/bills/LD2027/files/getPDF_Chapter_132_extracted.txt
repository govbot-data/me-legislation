Resolve, to Rename a Bridge in Woolwich the Woolwich Veterans Memorial
Bridge
Sec. 1. Bridge in Woolwich renamed. Resolved: That the Department of
Transportation shall designate Bridge 3039 on Main Street in the Town of Woolwich,
currently known as the Station 46 Bridge, the Woolwich Veterans Memorial Bridge.
