COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 217, L.D. 555
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment is the minority report of the committee. It changes the reporting and
33 effective dates from 2026 to 2027. It requires the Commissioner of Child and Family
44 Services to include a transition plan with the report to the joint standing committee of the
55 Legislature having jurisdiction over child welfare matters regarding the establishment and
66 implementation of the Department of Child and Family Services. It prohibits any person
77 who served as a commissioner or deputy commissioner of the Department of Health and
88 Human Services or as a director or deputy director or associate director of the Office of
99 Child and Family Services between January 1, 2019 and January 6, 2027 from being
1100 employed at the Department of Child and Family Services. It also adds an appropriations
1111 and allocations section.
