17 §1021. Definitions
18 As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise indicates, the following terms
19 have the following meanings.
20 1. Advisory committee. "Advisory committee" means a committee consisting of
21 members selected by each chamber using the process described in this chapter to perform
22 the duties described in this chapter.
23 2. Article V convention. "Article V convention" or "convention" means a convention
24 called pursuant to the United States Constitution, Article V for proposing amendments to
25 the United States Constitution.
26 3. Chamber. "Chamber" means either the Senate or the House of Representatives of
27 the Legislature.
28 4. Commissioner. "Commissioner" means a person selected by resolution of the
29 Legislature as provided in this chapter to represent the State at an Article V convention.
30 "Commissioner" includes an interim commissioner selected by the advisory committee
31 pursuant to section 1028 to fill a vacancy in the delegation.

7 §1022. Applicability
8 This chapter applies to an Article V convention.
9 §1023. Qualifications of commissioners
10 At the time of selection and throughout an Article V convention, a commissioner must
11 meet the following criteria.
12 1. Citizenship. The commissioner must be a United States citizen and have been a
13 United States citizen for at least 5 years prior to the date of selection.
14 2. State residency. The commissioner must be a resident of the State and have been
15 a resident of the State for at least 5 years prior to the date of selection.
16 3. Age. The commissioner must be at least 25 years of age.
17 4. Registered voter. The commissioner must be a registered voter in the State.
18 5. Federal lobbyist restriction. The commissioner may not be registered or required
19 to be registered as a federal lobbyist or have been registered or required to be registered as
20 a federal lobbyist at any time within the 5 years prior to the date of selection.
21 6. Federal employee restriction. The commissioner may not be a federal employee
22 or contractor and may not have been a federal employee or contractor within the 10 years
23 prior to the date of selection. This restriction does not apply to members of the United
24 States Armed Forces.
25 7. Federal official restriction. The commissioner may not have held a federal elected
26 or appointed office at any time within the 10 years prior to the date of selection.
27 8. Felony conviction. The commissioner may not have been convicted of a violation
28 of a state or federal criminal law involving moral turpitude that is punishable by
29 imprisonment for one year or more.
30 9. Statewide public office restriction. The commissioner may not hold a statewide
31 public office, which includes, but is not limited to, Governor, Secretary of State, Treasurer
32 of State, State Auditor or Attorney General, while performing the duties of commissioner.
33 For the purposes of this subsection, serving as a state Legislator is not considered holding
34 a statewide public office.

40 number of seats in the Legislature and at least 2 commissioners must be members of the
41 party holding the 2nd largest number of seats in the Legislature. All commissioners must

14 §1025. Commissioning resolution
15 A commissioning resolution must include, but is not limited to, the following
16 components.
17 1. Convention rules; voting restrictions. A commissioner may not:
18 A. Vote for or otherwise promote any change to the traditional convention rule or
19 decision on the floor and in the committee of the whole that each state has one vote;
20 B. Vote for or otherwise promote a convention rule that does not require recorded roll
21 call votes or the electronic equivalent;
22 C. Propose an amendment with subject matter that is inconsistent with the resolutions
23 of the legislatures of the several states that were the basis of calling the convention. A
24 proposed amendment is not germane unless its subject matter is consistent with the
25 resolutions of the legislatures of the several states that were the basis of calling the
26 convention; or
27 D. Vote in favor of any proposed amendment that would alter the text of the specific
28 guarantees of individual liberty established by the United States Constitution, including
29 the original United States Constitution, the Bill of Rights and the following
30 amendments: Amendment XIII, Amendment XIV, Amendment XV, Amendment XIX,
31 Amendment XXIII, Amendment XXIV and Amendment XXVI.
32 2. Scope of authority. The commissioning resolution must clearly state the scope of
33 the commissioners' authority, which must be limited to:
34 A. The subject matter enumerated in the 34 state applications that triggered the
35 convention if this State was not one of the 2/3 of the states applying for the Article V
36 convention or the subject matter in this State's application if this State was one of the
37 2/3 of the states applying for the Article V convention; and
38 B. Any additional instructions from the Legislature, whether provided in the
39 commissioning resolution or issued by the Legislature after the adoption of the
40 commissioning resolution.

17 §1028. Vacancies
18 A commissioner vacancy must be filled by the advisory committee, which shall select
19 an interim commissioner until such time as a vote by a joint session of the Legislature
20 selects a permanent replacement.
21 §1029. Compensation and expenses
22 Commissioners must be compensated for attending a meeting of the convention as
23 follows.
24 1. Members of Legislature. A commissioner who is a member of the Legislature is
25 entitled to receive per diem and reimbursement of expenses and mileage equal to that
26 received by a member of the Legislature for attending a special session outside the time
27 specified for a first regular session or second regular session pursuant to section 2 for
28 attending a meeting of the convention.
29 2. Members of public. Except as provided in subsection 3, a commissioner who is a
30 member of the public is entitled to receive the same per diem and reimbursement of
31 expenses and mileage provided to members of the Legislature for attending a meeting of
32 the convention as provided under subsection 1.
33 3. Commissioners otherwise compensated. A commissioner who is serving in that
34 commissioner's capacity as an employee of this State as defined by Title 5, section 20,
35 subsection 1 is not entitled to receive additional compensation for attending meetings of
36 the convention but is entitled to reimbursement for expenses and mileage.
37 §1030. Emolument and gift prohibition
38 A commissioner may not accept, during that commissioner's time of service, any gifts
39 or benefits with a combined value of more than $200 other than from a member of that
40 commissioner's family and of the kind customarily granted by a member of one's family.

3 §1031. Delegation procedures and standards of behavior
4 The following procedures and standards of behavior apply to members of the
5 delegation.
6 1. Assigning roles. The commissioners within the delegation shall choose from
7 among them a commissioner to chair the delegation, a commissioner to cast the State's vote
8 on the convention floor and a commissioner to communicate with the media on behalf of
9 the delegation. If the delegation so decides, the same commissioner may exercise any 2 or
10 all 3 roles. The delegation may designate a different commissioner to perform any of the
11 roles at any time.
12 2. Avoiding public implications of division. Each commissioner shall take care to
13 avoid communicating the impression to any person outside the delegation that the
14 delegation is divided on a question on which the delegation has taken a formal position,
15 including but not limited to casting a vote.
16 3. Prohibition on media engagement. A commissioner other than the commissioner
17 designated to communicate with the media on behalf of the delegation may not
18 communicate with the media about convention business during the convention or during
19 any temporary recess or temporary adjournment of the convention.
20 4. Violations of conduct by members of delegation. A commissioner who violates
21 subsection 2 or 3 may be recalled or suspended by the advisory committee or by the
22 Legislature in accordance with this chapter.
23 5. Presentation of opinions to delegation. Subsections 2 and 3 may not be construed
24 to prevent a commissioner from presenting that commissioner's opinions to the convention
25 or debating a matter at the convention on which the delegation has not formally taken a
26 position.
27 6. Quorum. A majority of commissioners present and voting at the time the delegation
28 is polled constitutes a quorum for decisions by the delegation, including the designation of
29 commissioners for particular duties and the determination of how the State's vote will be
30 cast. A decision may not be made and a vote may not be cast if less than a majority of the
31 delegation votes in the poll.
32 7. Rule of decision within delegation. A quorum being present, a rule of decision
33 must be a majority of those present and voting at the time of polling.
34 §1032. Advisory committee
35 The following provisions govern the membership and conduct of the advisory
36 committee.
37 1. Membership; chair. The advisory committee consists of the following members:
38 a State Senator selected by the President of the Senate; a State Representative selected by
39 the Speaker of the House; and a member of the Legislature nominated jointly by the
40 President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House and approved by a majority of those
41 present and voting in each chamber. The advisory committee shall select one of its
42 members as chair.

1 2. Advising function. The advisory committee may develop appropriate procedures
2 and mechanisms for monitoring the convention and its committees and subcommittees. A
3 commissioner may request that the advisory committee provide advice as to whether a
4 prospective action by the commissioner would violate the commissioning resolution or any
5 subsequent instructions. The advisory committee shall communicate to the commissioner
6 requesting such advice a determination within 24 hours of receiving the request. The
7 advisory committee may communicate the determination by any appropriate means.
8 3. Staffing. Only upon the approval of the Legislative Council, Legislative Council
9 staff may provide staffing assistance to the advisory committee, except that Legislative
10 Council staff support is not authorized when the Legislature is in regular or special session.
11 §1033. Advisory committee authority
12 The following provisions govern the exercise of the advisory committee's authority.
13 1. Notification. If the advisory committee has reason to believe that a commissioner
14 has exceeded the scope of the commissioner's authority, the advisory committee shall notify
15 the President of the Senate, the Speaker of the House and the Attorney General.
16 2. Issuing determination. Upon a request by the President of the Senate, the Speaker
17 of the House or the Attorney General for a determination on whether a commissioner has
18 exceeded the scope of the commissioner's authority pursuant to section 1025, subsection 2,
19 the advisory committee shall expeditiously make a determination on whether the
20 commissioner has exceeded the scope of the commissioner's authority and immediately
21 communicate the determination to the President of the Senate, the Speaker of the House or
22 the Attorney General, as applicable.
23 3. Removal. Upon determining that a commissioner has exceeded the scope of the
24 commissioner's authority pursuant to this section, the advisory committee shall
25 immediately remove that commissioner and communicate that action and the reasons for
26 that action to the President of the Senate, the Speaker of the House, the Attorney General
27 and the presiding officers of the convention.
28 §1034. Interference with commissioner's duties
29 A person who intentionally or knowingly bribes, threatens, intimidates or obstructs or
30 attempts to bribe, threaten, intimidate or obstruct a commissioner in the performance of the
31 commissioner's duties at an Article V convention commits a Class C crime.'

34 SUMMARY
3355 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill, which
3366 is a concept draft. The amendment establishes a process for the selection and oversight of
3377 delegates, referred to in the bill as "commissioners," to a convention under Article V of the
3388 United States Constitution assembled for the purpose of proposing amendments to the
3399 United States Constitution.
