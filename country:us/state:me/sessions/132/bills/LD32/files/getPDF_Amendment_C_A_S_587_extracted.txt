COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 49, L.D. 32
1 A. The duties of the interconnection ombudsman include but are not limited to:
2 (1) Tracking interconnection disputes;
3 (2) Facilitating the efficient and fair resolution of disputes between customers
4 seeking to interconnect and investor-owned transmission and distribution utilities;
5 (3) Reviewing investor-owned transmission and distribution utility
6 interconnection policies to assess opportunities for reducing interconnection
7 disputes;
8 (4) Convening stakeholder groups as necessary to facilitate effective
9 communication between interconnection stakeholders;
10 (5) Preparing reports that detail the number, type, resolution timeline and outcome
11 of interconnection disputes; and
12 (6) Any other technical or regulatory work involving public utilities.
13 B. The commission by rule shall establish a fee to be paid by persons seeking
14 interconnections to fund the interconnection ombudsman. Notwithstanding Title 5,
15 section 8071, rules adopted under this paragraph are routine technical rules as defined
16 in Title 5, chapter 375, subchapter 2‑A.
17 C. The interconnection ombudsman fund is established within the commission as a
18 nonlapsing fund for the purposes of offsetting costs for interconnection‑related
19 activities undertaken by the interconnection ombudsman pursuant to paragraph A,
20 subparagraphs (1), (2), (3), (4) and (5). The commission shall deposit all fees collected
21 under this subsection into the fund and all money in the fund must be used to fund the
22 interconnection ombudsman. In addition to the fees established in accordance with this
23 subsection, the fund may accept federal money and contributions from private and
24 public sources.
25 Any activities undertaken by the interconnection ombudsman pursuant to paragraph A,
26 subparagraph (6) must be funded in accordance with section 116.'
27 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
28 number to read consecutively.
29 SUMMARY
3300 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. It removes the requirement that
3311 the interconnection ombudsman be appointed by the Public Utilities Commission and
3322 instead requires that the commission designate an interconnection ombudsman. It also
3333 clarifies the activities that may be funded by the interconnection ombudsman fund
3344 established within the Public Utilities Commission.
