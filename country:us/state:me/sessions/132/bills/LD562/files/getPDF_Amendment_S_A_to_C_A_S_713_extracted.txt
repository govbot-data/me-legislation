SENATE AMENDMENT “ ” to COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “A” to S.P. 241, L.D. 562
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment amends Committee Amendment "A" to reduce the increases made by
33 Committee Amendment "A" to travel reimbursement rates for transporting a body to
44 Augusta at the request of the Office of Chief Medical Examiner. Under this amendment,
55 the reimbursement rates are $135 for the first 25 miles, $2.20 per mile for the next 25 miles
66 and $2 per mile for miles in excess of 50 miles. This amendment also removes the ongoing
77 funding to increase the transportation reimbursement rate in fiscal year 2025-26 and
88 decreases the appropriation in fiscal year 2026-27.
