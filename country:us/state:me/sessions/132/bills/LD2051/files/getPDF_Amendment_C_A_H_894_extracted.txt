29 SUMMARY
3300 This amendment replaces the bill. It repeals language describing eligibility for the state
3311 Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program. It adds language clarifying current eligibility
3322 and establishing eligibility for the program for individuals who became ineligible for the
3333 federal Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program under Public Law 119-21 (2025),
3344 commonly known as the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.
