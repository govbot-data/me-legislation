10 Access to Affordable Prescription Drugs in Underserved Areas”
11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'Resolve, to Improve Access to Affordable Prescription Drugs in Underserved Areas'
13 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
14 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
15 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
16 Whereas, federally qualified health centers serve rural and other underserved areas,
17 delivering health care that would otherwise be inaccessible to residents of those areas; and
18 Whereas, the number of retail pharmacies and the hours of operation of retail
19 pharmacies in underserved areas of the State have been dramatically reduced in recent years
20 due to workforce challenges and other economic challenges; and
21 Whereas, federally qualified health centers can compensate for the decline in
22 availability of retail pharmacies and for the restrictive practices of prescription drug
23 manufacturers only by developing or expanding their capacity to provide retail pharmacy
24 services as part of their health center services, yet existing health center revenues and
25 resources are insufficient to support such development and expansion; and
26 Whereas, it is therefore essential to provide immediate funding support for federally
27 qualified health centers to develop and expand retail pharmacy capacity in this State to
28 address the critical shortage of access to affordable prescription drugs; and
29 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
30 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
31 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
32 therefore, be it
33 Sec. 1. Department of Health and Human Services to distribute funding to
34 expand retail pharmacy services in federally qualified health centers.

88 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill, which
99 is a concept draft, with a resolve. The amendment provides funding to the Department of
1100 Health and Human Services, Maine Center for Disease Control and Prevention to expand
1111 retail and mobile pharmacy services in federally qualified health centers. The amendment
1122 directs the department to, no later than December 15, 2026 and upon the receipt of a
1133 proposal to expand pharmacy services and a proposed budget, grant funds in specified
1144 amounts to 9 federally qualified health centers that serve residents of the State in rural and
1155 underserved communities to support the planning, designing, construction and expansion
1166 of one or more licensed retail or mobile pharmacies as part of the services offered by those
1177 federally qualified health centers. The amendment requires the Maine Primary Care
1188 Association to, no later than February 15, 2027, report to the joint standing committee of
1199 the Legislature having jurisdiction over health coverage, insurance and financial services
2200 matters on the grant funds provided to the federally qualified health centers, including
2211 information on the status of implementation of the proposals to expand retail and mobile
2222 pharmacy services, an updated proposal budget and an accounting of any money spent.
