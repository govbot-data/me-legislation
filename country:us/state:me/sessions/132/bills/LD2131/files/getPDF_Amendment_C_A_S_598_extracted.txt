13 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
14 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
15 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
16 Whereas, since 2014, 29 nursing facilities have closed across the State, and these
17 closures have been driven in large part by MaineCare reimbursement rates and a workforce
18 crisis that threatens the stability of long-term care services; and
19 Whereas, Maine has the oldest population in the nation, and the demand for long-
20 term care services continues to rise even as access to such care rapidly declines, placing
21 vulnerable older and disabled adults at immediate risk; and
22 Whereas, severe workforce shortages within nursing facilities have created ongoing
23 instability in the sector, and timely adjustments to MaineCare reimbursement rates for
24 nursing facilities are essential to support the recruitment and retention of qualified staff
25 necessary to ensure the safety and well-being of residents; and
26 Whereas, the Legislature established the Nursing Facility Reform Transition Fund to
27 assist facilities in moving toward fully implemented MaineCare rate reforms by calendar
28 year 2028, and all facilities should receive cost-of-living increases in their reimbursement
29 rates during this transition; and
30 Whereas, unappropriated General Fund surplus amounts were transferred to the
31 Department of Health and Human Services, Nursing Facilities program, Other Special
32 Revenue Funds account, including by Part SSS of Public Law 2023, chapter 643, to provide
33 the financial support necessary for facilities to implement new nursing facility rates
34 beginning January 1, 2025; and

1 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
2 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
3 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
4 therefore, be it
5 Sec. 1. Department of Health and Human Services to amend its rules to
6 provide cost-of-living adjustments. Resolved: That, as soon as practicable, the
7 Department of Health and Human Services shall amend its rule Chapter 101: MaineCare
8 Benefits Manual, Chapter III, Section 67, Principles of Reimbursement for Nursing
9 Facilities, to remove the prohibition on adjusting the guardrails for inflation and require
10 that the guardrails used in the 3-year transition period to the new reimbursement
11 methodology are adjusted for inflation. The cost-of-living adjustment applied to rates for
12 certain MaineCare services under Public Law 2025, chapter 388, Part GGG on January 1,
13 2026 must be applied to the guardrails and retroactively applied to January 1, 2026. The
14 guardrails must be adjusted for any subsequently implemented cost-of-living adjustment
15 until the end of the rate reform transition period or until guardrails are no longer applied,
16 whichever is later.
17 Sec. 2. Nursing Facility Reform Transition Fund stakeholder group.
18 Resolved: That the Department of Health and Human Services shall convene a
19 stakeholder group to examine the use of the Nursing Facility Reform Transition Fund. The
20 stakeholder group must include, but is not limited to, representatives from the department,
21 representatives of a statewide organization that represents nursing facilities, operators of
22 nursing facilities and the long-term care ombudsman described under the Maine Revised
23 Statutes, Title 22, section 5107-A. The department shall ensure that meetings are open to
24 the public and interested parties are provided with notice of meetings. The stakeholder
25 group shall examine the following issues related to the Nursing Facility Reform Transition
26 Fund established in Public Law 2023, chapter 643, Part SSS:
27 1. The uses for funding that has been spent from the fund;
28 2. The plan for the expenditure of the remaining funds in the fund;
29 3. The planned cycle for rebasing reimbursement rates; and
30 4. The use of the fund to pay for cost-of-living increases for staff pay compared to the
31 use of other funding sources for other cost-of-living increases for direct care services.
32 The stakeholder group shall submit a report, no later than January 15, 2027, to the joint
33 standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over health and human services
34 matters. The committee is authorized to report out legislation related to the report to the
35 133rd Legislature in 2027.
36 Emergency clause. In view of the emergency cited in the preamble, this legislation
37 takes effect when approved.'
38 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
39 number to read consecutively.
