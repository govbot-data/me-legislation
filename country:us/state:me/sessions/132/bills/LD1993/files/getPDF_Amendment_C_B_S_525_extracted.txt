1111 The bill raises the limit on the total annual amount of the assessment that supports the
1122 Department of Labor's Safety Education and Training Fund from 1% to 2% of the total of
1133 the workers' compensation benefits paid by all licensed carriers and group and individual
1144 self-insured employers during the most recent calendar year for which data is available.
1155 This amendment changes that amount to 1.25%.
16 The amendment also directs the Commissioner of Labor or the commissioner's
17 designee to conduct an efficiency review of the operations and delivery of the safety
18 education and training programs the Department of Labor provides to identify any
19 duplicative efforts offered by the department and by private employers. The amendment
20 also requires the commissioner to submit a report on the findings of the review to the joint
21 standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over labor matters and provides
22 the committee the authority to report out legislation to the 133rd Legislature in 2027. The
23 amendment adds an appropriations and allocations section.
