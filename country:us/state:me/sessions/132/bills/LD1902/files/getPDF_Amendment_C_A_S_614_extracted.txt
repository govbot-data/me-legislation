13 The following provisions apply to the operation of electronic sealed ticket games by

21 2. Electronic sealed ticket game system; operation. A licensed organization may
22 lease an electronic sealed ticket game system only from a licensed electronic sealed ticket

28 electronic sealed ticket game system.

33 ticket game terminal may issue to a player only a printed voucher for any prize, including
34 a monetary prize or single set of prizes won by that player, or for any remaining amount
35 due to the player at the end of play. An electronic sealed ticket game terminal may not
36 dispense cash to a player.
37 4. Number and location of electronic sealed ticket game terminals. A licensed
38 organization may operate the following number of electronic sealed ticket game terminals
39 per eligible premises of the licensed organization:
40 A. No more than 4 electronic sealed ticket game terminals for a licensed organization

6 SUMMARY
77 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. The amendment authorizes the
88 Gambling Control Unit to issue licenses to operate electronic sealed ticket games to bona
99 fide fraternal, patriotic or veterans' organizations that are eligible for a license to conduct
1100 games of chance and that have restricted social quarters on their premises designated for
1111 use predominately by members of the organization for social and recreational activities,
1122 that are accessible exclusively to members of the organization and their guests and that are
1133 not advertised or open to the general public. It also authorizes the Gambling Control Unit
1144 to issue a license to an electronic sealed ticket game system distributor. The amendment
1155 establishes operating requirements for licensed organizations, including limits on the
1166 location and number of terminals, requirements related to playing an electronic sealed
1177 ticket game and reporting requirements. It also establishes requirements for distributors,
1188 which also include reporting requirements. The amendment includes definitions for
1199 relevant terms and makes other changes to the laws governing games of chance related to
2200 records and reports and games conducted during a beano game.
