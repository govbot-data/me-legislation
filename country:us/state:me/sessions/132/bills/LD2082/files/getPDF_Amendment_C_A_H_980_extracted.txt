13 'Sec. 1. 10 MRSA §1500-EE is enacted to read:
14 §1500-EE. Use of artificial intelligence in therapy or psychotherapy services
15 prohibited unless provided by licensed professional
16 1. Definitions. As used in this section, unless the context otherwise indicates, the
17 following terms have the following meanings.
18 A. "Artificial intelligence" means a machine-based system that, for explicit or implicit
19 objectives, infers, from the input it receives, how to generate outputs, such as
20 predictions, content, recommendations or decisions, that can influence physical or
21 virtual environments.
22 B. "Licensed professional" means an individual who holds a valid license or certificate
23 issued in this State to practice psychotherapy or behavioral health therapy, including:
24 (1) An individual licensed pursuant to Title 32, chapter 56;
25 (2) An individual licensed pursuant to Title 32, chapter 83;
26 (3) An individual licensed pursuant to Title 32, chapter 119;
27 (4) An individual licensed or certified pursuant to Title 32, chapter 81 and
28 authorized to provide therapy or psychotherapy services;
29 (5) An individual licensed pursuant to Title 32, chapter 31; and
30 (6) A physician or physician associate licensed under Title 32, chapter 36 or 48,
31 who specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders.
32 C. "Therapeutic communication" means any verbal, nonverbal or written interaction,
33 conducted in a clinical or professional setting, that is intended to diagnose, treat or

325 communication" includes, but is not limited to:
3 (1) Direct interactions with individuals for the purpose of understanding their
4 thoughts, emotions or experiences;
5 (2) Providing guidance, therapeutic strategies or interventions designed to achieve
6 mental health outcomes;

8 psychological or emotional distress;
9 (4) Collaborating with individuals to develop or modify therapeutic goals or
10 treatment plans; and
11 (5) Offering behavioral feedback intended to promote psychological growth or
12 address mental health conditions.
13 D. "Therapy or psychotherapy services" means services provided to diagnose, treat or
14 address an individual's mental health or behavioral health through therapeutic
15 communication.
16 2. Offering therapy or psychotherapy services through use of Internet-based
17 artificial intelligence or otherwise prohibited unless person licensed. A person may not
18 provide, advertise or otherwise offer therapy or psychotherapy services, including through
19 the use of Internet-based artificial intelligence, to the public unless the therapy or
20 psychotherapy services are provided by a licensed professional.
21 3. Violation. A violation of subsection 2 is a violation of the Maine Unfair Trade
22 Practices Act.
23 4. Exceptions. This section does not apply to an artificial intelligence-based
24 intervention that is used solely within a research project approved by an institutional review
25 board, as defined in Title 22, section 1711-C, subsection 6, paragraph G, and conducted in
26 compliance with all applicable federal protections for human subjects in research.
27 Sec. 2. 32 MRSA §2113 is enacted to read:
28 §2113. Use of artificial intelligence to assist in providing administrative support and
29 supplementary support in therapy or psychotherapy services
30 1. Definitions. As used in this section, unless the context otherwise indicates, the
31 following terms have the following meanings.
32 A. "Administrative support" means tasks performed to assist a licensed professional
33 in the delivery of therapy or psychotherapy services that do not involve therapeutic
34 communication. "Administrative support" includes, but is not limited to:
35 (1) Managing appointment scheduling and reminders;
36 (2) Processing billing and insurance claims; and
37 (3) Drafting general communications related to the logistics of delivering therapy
38 or psychotherapy services that do not include therapeutic communication.
39 B. "Artificial intelligence" means a machine-based system that, for explicit or implicit
40 objectives, infers, from the input it receives, how to generate outputs, such as

3 C. "Consent" means a clear, explicit and affirmative act by an individual that:
4 unambiguously communicates the individual's express, informed, voluntary, specific
5 and unambiguous written agreement, including a written agreement provided by
6 electronic means or provided by initialing a specific section of an individual's general
7 consent to treatment agreement; and is revocable by the individual.
8 "Consent" does not include an agreement that is obtained by:
9 (1) The acceptance of a general or broad terms-of-use agreement or a similar
10 document that contains descriptions of artificial intelligence along with unrelated
11 information;
12 (2) An individual's hovering over, muting, pausing or closing a given piece of
13 electronic content; or
14 (3) An agreement obtained through the use of deceptive actions.
15 D. "Supplementary support" means tasks performed to assist a licensed professional
16 in the delivery of therapy or psychotherapy services that do not involve therapeutic
17 communication and that are not administrative support. "Supplementary support"
18 includes, but is not limited to:
19 (1) Preparing and maintaining client records, including therapy notes;
20 (2) Analyzing anonymized data to track client progress or identify trends, subject
21 to review by a licensed professional; and
22 (3) Identifying and organizing external resources or referrals for client use.
23 E. "Therapeutic communication" means any verbal, nonverbal or written interaction,
24 conducted in a clinical or professional setting, that is intended to diagnose, treat or
25 address an individual's mental, emotional or behavioral health concerns. "Therapeutic
26 communication" includes, but is not limited to:
27 (1) Direct interactions with individuals for the purpose of understanding their
28 thoughts, emotions or experiences;
29 (2) Providing guidance, therapeutic strategies or interventions designed to achieve
30 mental health outcomes;
31 (3) Offering emotional support, reassurance or empathy in response to
32 psychological or emotional distress;
33 (4) Collaborating with individuals to develop or modify therapeutic goals or
34 treatment plans; and
35 (5) Offering behavioral feedback intended to promote psychological growth or
36 address mental health conditions.
37 F. "Therapy or psychotherapy services" means services provided to diagnose, treat or
38 address an individual's mental health or behavioral health through therapeutic
39 communication.

412 supplementary support in the delivery of therapy or psychotherapy services through the use
423 of artificial intelligence as long as the licensee acts within the scope of practice of the
434 licensee's license, in accordance with any requirements and restrictions imposed by this
445 chapter and in accordance with standards of practice, and only if:
5 A. The licensee maintains full responsibility for all interactions, outputs and data use
6 associated with the use of artificial intelligence; and
7 B. For the use of artificial intelligence to assist in providing supplementary support,
8 the licensee satisfies the requirements of subsection 3.
9 3. Requirements of use; supplementary support. A licensee may use artificial
10 intelligence to assist in providing supplementary support in therapy or psychotherapy
11 services only when the client's therapeutic session is recorded or transcribed, and only if:
12 A. The client or the client's legally authorized representative is informed in writing of
13 the following:
14 (1) That artificial intelligence will be used;
15 (2) The specific purpose of the artificial intelligence tool or system that will be
16 used; and
17 (3) How session data collected by artificial intelligence will be stored, retained,
18 used for training and deleted upon termination of therapy or psychotherapy
19 services; and
20 B. The client or the client's legally authorized representative provides consent to the
21 use of artificial intelligence.
22 4. Prohibition of use. A licensee may use artificial intelligence to provide
23 supplementary support only to the extent the use meets the requirements of subsection 3.
24 A licensee may not allow artificial intelligence to:
25 A. Make independent therapeutic decisions;
26 B. Directly interact with clients in any form of therapeutic communication; or
27 C. Generate therapeutic recommendations or treatment plans without review and
28 approval by the licensee.
29 5. Denial of treatment prohibited; failure to provide consent. A licensee may not
30 deny, or refuse to provide, therapy or psychotherapy services to a client on the sole basis
31 that the client has not provided consent to the use of artificial intelligence to assist in
32 providing supplementary support in therapy or psychotherapy services.
33 6. Confidentiality. When using artificial intelligence pursuant to this section, a
34 licensee shall comply with all state and federal confidentiality and privacy laws and shall
35 ensure that any artificial intelligence technology is compliant with all state and federal
36 confidentiality and privacy laws.
37 7. Professional responsibility. All laws and rules governing professional
38 responsibility, unprofessional conduct and generally accepted standards of practice that
39 apply to a licensee also apply to that licensee when using artificial intelligence pursuant to
40 this section.

12 SUMMARY
1133 This amendment replaces the bill. The amendment prohibits any person from
1144 providing, advertising or otherwise offering therapy or psychotherapy services, including
1155 through the use of Internet-based artificial intelligence, to the public unless the therapy or
1166 psychotherapy services are provided by a licensed professional. The amendment provides
1177 an exception for an artificial intelligence-based intervention that is used solely within a
1188 research project conducted in compliance with all applicable federal protections for human
1199 subjects in research. A violation of the prohibition is a violation of the Maine Unfair Trade
2200 Practices Act.
21 The amendment authorizes certain licensed professionals who are able to provide
22 therapy or psychotherapy services within the scope of their license to use artificial
23 intelligence to assist in providing administrative support or supplementary support in
24 therapy or psychotherapy services. The amendment provides requirements for the use of
25 artificial intelligence as well as prohibitions on its use. The amendment establishes that
26 any violation by a licensed professional is subject to disciplinary action by the relevant
27 licensing board and clarifies that the statutory language may not be construed to prohibit a
28 client or a client's authorized representative from maintaining an action for harm caused to
29 a client that is attributable to the use of artificial intelligence to assist in providing
30 administrative or supplementary support as part of therapy or psychotherapy services;
31 maintaining an action alleging professional negligence by a licensee; or seeking any other
32 remedies available under other provisions of law. The amendment also requires that each
33 respective licensing board adopt rules to implement these requirements and designates
34 those rules as major substantive subject to legislative review and approval.
