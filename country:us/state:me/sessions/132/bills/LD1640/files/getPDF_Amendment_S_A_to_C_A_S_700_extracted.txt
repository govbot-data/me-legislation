66 This amendment amends Committee Amendment "A" by changing the reporting date
77 required of the study commission established in the bill from December 3, 2025 to
88 November 4, 2026 and changing a reference to the Second Regular Session of the 132nd
99 Legislature to the 133rd Legislature. It also amends the appropriations and allocations
1100 section to provide funds in fiscal year 2026-27 instead of fiscal year 2025-26. The
1111 amendment also removes the emergency preamble and emergency clause.
