24 SUMMARY
2255 This amendment, which is a minority report of the committee, replaces the bill, which
2266 is a concept draft, and changes the title. Upon the adjournment sine die of the First Special
2277 Session of the 132nd Legislature on June 25, 2025, the Governor remained in possession
2288 of certain legislation finally enacted or passed by the Legislature and presented to the
2299 Governor. Pursuant to the Constitution of Maine, the final disposition of such legislation
3300 not signed by the Governor was determined in January 2026 during the Second Regular
3311 Session of the 132nd Legislature. This amendment updates, as necessary, certain dates for
3322 implementation or reporting contained in such legislation held by the Governor that became
3333 law in January 2026, as described below.
