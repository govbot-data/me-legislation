COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 879, L.D. 2169
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment requires the chief executive officer of the Maine Public Employees
33 Retirement System to waive the reduction in the amount that a person’s disability
44 retirement benefits must be reduced when the person receives compensation in any year
55 from engaging in any gainful activity or from employment if the person demonstrates that
66 the excess compensation has ceased and that continuing the reduction would cause a
77 hardship.
8 The amendment also provides that any person required to reimburse the Maine Public
9 Employees Retirement System for any excess payments due to the receipt of excess
10 compensation from employment under current law as of the effective date of this legislation
11 does not have to pay that reimbursement.
