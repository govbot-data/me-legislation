15 'Sec. 1. 36 MRSA §5219-CCC is enacted to read:
16 §5219-CCC. Dental care access credit
17 1. Definitions. As used in this section, unless the context otherwise indicates, the
18 following terms have the following meanings.
19 A. "Eligible dentist" means a person licensed as a dentist under Title 32, chapter 143
20 who, on or after January 1, 2027:
21 (1) First begins practicing dentistry in the State by joining an existing dental
22 practice in an underserved area or establishing a new dental practice or purchasing
23 an existing dental practice in an underserved area;
24 (2) Agrees to practice full-time for at least 5 years in an underserved area;
25 (3) Is an enrolled MaineCare provider serving a patient panel consisting of no less
26 than 15% MaineCare members; and
27 (4) Is certified under subsection 3 to be eligible by the oral health program.
28 B. "Oral health program" means the program within the Department of Health and
29 Human Services with responsibility for oral health promotion and dental disease
30 prevention activities.
31 C. "Underserved area" means an area in the State that is a dental health professional
32 shortage area as defined by the federal Department of Health and Human Services,
33 Health Resources and Services Administration.

1 2. Credit. An eligible dentist determined to be eligible on or after January 1, 2027 but
2 before January 1, 2033 is allowed a credit for each tax year, not to exceed $6,000 in the
3 first year, $9,000 in the 2nd year, $12,000 in the 3rd year, $15,000 in the 4th year and
4 $18,000 in the 5th year, against the taxes due under this Part. The credit may be claimed
5 in the first year that the eligible dentist meets the conditions of eligibility for at least 6
6 months and each of the 4 subsequent years. The credit is not refundable.
7 3. Eligibility limitation; certification. The oral health program shall certify up to 5
8 eligible dentists in each year from 2027 to 2032. Additional dentists may not be certified
9 after 2032. The oral health program shall monitor certified dentists to ensure that they
10 continue to be eligible for the credit under this section and shall decertify any dentist who
11 ceases to meet the conditions of eligibility. The oral health program shall notify the bureau
12 whenever a dentist is certified or decertified. A decertified dentist ceases to be eligible for
13 the credit under this section beginning with the tax year during which the dentist is
14 decertified.
15 4. Annual report. By March 1, 2028 and annually thereafter, the oral health program
16 shall submit to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over
17 taxation matters a report that analyzes the effectiveness of the credit provided by this
18 section in attracting dentists to underserved areas and recommending whether the credit
19 should be retained, repealed or amended. The committee may submit legislation to any
20 regular or special session of the Legislature related to the report.
21 5. Rules. The Department of Health and Human Services may adopt rules to
22 implement this section. Rules adopted pursuant to this subsection are routine technical
23 rules as defined in Title 5, chapter 375, subchapter 2‑A.
24 6. Repeal. This section is repealed December 31, 2037.'
25 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
26 number to read consecutively.
27 SUMMARY
2288 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. The amendment reestablishes
2299 the dental care access credit beginning January 1, 2027. The credit allows 5 eligible
3300 dentists per year to receive a credit against income tax for 5 years in the amount of $6,000
3311 in the first year, $9,000 in the 2nd year, $12,000 in the 3rd year, $15,000 in the 4th year
3322 and $18,000 in the 5th year. The Department of Health and Human Services must certify
3333 that the eligible dentists meet the conditions of eligibility. The amendment requires annual
3344 reporting to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over
3355 taxation matters. It repeals the laws governing the dental care access credit on December
3366 31, 2037.
