9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 166, L.D. 374, “An Act to Provide
10 Comparable Compensation and Benefits for Deputy and Assistant District Attorneys,
11 Assistant Attorneys General and Public Defenders”
12 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
13 'Resolve, to Establish the Commission to Make Recommendations for Equity in the
14 Compensation of Prosecutors and Public Defenders in the State'
15 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
16 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
17 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
18 Whereas, prosecutors and public defenders fill vital roles in the State's criminal
19 justice system, ensuring not only public safety within our communities but also the
20 preservation of the fundamental rights of the accused; and
21 Whereas, in recent years, the State has invested in developing a public defender
22 system to address the constitutional crisis created by the limited availability of counsel to
23 represent indigent defendants in criminal proceedings in the State; and
24 Whereas, it is essential to ensure equity in the compensation and benefits afforded to
25 assistant district attorneys, assistant attorneys general and assistant public defenders to
26 address and prevent recruitment and retention difficulties that exacerbate delays in the
27 criminal justice system; and
28 Whereas, this legislation establishes a commission tasked with studying the
29 compensation packages and benefits of prosecutors and public defenders and developing
30 recommendations to ensure equity in compensation and benefits; and
31 Whereas, the study must be initiated before the 90-day period expires so that
32 sufficient time is available for a thorough study of these issues and the preparation of a
33 report for submission to the next Legislature; and

5 Sec. 1. Commission established. Resolved: That the Commission to Make
6 Recommendations for Equity in the Compensation of Prosecutors and Public Defenders in
7 the State, referred to in this resolve as "the commission," is established.
8 Sec. 2. Commission membership. Resolved: That, notwithstanding Joint Rule
9 353, the commission consists of 11 members appointed or designated as follows:
10 1. Two members of the Senate appointed by the President of the Senate, including one
11 member of the party holding the largest number of seats in the Legislature and one member
12 of the party holding the 2nd largest number of seats in the Legislature;
13 2. Two members of the House of Representatives appointed by the Speaker of the
14 House of Representatives, including one member of the party holding the largest number
15 of seats in the Legislature and one member of the party holding the 2nd largest number of
16 seats in the Legislature;
17 3. One member who is a representative of the Maine Prosecutors' Association,
18 appointed by the President of the Senate;
19 4. One member who is employed by a law school in the State and who has experience
20 in career placement for law students, appointed by the President of the Senate;
21 5. One member who is a licensed attorney whose legal practice includes work as
22 assigned counsel for the Maine Commission on Public Defense Services, appointed by the
23 Speaker of the House of Representatives;
24 6. Two members from the Office of the Attorney General, at least one of whom has
25 expertise in the compensation and benefits provided to assistant attorneys general and
26 deputy attorneys general, designated by the Attorney General; and
27 7. Two members who are employees of or who provide services to the Maine
28 Commission on Public Defense Services, at least one of whom has expertise in the
29 compensation and benefits provided to assistant district attorneys, deputy district attorneys
30 and district public defenders, designated by the executive director of the Maine
31 Commission on Public Defense Services.
32 Sec. 3. Chairs. Resolved: That the first-named Senate member is the Senate chair
33 and the first-named House of Representatives member is the House chair of the
34 commission. The Speaker of the House and the President of the Senate shall confer to
35 ensure that one chair is a member of the party holding the largest number of seats in the
36 Legislature and the other chair is a member of the party holding the 2nd largest number of
37 seats in the Legislature.
38 Sec. 4. Appointments; convening of commission. Resolved: That all
39 appointments must be made no later than 30 days following the effective date of this
40 resolve. The appointing authorities shall notify the Executive Director of the Legislative
41 Council once all appointments have been completed. After appointment of all members,
42 the chairs shall call and convene the first meeting of the commission. If 30 days or more
43 after the effective date of this resolve a majority of but not all appointments have been

3 Sec. 5. Duties. Resolved: That the commission shall gather and examine
4 information regarding the compensation packages and benefits available to both new and
5 experienced attorneys who serve as prosecutors in the Office of the Attorney General,
6 prosecutors in district attorneys' offices across the State and public defenders employed by
7 the Maine Commission on Public Defense Services. The commission shall further examine
8 other benefits available to prosecutors and public defenders that may assist with
9 recruitment and retention of qualified individuals for these positions, including eligibility
10 for loan forgiveness, collective bargaining and other employment benefits.
11 Sec. 6. Staff assistance. Resolved: That the Legislative Council shall provide
12 necessary staffing services to the commission, except that Legislative Council staff support
13 is not authorized when the Legislature is in regular or special session.
14 Sec. 7. Report. Resolved: That, no later than November 4, 2026, the commission
15 shall submit a report to the Joint Standing Committee on Judiciary that includes the
16 commission's findings and recommendations, including any suggested legislation, for
17 ensuring equity in the compensation and benefits of prosecutors and public defenders in
18 the State. The joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over judiciary
19 matters is authorized to report out legislation related to the report to the 133rd Legislature
20 in 2027.
