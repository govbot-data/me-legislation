COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 505, L.D. 1216
1 The department shall establish crisis intervention support services in all 16 counties.
2 These services must be community-based programs that provide counseling, consultation,
3 evaluation, treatment, referral, education and training services, delivered by a crisis
4 intervention team, including mobile crisis intervention teams. The crisis intervention
5 support services must include:
6 1. Emergency room services. Crisis intervention and psychiatric emergency services
7 based in a hospital emergency room;
8 2. Outreach services. Outreach services and crisis intervention beyond the hospital
9 setting; and
10 3. Telephone hotline services. A community-based telephone crisis intervention
11 hotline, which must coordinate with the 9-8-8 suicide and crisis lifeline, offering
12 counseling, consultation, evaluation, treatment and referral services 24 hours a day, 7 days
13 a week.'
14 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
15 number to read consecutively.
16 SUMMARY
1177 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft. The amendment repeals a
1188 section of law that requires crisis intervention services in 4 counties and replaces it with a
1199 requirement for crisis intervention support services to be made available by the Department
2200 of Health and Human Services in all 16 counties in the State. It requires that a community-
2211 based telephone crisis intervention hotline be provided by crisis intervention support
2222 services. The hotline must coordinate with the national 9-8-8 suicide and crisis lifeline.
