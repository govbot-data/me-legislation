2 Sec. D-1. 23 MRSA §1652, 2nd ¶, as amended by PL 2011, c. 392, Pt. L, §2, is
3 further amended to read:
4 Any balance of any allocation or subdivision of an allocation from the Highway Fund
5 made by the Legislature for any department or agency, which that at any time may is not
6 be required for the purposes named in that allocation or subdivision, may be transferred at
7 any time prior to the closing of the books, to any other allocation or subdivision of an
8 allocation from the Highway Fund made by the Legislature for the same fiscal year subject
9 to review by the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over
10 transportation matters. In addition to transfers of Highway Fund allocations and
11 subdivisions of allocations from the Highway Fund, the joint standing committee of the
12 Legislature having jurisdiction over transportation matters shall review all other allocations
13 or subdivisions of allocations to the Department of Transportation. Financial orders
14 describing these transfers must be submitted by the Department of Administrative and
15 Financial Services, Bureau of the Budget to the Office of Fiscal and Program Review 30
16 days before the transfer is to be implemented. In the case of extraordinary emergency
17 transfers, the 30-day prior submission requirement may be waived by vote of the joint
18 standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over transportation matters.

5 SUMMARY
66 PART A
7 This Part allocates money not otherwise allocated in order to provide for the necessary
8 expenditures of State Government and other purposes for the fiscal years ending June 30,
9 2026 and June 30, 2027.
10 PART B
11 This Part allocates money to provide funding for approved reclassifications and range
12 changes.
13 PART C
14 This Part directs the State Controller to carry forward up to $2,500,000 in unexpended
15 balances in the Personal Services and All Other line categories in the Department of
16 Secretary of State, Administration - Motor Vehicles program, after all financial
17 commitments for obligations and budgetary adjustments have been made, at the end of
18 fiscal year 2025-26 to the next fiscal year to the All Other line category in the Department
19 of Secretary of State, Administration - Motor Vehicles program to be used to continue the
20 Bureau of Motor Vehicles modernization project.
21 PART D
22 This Part establishes an exception to the requirement that a financial order describing
23 the transfer of unused funds within the Highway Fund and any other allocations to the
24 Department of Transportation be submitted to the Office of Fiscal and Program Review 30
25 days before the transfer is to be implemented. This Part provides that in the case of
26 extraordinary emergency transfers, the 30-day waiting period may be waived by a vote of
27 the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over transportation
28 matters.
29 PART E
30 This Part authorizes the Department of Public Safety to transfer, for fiscal year 2026-
31 27, up to $339,948 of available balances of Personal Services allocations, after all salary,
32 benefit and other obligations are met, to the All Other line category of the State Police
33 program for the purposes of funding consoles at 3 regional emergency communication
34 centers and a contract for firearms examiner services. This Part also authorizes the
35 department to transfer, for fiscal year 2026-27, up to $16,450 of available balances of
36 Personal Services allocations, after all salary, benefit and other obligations are met, to the
37 Capital Expenditures line category of the State Police program for the purposes of funding
38 a heat pump and generator for the Pittsfield barracks and updating the security system at
39 the Bangor barracks.
