9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1402, L.D. 2087, “An Act to Amend the
10 Laws Governing the Licensure of American Sign Language Interpreters”
11 Amend the bill by inserting after the title and before the enacting clause the following:
12 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
13 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
14 Whereas, this legislation implements certain recommendations from the
15 Transforming Interpreting Maine needs assessment report issued by the Department of
16 Health and Human Services following the October 2023 Lewiston shooting; and
17 Whereas, this legislation would provide more opportunities for persons in need of
18 American Sign Language interpreting to access these services, especially during an
19 emergency; and
20 Whereas, immediate enactment of this legislation is necessary to expand access to
21 American Sign Language interpreting in this State; and
22 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
23 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
24 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
25 therefore,'
26 Amend the bill in section 3 in subsection 3 in the 4th line (page 1, line 17 in L.D.) by
27 inserting after the following: "situation" the following: 'is occurring, or did occur, that'
28 Amend the bill by adding before the summary the following:
29 'Emergency clause. In view of the emergency cited in the preamble, this legislation
30 takes effect when approved.'
31 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
32 number to read consecutively.
