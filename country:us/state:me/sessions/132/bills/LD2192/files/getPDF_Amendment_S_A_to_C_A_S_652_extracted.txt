1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment amends Committee Amendment "A" as follows.
3 1. It expands the definition of "covered investigation" of a school employee to include
4 misconduct that is harassment as described in the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 5, chapter
5 337-A; sexual assault as described in Title 17-A, chapter 11; stalking as described in Title
6 17-A, section 210-A; sexual exploitation of a minor as described in Title 17-A, chapter 12;
7 or similar behavior that endangers the health, safety or welfare of a student or another
8 school employee. This definition captures misconduct of all school employees and captures
9 misconduct of a school employee against another school employee, as well as misconduct
10 against students.
11 2. Upon receipt of a complaint of alleged misconduct, it requires a superintendent of
12 a school entity to conduct a covered investigation, instead of a preliminary investigation,
13 as required under Committee Amendment "A." The amendment retains the provisions of
14 Committee Amendment "A" that provide that school entities must put anyone who is the
15 subject of a covered investigation on paid leave, must complete covered investigations once
16 they've begun, and superintendents must notify the Department of Education immediately
17 of the outcome of a covered investigation.
18 3. Upon receipt of notification from a school entity of the initiation of a covered
19 investigation of a school employee, it requires the Department of Education to enter a
20 notation on a school employee's educator credentialing records or other school employee
21 records maintained by the department for that school employee indicating that a covered
22 investigation is pending. The notation must be visible to superintendents and other school
23 administrators authorized to review educator credentialing records and school employee
24 records maintained by the department. Upon receipt from a school entity of the outcome of
25 a covered investigation, the department shall update the notation on the school employee's
26 file. If the school employee is found to have committed the misconduct, the department
27 must update the notation to reflect that finding; if the school employee is found to have not
28 committed the misconduct, the department must remove the notation from the file.
29 4. It requires that prior to offering employment to an applicant, a school entity must
30 review the applicant's educator credentialing records or other school employee records
31 maintained by the department to determine whether the applicant holds a valid and active
32 certification appropriate for the position or is otherwise eligible for employment and
33 whether any notation regarding a covered investigation has been entered on the applicant's
34 records, and, if so, the current status of that notation.
35 5. It requires that, notwithstanding any confidentiality clause or any agreement
36 between the school entity and a school employee, if, by a preponderance of the evidence,
37 a school entity determines through its covered investigation that the school employee
38 committed misconduct, the information pertaining to that covered investigation and
