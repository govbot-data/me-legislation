23 SUMMARY
2244 This amendment, which is the minority report of the committee, changes the title and
2255 clarifies the list of entities to which sealed criminal history record information may be
2266 disseminated; the basis for such dissemination; and the permissible use of the disseminated
2277 sealed criminal history record information by the Department of Professional and Financial
2288 Regulation, its agencies and boards and certain other entities. The amendment also clarifies
2299 a license applicant's obligation to disclose sealed criminal history record information to
3300 these entities. The amendment also adds an appropriations and allocations section.
