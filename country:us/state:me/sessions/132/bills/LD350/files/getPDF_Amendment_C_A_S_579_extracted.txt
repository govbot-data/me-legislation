9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 134, L.D. 350, “An Act Regarding the
10 Operations of the Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife”
11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'Resolve, Directing the Landowners and Land Users Relations Advisory Board to
13 Consider Issues Related to All-terrain Vehicles in the State'
14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
15 'Sec. 1. Study ATV issues. Resolved: That the Landowners and Land Users
16 Relations Advisory Board, established in the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 12, section
17 10157 and referred to in this resolve as "the advisory board," shall consider issues related
18 to weights, sizes and classifications of all-terrain vehicles, referred to in this resolve as
19 "ATVs," in the State. When considering issues related to ATVs, the advisory board shall
20 consult with the following stakeholders:
21 1. An individual who registered an oversized ATV and had that registration rescinded
22 by the State;
23 2. An ATV manufacturer;
24 3. An ATV dealer; and
25 4. Any other stakeholders as determined by the advisory board.
26 Sec. 2. Report. Resolved: That the advisory board shall report to the joint standing
27 committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over inland fisheries and wildlife matters
28 with findings and recommendations no later than February 1, 2027. The report must include
29 a summary of the meetings of the advisory board pertaining to the issues described in
30 section 1 and a list of the additional stakeholders included in those meetings as described
31 by section 1. The committee may report out a bill related to the report to the 133rd
32 Legislature in 2027.'
33 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
34 number to read consecutively.
