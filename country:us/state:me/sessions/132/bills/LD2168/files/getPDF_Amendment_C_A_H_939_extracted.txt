27 SUMMARY
2288 This amendment replaces the bill and provides that a person who committed a crime
2299 that caused or contributed to the injury or death for which compensation is sought may
3300 receive funds from the Victims' Compensation Fund if that person was a victim of
3311 aggravated sex trafficking, sex trafficking, sexual exploitation of a minor, commercial
3322 sexual exploitation of a minor or commercial sexual exploitation of a person with a mental
3333 disability and the crime committed by that person was the direct result of sex trafficking or
3344 sexual exploitation. The amendment increases the mandatory fee for a conviction of
3355 soliciting a child for commercial sexual exploitation and for a conviction of commercial
3366 sexual exploitation of a minor or person with a mental disability.
