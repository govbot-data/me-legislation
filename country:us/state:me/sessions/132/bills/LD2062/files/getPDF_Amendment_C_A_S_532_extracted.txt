15 §4209-B. Public transit bus operators; vehicle security barriers
16 1. Short title. This section may be known and cited as "the Public Transit Bus
17 Operator Safety Act."
18 2. Definitions. As used in this section, unless the context otherwise indicates, the
19 following terms have the following meanings.
20 A. "Fund" means the Public Transit Bus Operator Safety Fund established in
21 subsection 6.
22 B. "Large heavy-duty bus" means a bus, as defined in Title 29-A, section 101,
23 subsection 11, that has a gross vehicle weight of 26,001 pounds or greater and has a
24 length of 30 feet or greater.
25 C. "Public transit bus operator" means an individual employed by an urban fixed-route
26 transit provider to operate a large heavy-duty bus.
27 D. "Urban fixed-route transit provider" means a transit district or a regional
28 transportation corporation, as defined in Title 30-A, section 3501, that provides public
29 transportation within one or more municipalities, at least one of which has a population
30 greater than 20,000, on prescribed routes at prescribed times.
31 E. "Vehicle security barrier" means a transparent, protective partition made of hard,
32 durable, glare-minimizing materials that:

9 3. Urban fixed-route transit providers; installation of vehicle security barriers.
10 Beginning August 1, 2029, an urban fixed-route transit provider shall ensure that any large
11 heavy-duty buses that are in service are equipped with a vehicle security barrier.
12 4. Procurements requirements; vehicle security barriers. When procuring vehicle
13 security barriers and any other materials funded by the fund, an urban fixed-route transit
14 provider shall solicit and incorporate input from a labor organization representing the urban
15 fixed-route transit provider's public transit bus operators. Such solicitation must occur via
16 a joint labor-management safety committee, if the urban fixed-route transit provider is party
17 to such a committee.
18 5. Prohibition; large heavy-duty buses in service without vehicle security barrier.
19 On or after August 1, 2029, an urban fixed-route transit provider may not operate a large
20 heavy-duty bus that is not equipped with a vehicle security barrier.
21 6. Public Transit Bus Operator Safety Fund. The Public Transit Bus Operator
22 Safety Fund is established as an Other Special Revenue Funds account within the
23 department to provide grants to urban fixed-route transit providers for the purchase and
24 installation of vehicle security barriers that comply with the requirements of this section.
25 The fund consists of money received as transfers, appropriations, allocations and
26 contributions from public and private sources. Any funds remaining in the fund after
27 August 1, 2029 must lapse or be transferred to the General Fund. The fund may not be used
28 for the maintenance or repair of vehicle security barriers or to equip a large heavy-duty bus
29 that is not in service.
30 Sec. 2. Reporting requirements. By August 1st in 2027, 2028 and 2029, an urban
31 fixed-route transit provider shall submit an annual report to the Department of
32 Transportation regarding the progress of the urban fixed-route transit provider in meeting
33 the requirements of the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 23, section 4209-B. The report must
34 set forth the steps that the urban fixed-route transit provider has taken to comply with the
35 requirements to install vehicle security barriers, the tasks remaining for the urban fixed-
36 route transit provider to complete in order to achieve full compliance and an estimated
37 completion date for each of these tasks. The department shall make a report received under
38 this section available to the public within 30 days of receipt of the report.

18 SUMMARY
1199 This amendment replaces the bill. The amendment does the following.
20 1. It requires, by August 1, 2029, an urban fixed-route transit provider that operates
21 within a municipality with a population greater than 20,000 to install vehicle security
22 barriers in any large heavy-duty buses that are in service.
23 2. It maintains the bill's establishment of the Public Transit Bus Operator Safety Fund
24 while clarifying that the fund may be used only for the purchase and installation of vehicle
25 security barriers for large heavy-duty buses that are in service. Any maintenance of a
26 vehicle security barrier is the responsibility of the urban fixed-route transit provider.
27 3. It requires any remaining funds in the fund to lapse or be transferred to the General
28 Fund after August 1, 2029.
29 4. It reduces the appropriations and allocations from $2,000,000 to $1,000,000.
