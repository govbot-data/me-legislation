1 MAINE LIFE SCIENCES INNOVATION CENTER
2 §15331. Maine Life Sciences Innovation Center
3 1. Establishment; purpose. The Maine Life Sciences Innovation Center, referred to
4 in this subchapter as "the center," is established within the institute to support life sciences
5 innovation, research, entrepreneurship and workforce development in the State. The center
6 is established to serve the following purposes:
7 A. To leverage and expand the State's life sciences sector by supporting collaborative
8 research, workforce training and entrepreneurial ventures and seeking capital
9 investment;
10 B. To advance life sciences innovation and sector research across disciplines, including
11 human and animal health, marine sciences, agriculture, forestry and data science;
12 C. To coordinate with public and private partners, educational and research institutions,
13 nonprofit organizations and business stakeholders to strengthen the State's innovation
14 efforts;
15 D. To investigate opportunities for grants, loans and other resources to support life
16 sciences entities;
17 E. To develop strategies to attract federal, philanthropic and private investment;
18 F. To advise the Maine Technology Institute Director under section 15310 on matters
19 related to life sciences innovation; and
20 G. To report regularly to the Legislature regarding the center's operations, activities
21 and recommendations.
22 2. Membership. The center is composed of 10 members. The members include:
23 A. The Commissioner of Economic and Community Development, or the
24 commissioner's designee, who serves as an ex officio, nonvoting member; and
25 B. Nine voting members appointed by the Governor, upon the recommendation of the
26 Finance Authority of Maine and the institute, with expertise spanning the life sciences
27 sector and its allied disciplines, including representatives of industry, life sciences
28 entrepreneurs, research institutions, academia, hospitals, nonprofit organizations,
29 investors and counties, including rural counties. Each member under this paragraph is
30 entitled to one vote.
31 The Governor shall designate a chair from among the center's members to preside at, set
32 the agenda for and schedule center meetings.
33 Center members shall disclose any direct or indirect pecuniary interest in any matter before
34 the center and recuse themselves from any participation in the consideration of such a
35 matter.
36 3. Operations. The following provisions govern the operations of the center.
37 A. The center shall meet as often as it considers necessary for the center to perform its
38 work.
39 B. To the extent practicable, the center shall conduct its work in a manner that is open
40 and accessible to the public.

1 C. The center may conduct its work through subcommittees.
2 4. Staff and administrative support. The institute shall provide staff as may be
3 necessary to fulfill the center's mission and may seek assistance from other state agencies
4 and private entities to accomplish the goals and work of the center.
5 5. Annual report. The center shall submit annually to the Governor, the Executive
6 Director of the Legislative Council and the joint standing committee of the Legislature
7 having jurisdiction over economic development matters, no later than 120 days after the
8 close of the center's fiscal year, a complete report on the activities of the center. The report
9 must be made available to the public and must include the following for that fiscal year:
10 A. A description of the center's operations;
11 B. An accounting of the center's receipts and expenditures, assets and liabilities at the
12 end of its fiscal year;
13 C. A listing of all investments, grants, loans and incentives provided by the center
14 during the fiscal year;
15 D. A statement of the center's proposed and projected activities for the ensuing fiscal
16 year; and
17 E. Recommendations regarding further actions that may be suitable for achieving the
18 purposes of this subchapter.
19 After reviewing the report under this subsection, the joint standing committee may report
20 out legislation relating to the report.
21 Sec. 5. Maine Life Sciences Innovation Center; interim report. The Maine
22 Life Sciences Innovation Center established pursuant to the Maine Revised Statutes, Title
23 5, section 15331 shall submit an interim report to the joint standing committee of the
24 Legislature having jurisdiction over economic development matters no later than 180 days
25 after all members of the center have been appointed pursuant to Title 5, section 15331,
26 subsection 2. The interim report must include, but is not limited to, the following:
27 1. A description of the center's operations;
28 2. Proposed and projected activities for the ensuing fiscal year; and
29 3. Recommendations for future actions necessary to achieve the center's goals,
30 including a recommendation regarding whether the center should be changed to a quasi-
31 independent state entity.
32 After reviewing the report under this section, the joint standing committee may report
33 out legislation relating to the report.
34 Emergency clause. In view of the emergency cited in the preamble, this legislation
35 takes effect when approved.'
