11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'An Act to Protect the Right to Harvest Fish and Wildlife'
13 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
14 following:
15 'Sec. 1. 12 MRSA §10050 is enacted to read:
16 §10050. Right to harvest fish and wildlife; limitations
17 1. Right to harvest fish and wildlife. Pursuant to the right to food as declared in the
18 Constitution of Maine, Article I, Section 25, the people of this State have the right to harvest
19 fish and wildlife by hunting, fishing or trapping for their own nourishment, sustenance,
20 bodily health or well-being.
21 2. Limitation. The exercise of the right to harvest fish and wildlife is expressly limited
22 by all applicable laws, rules and regulations authorized pursuant to this Title, which the
23 department shall enforce.'
24 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
25 number to read consecutively.
26 SUMMARY
