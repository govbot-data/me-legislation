15 Sec. IIII-1. Department of Health and Human Services to reimburse
16 hospitals after receipt of cost reports. The Department of Health and Human Services
17 shall amend its rules in Chapter 101: MaineCare Benefits Manual, Chapter III, Section 45,
18 Hospital Services, to require the department to reimburse 75% of the as-filed settlement

20 remaining 25% is paid incrementally after the audits are finished.
