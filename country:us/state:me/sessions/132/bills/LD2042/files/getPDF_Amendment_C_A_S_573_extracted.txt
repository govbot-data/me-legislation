9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 831, L.D. 2042, “An Act to Eliminate
10 the Requirement for Municipalities to Provide Public Notice in a Newspaper”
11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'An Act to Clarify the Requirement for Municipalities to Provide Public Notice in a
13 Newspaper'
14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
15 following:
16 'Sec. 1. 30-A MRSA §2007 is enacted to read:
17 §2007. Public notice
18 Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, a municipality required by this
19 Title to place a notice in a newspaper may meet this requirement by placing the notice on
20 the newspaper's publicly accessible website or the statewide repository for legal notices as
21 described in Title 1, section 603, subsection 2, as long as either action is followed by
22 placement of the notice in the next available print edition of the newspaper. The notice on
23 the newspaper's publicly accessible website must contain all information required to be
24 published in a newspaper.'
25 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
26 number to read consecutively.
