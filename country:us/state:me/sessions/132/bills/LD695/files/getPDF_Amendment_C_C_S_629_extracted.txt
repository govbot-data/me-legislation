12 'Resolve, to Establish a Stakeholder Group to Develop Recommendations for

14 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
15 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
16 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
17 Whereas, members of the Legislature and their families are facing increasing threats
18 to their physical safety and security; and
19 Whereas, threats to the safety and security of public servants, including members of
20 the Legislature and their families, are a growing concern and must be addressed as quickly
21 as possible; and
22 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
23 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
24 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
25 therefore, be it
26 Sec. 1. Commissioner of Public Safety to convene stakeholder group to
27 examine issues related to physical safety and security of Legislators and their
28 families. Resolved: That the Commissioner of Public Safety, referred to in this resolve
29 as "the commissioner," shall convene a stakeholder group to examine issues related to the
30 physical safety and security of members of the Legislature and their families and develop
31 recommendations for improving the physical safety and security of members of the
32 Legislature and their families.
33 1. Membership. The stakeholder group must include, at a minimum, the following

18 2. Duties and recommendations. The stakeholder group shall examine issues related
19 to increasing threats to the physical safety and security of members of the Legislature and
20 their families. The stakeholder group shall:
21 A. Collect information on the number of threats received by members of the
22 Legislature and their families in the 132nd Legislature;
23 B. Consult with organizations that have professional experience in protecting the
24 safety and security of public officials and their families, such as the United States
25 Department of Homeland Security, United States Secret Service, to understand current
26 best practices nationally for ensuring the safety and security of public officials and their
27 families;
28 C. Consult with organizations with expertise in state legislatures, including but not
29 limited to the National Conference of State Legislatures;
30 D. Develop best practices specific to the physical safety and security of members of
31 the Legislature and their families;
32 E. Identify or create educational materials that may be distributed to members of the
33 Legislature and their families that identify resources available to both proactively and
34 reactively address threats to the physical safety and security of members of the
35 Legislature and their families; and
36 F. Identify opportunities for education and training for members of the Legislature on
37 best practices to increase their safety and security and the safety and security of their
38 families.
39 The stakeholder group shall examine and make recommendations regarding the
40 information obtained pursuant to this subsection.

1122 Safety to convene a stakeholder group to examine issues related to the physical safety and
1133 security of members of the Legislature and their families and develop recommendations

1155 families. The amendment also adds an emergency preamble and an emergency clause.
