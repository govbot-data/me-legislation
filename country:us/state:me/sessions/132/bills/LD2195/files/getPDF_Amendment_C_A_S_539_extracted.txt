21 Sec. 2. 4 MRSA §1802-A, sub-§3 is enacted to read:
22 3. Compensation of attorneys appointed or assigned by court to provide indigent
23 legal services. The commission is not required to reimburse the expenses of or to
24 compensate an attorney appointed or assigned by a court to provide indigent legal services
25 unless the attorney is an assigned counsel; the commission has determined that the attorney
26 is eligible for appointment or assignment in the type of court proceeding to which the
27 attorney has been appointed or assigned; and the attorney consents in advance to the
28 appointment or assignment.'
29 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
30 number to read consecutively.
