1 §2430-P. Annual inspections; mandatory sample testing
2 The office shall conduct testing of samples collected by the office during the annual
3 inspection required under section 2430-K for registered caregivers, registered dispensaries
4 and manufacturing facilities in accordance with this section. This section may not be
5 construed to limit the authority of the office to take samples of harvested cannabis pursuant
6 to an investigation by the office into the conduct of a registrant or a registrant agent.
7 1. Mandatory testing; costs. The office shall test a sample of harvested cannabis
8 collected under this section for:
9 A. Residual solvents;
10 B. Metals; and
11 C. Pesticides.
12 The costs associated with conducting tests under this subsection must be paid for by the
13 registered caregiver, registered dispensary and manufacturing facility from which the
14 sample was collected.
15 2. Other testing; costs. The office may conduct other tests on a sample of harvested
16 cannabis collected under this section for the purpose of research and data collection. Any
17 test conducted under this subsection must be paid for by the office.
18 3. Notification of results; records. The office shall notify a registered caregiver,
19 registered dispensary and manufacturing facility within one business day of the office
20 receiving the results of any test conducted under this section. The office shall provide a
21 written copy of any test results with the notification. A registered caregiver, registered
22 dispensary and manufacturing facility shall keep a record of all test results under this
23 section for a period of 4 years in the same manner as other records required to be kept under
24 this chapter.
25 4. Results exceed maximum allowable contamination level; additional testing. If
26 a test conducted under this section for residual solvents or pesticides indicates that the
27 sample of harvested cannabis exceeds the maximum level of allowable contamination for
28 residual solvents or pesticides, as determined by the office pursuant to rules adopted
29 pursuant to this section, which are major substantive rules pursuant to Title 5, chapter 375,
30 subchapter 2-A, the office shall:
31 A. Provide notification as required under subsection 3 and, if applicable, provide
32 notice to any registered caregiver, registered dispensary or manufacturing facility from
33 which the harvested cannabis sample originated;
34 B. Collect up to 3 additional samples of harvested cannabis from the registrant whose
35 sample exceeded the maximum level of allowable contamination for residual solvents
36 or pesticides and from any registrant from which the harvested cannabis sample
37 originated from any location where a registered caregiver, registered dispensary or
38 manufacturing facility conducts activity authorized under this chapter;
39 C. Restrict the transfer or sale of the batch of harvested cannabis that exceeded the
40 maximum level of allowable contamination for residual solvents or pesticides under
41 this subsection and may initiate a recall of any harvested cannabis from the batch that
42 may have already been sold or transferred; and

16 SUMMARY
1177 This amendment amends Committee Amendment "B" to require the Department of
1188 Administrative and Financial Services, Office of Cannabis Policy to conduct inspections
1199 of initial applicants to be registered caregivers, registered dispensaries, cannabis testing
2200 facilities and manufacturing facilities in accordance with the laws and rules governing the
2211 medical use of cannabis. It also requires the office to conduct annual inspections of
2222 registered caregivers, registered dispensaries, cannabis testing facilities and manufacturing
2233 facilities. The amendment provides that the office may deny registration to an applicant to
2244 be a registered caregiver, registered dispensary, cannabis testing facility or manufacturing
2255 facility if during an inspection of that applicant the office finds evidence of a violation of
2266 the laws or rules governing the medical use of cannabis.
27 The amendment removes the testing provisions of Committee Amendment "B" and
28 replaces them with the testing provisions of Committee Amendment "C," which require the
29 Office of Cannabis Policy to conduct testing of samples collected by the office during
30 required annual inspections for registered caregivers, registered dispensaries and
31 manufacturing facilities.
32 The amendment removes the provisions regarding the excise tax on cannabis pre-roll.
33 The amendment provides that the term of the initial contract with a vendor for the
34 electronic inventory tracking system may not exceed 3 years. It also provides that the total
35 cost of the initial contract may not exceed $250,000 for 3 years. The amendment provides
36 that the office may not accept a bid, proposal or application or award a contract for the
37 development, implementation, maintenance, administration or operation of the electronic
38 inventory tracking system from or to any person or entity that holds a contract with the
39 State for the provision or operation of a cannabis industry tracking system required under
40 the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 28-B, section 105 or is involved with; is an affiliate,
