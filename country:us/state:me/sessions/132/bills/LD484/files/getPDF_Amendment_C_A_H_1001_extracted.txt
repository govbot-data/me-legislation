24 SUMMARY
2255 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft, and changes the title. It
2266 authorizes certain emergency medical services providers and personnel that are licensed to
2277 or authorized to provide advanced emergency medical treatment or community
2288 paramedicine by the Emergency Medical Services' Board to procure, possess, administer
2299 and furnish controlled substances in accordance with rules adopted by the board. The
3300 amendment defines "community paramedicine service" and "community paramedicine
3311 clinician."
