17 1. Definitions. For the purposes of this section, the following terms have the following
18 meanings.
19 A. "Commission" means the Public Utilities Commission.
20 B. "Data center" means any facility in the State, which may be a freestanding structure
21 or a facility within a larger structure, that primarily contains electronic equipment used
22 to process, store and transmit digital information that uses environmental control
23 equipment to maintain the proper conditions for the operation of electronic equipment.
24 C. "Electric demand" means, with respect to a data center, the projected or current
25 electricity usage of the data center calculated based on aggregated load at a single site
26 or contiguous parcels under common ownership or control.
27 D. "Exemption order" means an order issued by the commission after an adjudicatory
28 proceeding as described in subsection 3.
29 2. Interim suspension. Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, prior
30 to November 1, 2027, a municipality, a quasi-municipal entity or an agency of the State
31 may not accept an application for or issue, approve or make finally effective a permit,
32 certificate, license, lease authorization or other approval for the development, construction
33 or operation of a data center with a projected or current electric demand of 20 megawatts

23 Sec. 2. Establishment of Maine Data Center Coordination Council. The
24 Department of Energy Resources shall convene the Maine Data Center Coordination
25 Council, referred to in this legislation as "the council," to provide strategic input, facilitate
26 coordinated state planning considerations and evaluate policy tools to address data center
27 opportunities and related benefits and risks to the State. For the purposes of this section,
28 "data center" means any facility in the State, which may be a freestanding structure or a
29 facility within a larger structure, that primarily contains electronic equipment used to
30 process, store and transmit digital information that uses environmental control equipment
31 to maintain the proper conditions for the operation of electronic equipment.
32 1. The council shall evaluate issues related to data centers located or proposed to be
33 located in the State, with the goals of protecting ratepayers, maintaining electric grid
34 reliability, minimizing environmental impacts and enabling responsible and appropriately
35 sited economic development. In conducting its evaluation, the council shall consider the
36 State's policy goals related to renewable energy in the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 35-A,
37 section 3210, greenhouse gas reduction objectives, including those in Title 38, section
38 576-A, goals for broadband service established in Title 35-A, section 9202-A, the state
39 economic development strategy and the legislative findings described in Title 38, section
40 480-A. In carrying out its work, the council shall:
41 A. Review and consider legislation related to data centers that has been considered or
42 adopted in other states;

17 SUMMARY
1188 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft, and changes the title.
19 It establishes a temporary suspension on the authority of a municipality, a quasi-
20 municipal entity or any agency of the State, prior to November 1, 2027, to accept an
21 application for or to issue, approve or make finally effective a permit, certificate, license,
22 lease authorization or other approval for the development, construction or operation of a
23 data center with an electric demand of 20 megawatts or more, other than a data center that
24 has received an exemption order from the Public Utilities Commission. To receive an
25 exemption order, a developer or operator of a data center must demonstrate that it has met
26 certain criteria.
27 The amendment also prohibits a state agency or quasi-independent entity from
28 approving, awarding or disbursing state-funded financial incentives, grants, loans,
29 refundable or transferable tax credits or other discretionary economic development benefits
30 for a data center with an electric demand of 20 megawatts or more prior to November 1,
31 2027. This prohibition does not apply to incentives that were finally approved and legally
32 binding prior to the effective date of this legislation.
33 The amendment also requires the Department of Energy Resources to convene the
34 Maine Data Center Coordination Council to provide strategic input, facilitate coordinated
35 state planning considerations and evaluate policy tools to address data center opportunities
36 and related benefits and risks to the State. By February 1, 2027, the council is required to
37 submit a final strategy report, including the findings of its evaluation and
38 recommendations, to the Governor and the joint standing committee of the Legislature
39 having jurisdiction over energy matters.
