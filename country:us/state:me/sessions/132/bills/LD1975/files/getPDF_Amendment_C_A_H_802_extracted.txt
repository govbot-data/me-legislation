9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1319, L.D. 1975, “An Act to Eliminate
10 the ConnectMaine Authority by Repealing the Advanced Technology Infrastructure Act”
11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'Resolve, Directing the Maine Connectivity Authority to Develop Recommendations
13 to Transition Functions Performed by the ConnectMaine Authority to the Maine
14 Connectivity Authority'
15 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
16 'Sec. 1. Maine Connectivity Authority; statutory review; report. Resolved:
17 That the Maine Connectivity Authority established under the Maine Revised Statutes, Title
18 35-A, section 9404, in consultation with the ConnectMaine Authority established under
19 Title 35-A, section 9203, shall review the provisions of Title 35-A, chapter 93 and develop
20 recommendations for any legislative changes necessary to transition functions performed
21 by the ConnectMaine Authority to the Maine Connectivity Authority by October 1, 2027.
22 On or before January 15, 2027, the Maine Connectivity Authority shall submit a report
23 containing the recommendations resulting from that review, including any proposed
24 legislation, to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over
25 energy, utilities and technology matters. After reviewing the report, the joint standing
26 committee may report out a bill related to the Maine Connectivity Authority's
27 recommendations to the 133rd Legislature in 2027.'
28 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
29 number to read consecutively.
