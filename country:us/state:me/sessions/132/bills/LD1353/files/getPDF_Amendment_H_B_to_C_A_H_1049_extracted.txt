21 SUMMARY
2222 This amendment amends Committee Amendment "A" to require the Commissioner of
2233 Marine Resources to adopt rules by December 31, 2027 to establish a limited entry
2244 commercial menhaden fishing license system that provides preference to individuals who
2255 meet the eligibility standards established in Committee Amendment "A" and reside closest
2266 to the coast of Maine. The commissioner is required to assign licenses under the preference
2277 system as soon as practicable following the effective date of this legislation.
28 Until the commissioner establishes the limited entry system, the eligibility standards
29 must be used, except that the rules may not take effect until all individuals who meet the
30 preference requirements have been offered the opportunity to obtain a license. The
31 amendment also removes the provision of Committee Amendment "A" that directs the
32 Department of Marine Resources to identify groups that should be prioritized for the first
33 available licenses and develop an entry plan to provide the first opportunity for licenses to
34 individuals in the identified groups.
