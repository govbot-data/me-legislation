3333 This amendment strikes and replaces the bill and amends the definition of
3344 "subdivision" in the portion of the site location of development laws in the Maine Revised
3355 Statutes, Title 38 that applies to the division of a parcel of land into 15 or more lots. It
3366 allows for lots with single detached residential housing structures containing up to 4
3377 dwelling units, with or without one accessory dwelling unit. It also excludes land or water
3388 areas that are located within lots used solely for single detached residential housing
3399 structures containing up to 4 dwelling units, with or without one accessory dwelling unit,
4400 from the 20-acre threshold calculation in the site location of development laws.
