31 SUMMARY
3322 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, does the following.
33 1. It removes a provision from the bill regarding the use of a drone to convey or attempt
34 to convey prison contraband in correctional facilities or jails.

COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1446, L.D. 2157
1 2. It removes a provision from the bill that establishes a Class C crime for unauthorized
2 use of a drone on or over the premises of a correctional facility or jail.
3 3. It creates a mens rea requirement that a person must intentionally or knowingly
4 operate a drone on or over the premises of a correctional facility or jail to be found liable
5 for unauthorized use of a drone. A person who violates this provision commits a civil
6 violation for which a fine of not less than $500 may be adjudged.
7 4. It adds a provision that if a person has previously been adjudicated for unauthorized
8 use of a drone on or over the premises of a correctional facility or jail or has been
9 adjudicated for substantially similar conduct in another jurisdiction, and the person
10 subsequently flies a drone on or over the premises of a correctional facility or jail without
11 authorization, the person is guilty of a Class D crime.
