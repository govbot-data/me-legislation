11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'Resolve, Directing the Maine Office of Community Affairs to Submit Legislation and
13 Take Other Actions to Effectuate the Transition of Responsibility for Regulating
14 Manufactured Housing Communities and the Construction and Installation of
15 Manufactured Homes from the Manufactured Housing Board to the Maine Office of
16 Community Affairs'
17 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the title and inserting the following:
18 'Sec. 1. Legislation regarding transfer of rights and obligations of
19 Manufactured Housing Board. Resolved: That the Maine Office of Community
20 Affairs, referred to in this resolve as "the office," shall develop legislation to transition the
21 statutory responsibility for the oversight of manufactured housing manufacturers and
22 manufactured housing communities currently held by the Manufactured Housing Board,
23 established in the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 10, section 9003, subsection 1, and
24 administered by the Department of Professional and Financial Regulation, Office of
25 Professional and Occupational Regulation and to update the law to place the State in a
26 better position to increase the supply of manufactured housing and industrialized housing
27 in the State and to balance and protect the rights and responsibilities of owners and residents
28 of manufactured housing communities. With the assistance of the Office of Policy and
29 Legal Analysis and the Office of the Revisor of Statutes, the office shall review Title 10,
30 chapters 951 and 953 and other applicable statutes governing the oversight of manufactured
31 housing communities and the construction and installation of manufactured housing to:
32 1. Effectuate the transition of responsibilities from the Manufactured Housing Board
33 to the office;
34 2. Reduce conflicts and ambiguities in existing law; and

3 Sec. 2. Office to consult. Resolved: That, in developing legislation under section
4 1 to effectuate the transition of responsibility and update and clarify the Maine Revised
5 Statutes, Title 10, chapters 951 and 953, the office shall consult with representatives of the
6 following groups:
7 1. Residents of manufactured housing communities;

9 3. Businesses that construct manufactured housing;
10 4. Persons that install manufactured housing;
11 5. Municipal officers responsible for inspections of manufactured housing
12 communities and newly constructed and installed manufactured housing;
13 6. Current or former members of the Manufactured Housing Board;
14 7. Members of the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over
15 housing matters;
16 8. The Department of Professional and Financial Regulation; and
17 9. The Office of Policy Innovation and the Future.
18 Sec. 3. Public input. Resolved: That the office shall solicit public input on its
19 work under this resolve. The office shall hold at least one meeting to solicit public input
20 and shall post adequate notice of the meeting on its publicly accessible website.
21 Sec. 4. Transition of authority to act as state administrative agency.
22 Resolved: That the office shall take all action necessary to be authorized by the United
23 States Department of Housing and Urban Development to act as the state administrative
24 agency under 24 Code of Federal Regulations, Part 3282, Subpart G and 42 United States
25 Code, Sections 5401 to 5426.
26 Sec. 5. Legislation; report. Resolved: That, by January 1, 2027, the office shall
27 submit legislation developed pursuant to this resolve to the First Regular Session of the
28 133rd Legislature. The office shall submit a report to the joint standing committee of the
29 Legislature having jurisdiction over housing matters explaining the legislation submitted
30 and shall include specific information on the authorization by the United States Department
31 of Housing and Urban Development for the office to act as the state administrative agency
32 pursuant to 24 Code of Federal Regulations, Part 3282, Subpart G and 42 United States
33 Code, Sections 5401 to 5426.'
34 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
35 number to read consecutively.
