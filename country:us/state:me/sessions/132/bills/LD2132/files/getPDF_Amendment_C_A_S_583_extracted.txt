24 Safety, or the director's designee, shall convene the State Trauma Prevention and Control

28 submit a report with the findings of the advisory committee to the joint standing committee
29 of the Legislature having jurisdiction over emergency medical services matters. The joint
30 standing committee may report out legislation to the Second Regular Session of the 133rd
31 Legislature based on the report.'
32 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
33 number to read consecutively.

COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 826, L.D. 2132
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, changes the title of the
33 bill, adds a definition for "emergency medical services educator" and removes from the bill
44 a provision requiring physicians and hospitals to report trauma information to the
55 Emergency Medical Services' Board. Current law permits physicians and hospitals to report
66 trauma information to the board. The amendment also requires the Director of Maine
77 Emergency Medical Services within the Department of Public Safety, or the director's
88 designee, to convene the State Trauma Prevention and Control Advisory Committee to
99 evaluate opportunities to enhance the State's trauma care system and to share uniform
1100 quality data as it relates to injury-related incidents. By September 1, 2027, the director must
1111 submit a report with the findings of the advisory committee to the joint standing committee
1122 of the Legislature having jurisdiction over emergency medical services matters. The joint
1133 standing committee may report out legislation to the Second Regular Session of the 133rd
1144 Legislature based on the advisory committee's findings.
