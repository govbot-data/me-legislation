31 SUMMARY
3322 This amendment makes the following changes to the bill.
33 1. It removes the requirement that the holder of a research and aquaculture lease submit
34 an annual seeding and harvesting report for the past year and a seeding and harvesting plan
35 for the coming year.
36 2. It establishes a 30-day period after the expiration of a limited-purpose lease for
37 commercial aquaculture research and development in which the holder of the lease may
38 submit an application to extend the lease and submit a $500 late fee for the lease to remain
39 in effect until the Commissioner of Marine Resources makes a decision regarding the
40 application.

1 3. It removes the development and management of water quality licensing and
2 monitoring criteria and analysis and evaluation of monitoring data from the allowable
3 expenditures from the Aquaculture Management Fund and allows funds to be used to
4 improve agency management of existing sites, including conducting education and
5 outreach about compliance and inspection.
6 4. It requires access to aquaculture lease sites, licenses and permits by the
7 commissioner or the commissioner's agent for the purpose of the inspection or the
8 collection of samples and establishes requirements for the collection of samples.
9 5. It clarifies that seeding and harvesting reports that were submitted to the Department
10 of Marine Resources that were confidential at the time the report was submitted remain
11 confidential until those reports are no longer retained by the department pursuant to any
12 records retention schedule.
13 6. It makes the results of testing of a sample collected by the Department of Marine
14 Resources confidential except in certain cases.
15 7. It prohibits the Department of Marine Resources from issuing limited-purpose
16 licenses or authorizing lease amendments for aquaculture until rules are adopted.
