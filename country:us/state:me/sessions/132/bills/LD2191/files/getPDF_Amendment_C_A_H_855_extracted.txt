25 SUMMARY
2266 This amendment does the following.
27 1. It reduces from 1,000 to 700 the minimum number of motor vehicles a business that
28 rents noncommercial motor vehicles must have registered in Maine to be issued a 2-year
29 certificate of inspection for each vehicle.
30 2. It sets the fee for inspection stickers issued to a business that rents noncommercial
31 motor vehicles at 2 times the fee for inspection stickers issued annually to persons who are
32 not new motor vehicle dealers.
