21 SUMMARY
2222 This amendment amends the bill to remove the provision that provides that the
2233 Department of Labor may not allow the pooling of risk, financial resources or
2244 administration among multiple employers under the law governing the paid family and
2255 medical leave benefits program. It provides that multiple employers in a private plan in
2266 the form of self-insurance may share the costs of legal, accounting, surety and 3rd-party
2277 administrator expenses, but also specifies that self-insured employers are strictly liable to
2288 pay all of their own claims. It removes the retroactivity provision of the bill.
