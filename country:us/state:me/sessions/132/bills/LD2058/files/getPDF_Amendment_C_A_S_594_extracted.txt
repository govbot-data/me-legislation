17 'Sec. 1. 25 MRSA §1502, 6th ¶, as amended by PL 1989, c. 757, is further amended
18 to read:
19 Municipal and county jails shall at all times be available for detention of persons

24 officer. Expense of any municipality or any damage to the jail resulting from the use of its
25 jail by any arresting officer shall must be reimbursed to the municipality by the law
26 enforcement agency for which the arresting officer is acting.'

28 number to read consecutively.
