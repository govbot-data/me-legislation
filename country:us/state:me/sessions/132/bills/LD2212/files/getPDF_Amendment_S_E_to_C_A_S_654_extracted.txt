15 Sec. IIII-1. Transfer from General Fund; Maine Agriculture, Food and
16 Forest Products Investment Fund. Notwithstanding any provision of law to the
17 contrary, on or before June 30, 2027, the State Controller shall transfer $24,000,000 from
18 the unappropriated surplus of the General Fund to the Maine Agriculture, Food and Forest
19 Products Investment Fund Other Special Revenue Funds account within the Department of
20 Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry to provide funds to strengthen the State's
21 agricultural, food and forest products economy and expand infrastructure investments in
22 the agricultural, food and forest products economy pursuant to the Maine Revised Statutes,
23 Title 7, section 320-A, including $12,000,000 for activities of the fund pertaining to
24 forestry, $4,000,000 for activities of the fund pertaining to milk producers and $8,000,000
25 for activities of the fund pertaining to agriculture other than the activities of milk producers.
26 Sec. IIII-2. Transfer from General Fund; Farmers Drought Relief Grant
27 Program. Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, on or before June 30,
28 2027, the State Controller shall transfer $4,000,000 from the unappropriated surplus of the
29 General Fund to the Farmers Drought Relief Grant Program Other Special Revenue Funds
30 account within the Department of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry to support the
31 activities of the program pursuant to the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 7, section 220-A.
32 Sec. IIII-3. Transfer from General Fund; Maine Healthy Soils Program.
33 Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, on or before June 30, 2027, the State
34 Controller shall transfer $4,000,000 from the unappropriated surplus of the General Fund
35 to the Maine Healthy Soils Program Other Special Revenue Funds account within the
36 Department of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry to provide funds to support the
37 activities of the program pursuant to the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 12, section 352.

18 SUMMARY
1199 This amendment transfers a total of $40,000,000 from the unappropriated surplus of
2200 the General Fund to support the activities of the Maine Agriculture, Food and Forestry
2211 Products Investment Fund, the Farmers Drought Relief Grant Program, the Maine Healthy
2222 Soils Program, the Maine Working Farmland Access and Protection Program and the Dairy
2233 Improvement Fund.
24 The amendment reduces the amount of funding provided in Part A for several
25 programs, including funding for schools with a high proportion of economically
26 disadvantaged students, the Maine Child Care Affordability Program, certain MaineCare
27 and Medicaid services and the shelter operating subsidy. It also eliminates an appropriation
28 for one-time funding for a supplemental payment to the Maine Veterans' Homes for
29 MaineCare residents.
30 The amendment also deappropriates $12,000,000 from the Department of Health and
31 Human Services. Those funds must lapse into the unappropriated surplus of the General
32 Fund. The amendment also directs the department to identify 135 positions or the
33 equivalent of 135 positions to eliminate in order to deappropriate $12,000,000 from the
34 department by no later than September 1, 2026.
