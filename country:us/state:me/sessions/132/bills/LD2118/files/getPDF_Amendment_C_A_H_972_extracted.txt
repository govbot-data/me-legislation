24 SUMMARY
2255 This amendment repeals on September 30, 2029 the provision of the bill that prohibits
2266 the Commissioner of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife from adopting rules to protect plants
2277 and plant communities except when required for the conservation of rare fish or wildlife
2288 species, threatened fish or wildlife species or endangered fish or wildlife species.
29 The amendment directs the Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife to provide a
30 report, including any findings and recommendations, to the joint standing committees of
31 the Legislature having jurisdiction over inland fisheries and wildlife matters and
32 agriculture, conservation and forestry matters regarding the effect of this prohibition. Each
33 committee may submit legislation related to the subject matter of the report to the 134th
34 Legislature in 2029.
35 The amendment allows the Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife to transfer
36 funds from available balances of Personal Services appropriations to the Resource
37 Management Services - Inland Fisheries and Wildlife program for the purposes of funding
38 early detection and rapid response efforts to eradicate invasive stilt grass and funding an
39 update to the State's official list of threatened and endangered plants.
40 The amendment also removes from the bill a provision allowing the Department of
41 Inland Fisheries and Wildlife to determine that a municipality has a comprehensive land
42 use plan in providing for the protection of wildlife habitat, fisheries, unusual natural areas
