24 1. The advisory group is made up of 6 members who must be residents of the State at
25 the time of their appointment. Members appointed to the advisory group are not subject to
26 legislative review or senate confirmation. All members of the advisory group must be
27 appointed prior to September 30, 2026. The advisory group consists of the following
28 members:
29 A. Two members of the Senate, one of whom must be appointed as joint chair of the
30 advisory group, appointed by the President of the Senate;
31 B. Two members of the House of Representatives, one of whom must be appointed as
32 joint chair of the advisory group, appointed by the Speaker of the House;
33 C. One member who is a resident of a county through which the Maine Turnpike runs,
34 appointed by the President of the Senate; and
35 D. One representative from the American Civil Liberties Union of Maine or another
36 civil liberties organization, appointed by the Speaker of the House.
