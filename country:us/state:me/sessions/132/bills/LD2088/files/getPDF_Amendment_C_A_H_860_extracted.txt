28 SUMMARY
2299 This amendment is the minority report of the committee. Like the bill, the amendment
3300 removes from current law the requirement that a physician associate with more than 4,000
3311 hours of clinical practice must have a signed practice agreement with a physician in order
3322 to be the principal clinical provider in a practice that does not include a physician partner.
3333 While the bill does not provide a replacement to this requirement, the amendment, instead
3344 of a practice agreement, requires a physician associate to file documentation with the
3355 appropriate licensing board that describes the physician associate's scope of practice as
3366 determined by the independent practice setting, including information about the location of
3377 the physician associate's independent practice and how the physician associate will consult
3388 with or collaborate with an appropriate physician or other health care professional as
3399 indicated by a patient's condition or refer a patient to a physician or other health care
4400 professional.
