21 SUMMARY
2222 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, makes the following
2233 changes to the bill.
24 1. It authorizes an eligible financial institution to charge an origination fee that is the
25 lesser of $60 and 1% of the total loan amount and requires the financial institution to add
26 the origination fee to the total amount of the loan.
27 2. Instead of removing language that provides that if the amount expended for loan
28 guarantee payments equals or exceeds 10% of the total of all loans issued, the Finance
29 Authority of Maine must stop approving claims, it changes the amount that must be
30 expended before the authority stops approving claims from 10% of the total of all loans
31 issued to 50% of the total of all loans issued.
32 3. If the total amount of funds expended for loan guarantee payments under the
33 Government Shutdown Loan Guarantee Program equals or exceeds $200,000, it requires
34 the authority to immediately notify the Treasurer of State, the joint standing committee of
35 the Legislature having jurisdiction over appropriations and financial affairs and the joint
36 standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over financial services matters
37 and requires the reporting out of legislation to address the funding needs of the program
38 after that notification is received.
