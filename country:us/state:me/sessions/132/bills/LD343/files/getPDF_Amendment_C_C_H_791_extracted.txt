14 'Sec. 1. 35-A MRSA §125 is enacted to read:
15 §125. Requests for proposals for establishment of small modular nuclear reactor
16 1. Small modular nuclear reactor or modular reactor defined. As used in this
17 section, unless the context otherwise indicates, "small modular nuclear reactor" or
18 "modular reactor" means a nuclear reactor that:
19 A. Has a rated generating capacity of no more than 350 megawatts;
20 B. Is capable of being constructed and operated at a single site on its own or in
21 combination with one or more nuclear reactors; and
22 C. Is required to be licensed by the United States Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
23 2. Requests for informational bids. By October 1, 2026, and triennially thereafter,
24 the commission shall request informational bids regarding the establishment of a small
25 modular nuclear reactor in the State. The informational bids must include the costs of
26 establishing a modular reactor in the State, either through manufacture on site or transport
27 into the State, the proposed location of a modular reactor, the time needed to establish a
28 modular reactor in the State, the annual operational costs of a modular reactor and any other
29 information the commission determines necessary. The commission shall issue protective
30 orders pursuant to section 1311-A to protect the information provided by persons
31 submitting bids. The bids are not binding on the State or the person submitting the bid.
32 3. Triennial report. By January 15, 2027, and triennially thereafter, the commission
33 shall submit to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over
34 energy and utility matters a report regarding the informational bids received by the
35 commission pursuant to subsection 2. The commission shall provide information in the

10 SUMMARY
1111 This amendment requires the Public Utilities Commission to request, by October 1,
1122 2026, and triennially thereafter, informational bids regarding the establishment of a small
1133 modular nuclear reactor in the State instead of requiring the commission to conduct an
1144 annual request for proposals for informational bids starting on October 1st, as proposed in
1155 the bill. It also directs the commission to issue protective orders to protect the information
1166 provided by persons submitting bids. The amendment changes the reporting date
