COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 704, L.D. 1804
1 1. It excludes financial matters related to the Highway Fund, including transfers of
2 allocations from the Highway Fund, from oversight by the joint standing committee of the
3 Legislature having jurisdiction over appropriations and financial affairs.
4 2. It clarifies that use of the Highway Fund for the multimodal transportation fund is
5 subject to limitations provided in the Constitution of Maine, Article IX, Section 19.
6 3. It removes provisions of the bill that require allocations from the Highway Fund to
7 meet minimum funding levels for construction and maintenance of highway bridges, lights
8 and multimodal transportation and replaces those provisions with language that establishes,
9 beginning July 1, 2027, baseline expenditure requirements to be used for capital in the
10 Department of Transportation, Highway and Bridge Capital; Highway Light Capital; and
11 Multimodal Transportation Fund programs.
12 4. It requires, beginning in fiscal year 2030-31, the State Budget Officer to adjust the
13 baseline for the Highway and Bridge Capital, Highway Light Capital and Multimodal
14 Transportation Fund programs in the Department of Transportation by the percentage
15 change in the Consumer Price Index from the 2 complete calendar years preceding the
16 biennial budget on or before September 1st of each even-numbered year.
17 5. It requires that, beginning in fiscal year 2027-28, state funding for the Department
18 of Public Safety, Bureau of State Police is no longer divided to require 35% from the
19 Highway Fund and 65% from the General Fund, but instead requires that certain functions
20 of the bureau be funded by the Highway Fund and certain other functions be funded by the
21 General Fund.
22 6. It clarifies that, beginning July 1, 2027, the Highway Fund may be used to fund the
23 administration of the Department of Public Safety, Bureau of State Police for certain
24 specified purposes only. It also provides that the Highway Fund may be used to fund the
25 Department of Public Safety, Bureau of Highway Safety.
26 7. It modifies language in the bill that amends the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 36,
27 section 1821 by increasing from 60% to 100% the percentage of revenue from automobile-
28 related sales and use taxes that must be transferred from the General Fund to the Highway
29 Fund beginning July 1, 2027.
