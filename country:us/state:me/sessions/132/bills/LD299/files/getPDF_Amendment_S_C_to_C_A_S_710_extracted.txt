24 Sec. 7. Prohibition of financial benefit to Legislators and their associates.
25 Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, any current or former member of the
26 132nd Legislature may not directly receive, solicit or retain any financial benefit derived
27 from the proceeds of any funds authorized under this Act during the member's term in the
28 132nd Legislature or for 5 years following the date on which the member's term in the
29 132nd Legislature ends.
30 This prohibition applies whether the financial benefit described in this section is for
31 the member's own benefit or for the benefit of any relative, household member, close
32 economic association or associated organization of the member. For the purposes of this
33 section, "relative," "close economic association" and "associated organization" have the
34 same meanings as in the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 1, section 1012.
