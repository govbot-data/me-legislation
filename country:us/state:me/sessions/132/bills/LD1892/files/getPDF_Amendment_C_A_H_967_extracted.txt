1 §15919. School Construction Accelerator Fund
2 1. Fund established. The School Construction Accelerator Fund, referred to in this
3 section as "the fund," is established as a nonlapsing fund within the department and
4 operated in conjunction with the state board for the purpose of awarding funding to school
5 administrative units for immediate heating, ventilation or air-conditioning system repair
6 needs or roof repair needs in schools.
7 2. Rulemaking. The state board, in consultation with the department, shall adopt rules
8 for the awarding of funds to school administrative units from the fund, which must include
9 award eligibility guidelines. Rules adopted under this subsection are routine technical rules
10 as defined in Title 5, chapter 375, subchapter 2‑A.
11 3. Funding. Beginning in fiscal year 2027-28 and in each subsequent fiscal year, the
12 State Controller shall transfer to the fund 50% of the unobligated balances from amounts
13 appropriated for general purpose aid for local schools that would otherwise be carried
14 forward.

35 SUMMARY
3366 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill and
3377 changes the title. It makes the following changes to the laws regarding school construction.
38 1. It provides that the maximum debt service limit for approved school construction
39 projects is increased to $188,779,298 in fiscal year 2028; $194,442,677 in fiscal year 2029;
40 $200,275,957 in fiscal year 2030; and $206,284,236 in fiscal year 2031.

1 2. It provides that, beginning in fiscal year 2027-28, debt service costs that exceed the
2 fiscal year 2027-28 maximum debt service limit may not be included in the total cost of
3 education calculated under the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 20-A, chapter 606-B.
4 3. It directs the State Board of Education, in consultation with the Department of
5 Education, to adopt rules establishing a cost-sharing formula for funding major school
6 construction projects.
7 4. It establishes the School Construction Accelerator Fund within the department for
8 the purposes of awarding funds to school administrative units for immediate heating,
9 ventilation or air-conditioning system repair needs or roof repair needs in schools.
10 5. It directs the State Controller to, at the end of fiscal year 2026-27 and at the close of
11 each fiscal year thereafter, transfer $50,000,000 from the lapsed funds or unappropriated
12 surplus of the General Fund to the School Revolving Renovation Fund.
13 6. It directs the Maine Municipal Bond Bank and the department to adopt rules to
14 increase to $8,000,000 the limit on the maximum loan amount from the School Revolving
15 Renovation Fund to address each priority in a school building within a 5-year period.
16 7. It directs the department to convene a working group to make recommendations
17 related to any objectives, strategies and actions identified by the Governor's Commission
18 on School Construction in a report issued by the commission in 2026 and submit a report
19 to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over education

21 appropriations and financial affairs and to the Governor by January 15, 2027.
