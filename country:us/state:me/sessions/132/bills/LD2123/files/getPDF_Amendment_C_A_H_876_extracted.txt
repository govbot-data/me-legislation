16 'Sec. 1. Department of Health and Human Services to remove requirement
17 for prior authorization for deep sedation or general anesthesia for dental
18 services. Resolved: That, effective July 1, 2026, the Department of Health and Human
19 Services shall provide that prior authorization may not be required for the provision of deep
20 sedation or general anesthesia for dental services to MaineCare members.
21 Sec. 2. Department of Health and Human Services to modify fee schedule;
22 reimbursement rates for anesthesia provided for dental services. Resolved:
23 That, effective July 1, 2026, the Department of Health and Human Services shall update
24 the department's fee schedule for MaineCare reimbursement for dental services as follows:
25 1. The reimbursement rate for deep sedation and general anesthesia for dental services
26 must be established at 67% of the commercial median benchmark and this rate must, at a
27 minimum, be maintained until June 30, 2028 or until rates established pursuant to a dental
28 rate determination conducted according to the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 22, section
29 3173-J are effective, whichever is sooner; and
30 2. Deep sedation and general anesthesia services must be billed using current dental
31 terminology codes D9222, D9223, D9224 and D9225.
