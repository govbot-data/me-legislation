9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 772, L.D. 1965, “Resolve, to Establish a
10 Task Force to Create a Court Navigation Program Plan”
11 Amend the resolve by striking out everything after the emergency preamble and before
12 the emergency clause and inserting the following:
13 'Sec. 1. University of Maine System to convene task force. Resolved: That
14 the University of Maine System shall convene a task force, referred to in this resolve as
15 "the task force," to create a court navigation program plan. The membership of the task
16 force must consist of at least one representative from each of the following:
17 1. The schools of social work of the University of Maine System;
18 2. The University of Maine School of Law;
19 3. Maine Pretrial Services, Inc.;
20 4. Pine Tree Legal Assistance;
21 5. Disability Rights Maine;
22 6. The National Alliance on Mental Illness Maine;
23 7. 211 Maine;
24 8. The Department of Health and Human Services; and
25 9. The judicial branch.
26 Sec. 2. Court navigation program plan. Resolved: That the task force shall
27 examine court navigation programs nationwide, recommend a model for a court navigation
28 program for use in the State and create a plan for implementing the program. At a
29 minimum, the court navigation program model recommended by the task force must be
30 designed to:
31 1. Provide guidance and information about court processes and procedures to persons
32 involved with the court system;

14 including a model for a court navigation program for use in this State, and that includes
15 suggested legislation to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction

17 the subject matter of the report to the 133rd Legislature in 2027.'
