31 SUMMARY
3322 This amendment, which is a minority report of the committee, replaces the bill and
3333 changes the title. The amendment amends several provisions of state law affecting the
3344 Passamaquoddy Tribe, the Penobscot Nation, the Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians and
3355 the Mi'kmaq Nation, referred to in this summary as "the Wabanaki Nations."
36 Part A of the amendment provides that, beginning with the First Regular Session of the
37 133rd Legislature, the Mi'kmaq Nation may, like the other Wabanaki Nations, elect a
38 representative to the House of Representatives of the Maine Legislature. The
39 representative of the Mi'kmaq Nation has, to the same extent as other members of the
40 Legislature, the right to receive a salary for each regular session of the Legislature, an
41 allowance for constituent services, allowances for travel-related expenses and per diem
42 payments for the duration of any special session of the Legislature.

1 Part B of the amendment clarifies that the sale of new manufactured housing to an
2 enrolled member of one of the Wabanaki Nations is exempt from Maine sales tax if the
3 new manufactured housing is intended to be installed on Indian territory or trust land. It
4 also newly exempts from Maine sales tax the sale of new manufactured housing to a
5 construction contractor or subcontractor if the new manufactured housing is intended to be
6 physically incorporated in, and become a permanent part of real property located on, Indian
7 territory or trust land for sale to one of the Wabanaki Nations or to an enrolled member of
