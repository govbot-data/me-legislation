9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 991, L.D. 1507, “An Act to Require
10 General Public Notification of Oil Terminal Facility Transfer Activities”
11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'An Act to Require the Installation of Warning Signs at Certain Oil Storage Facilities'
13 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
14 following:
15 'Sec. 1. 38 MRSA §590, sub-§1, ¶F is enacted to read:
16 F. A licensed petroleum storage facility that includes an aboveground petroleum
17 storage tank with a storage capacity greater than 39,000 gallons shall install and
18 maintain warning signs at the facility in accordance with the requirements of this
19 paragraph.
20 (1) Warning signs must be installed on all publicly accessible property boundaries
21 of the facility at intervals no greater than every 100 feet.
22 (2) Each warning sign must satisfy the visibility standards contained in the
23 American National Standards Institute standard Z35.1.
24 (3) Each warning sign must include the following text:
25 CAUTION
26 PETROLEUM STORAGE AREA
27 HAZARDOUS AIR POLLUTANTS, VOLATILE ORGANIC
28 COMPOUNDS AND FLAMMABLE VAPORS MAY BE PRESENT.
29 (4) Each warning sign must include emergency contact information for the facility.
30 (5) Each warning sign must indicate that the facility is licensed by the department
31 and must include the Internet address for the publicly accessible air emissions
32 licensing website maintained by the department.

66 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill and
77 changes the title. It amends the laws governing the licensing of petroleum storage facilities
88 to require the licensed facilities that include an aboveground petroleum storage tank with a
99 storage capacity greater than 39,000 gallons to install and maintain warning signs. The
1100 warning signs are required to be installed on all publicly accessible property boundaries of
1111 a licensed facility at intervals no greater than every 100 feet and must include the following
1122 text: CAUTION, PETROLEUM STORAGE AREA, HAZARDOUS AIR POLLUTANTS,
1133 VOLATILE ORGANIC COMPOUNDS AND FLAMMABLE VAPORS MAY BE
1144 PRESENT. The warning signs must also satisfy additional specified requirements
1155 regarding design and included information.
