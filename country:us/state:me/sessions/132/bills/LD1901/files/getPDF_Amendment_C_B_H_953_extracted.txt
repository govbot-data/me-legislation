9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1272, L.D. 1901, “An Act to Regulate
10 Shared Appreciation Agreements Relating to Residential Property”
11 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
12 following:
13 'Sec. 1. 9-A MRSA §1-301, sub-§17-B is enacted to read:
14 17-B. Dwelling. "Dwelling" has the same meaning as in section 13-102, subsection
15 3.
16 Sec. 2. 9-A MRSA §1-301, sub-§37-A is enacted to read:
17 37-A. Shared appreciation mortgage loan. "Shared appreciation mortgage loan"
18 means a writing evidencing a transaction or any option, any future or any other derivative
19 between a person and a consumer according to which the consumer receives money or any
20 other item of value in exchange for an interest or future interest in a dwelling or residential
21 real estate or a future obligation to pay an amount based on the value of the dwelling or
22 residential real estate that is secured by a mortgage, a deed of trust or an equivalent
23 consensual security interest in the dwelling or residential real estate on the occurrence of
24 an event, such as the transfer of ownership; a maturity date; the death of the consumer; or
25 any other event contemplated by the writing. "Shared appreciation mortgage loan" does
26 not include any shared appreciation mortgage loan extended through a federal or state
27 government agency, a government-sponsored enterprise or a municipal agency or
28 enterprise of the State.
29 Sec. 3. 9-A MRSA §3-317 is enacted to read:
30 §3-317. Moratorium on shared appreciation mortgage loans
31 Until January 1, 2030, a person may not enter into a shared appreciation mortgage loan
32 with a consumer that is secured by a dwelling or residential real estate in this State. Any
33 shared appreciation mortgage loan entered into in violation of this section is void and
34 unenforceable.'
