9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 802, L.D. 1990, “An Act to Update the
10 Requirements for Psychology Licensure”
11 Amend the bill by inserting before section 1 the following:
12 'Sec. 1. 32 MRSA §3831, sub-§1, as amended by PL 2007, c. 402, Pt. Q, §9, is
13 further amended to read:
14 1. Psychological examiner. A candidate for this license shall furnish the board with
15 satisfactory evidence that the candidate is trustworthy and competent to practice as a
16 psychological examiner in such manner as to safeguard the interests of the public; has had
17 a master's degree reflecting comprehensive training in psychology from an accredited
18 educational institution recognized by the board as maintaining satisfactory standards; has
19 had at least one year of full-time supervised experience in psychology of a type considered
20 by the board to be qualifying in nature; is competent as a psychological examiner as shown
21 by passing such examinations, written or oral, or both, as the board determines necessary;
22 and is not considered by the board to be engaged in unethical practice; and has not within
23 the preceding 6 months failed an examination.'
24 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
25 number to read consecutively.

2277 This amendment modifies the licensure requirements for psychological examiners in

3300 examiner at any time after successful passage of a qualifying examination.
