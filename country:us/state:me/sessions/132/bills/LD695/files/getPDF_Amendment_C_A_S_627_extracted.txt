COMMITTEE AMENDMENT

COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to S.P. 312, L.D. 695
1 1. Definitions. As used in this section, unless the context otherwise indicates, the
2 following terms have the following meanings.
3 A. "Fund" means the Legislator Residential Security Fund established in subsection 6.
4 B. "Program" means the Legislator Residential Security Program established in
5 subsection 2.
6 C. "Security professional" means a person selected by the Legislative Council pursuant
7 to subsection 5 to install, monitor or maintain home security systems.
8 2. Program established. The Legislator Residential Security Program is established
9 to mitigate the risks to the physical security of members of the Legislature in their
10 residences.
11 3. Home security system. At the request of a current member of the Legislature, if
12 sufficient funds are available, the Legislative Council shall use the fund, in accordance with
13 policies and procedures established under subsection 4, to:
14 A. Issue payment for the cost of installing a wireless, wired or hybrid home security
15 system by a security professional at that member's residence; and
16 B. Issue payments for service fees associated with monitoring and maintaining a home
17 security system installed at that member's residence by a security professional.
18 Upon the termination of a member's legislative service, the Legislative Council may not
19 expend funds from the fund for service fees associated with monitoring or maintaining that
20 former member's home security system.
21 4. Administration of program. The Executive Director of the Legislative Council
22 shall administer the program in accordance with policies and procedures established by the
23 Legislative Council as required by Title 3, section 162, subsection 20 and in the manner
24 determined by the Executive Director of the Legislative Council.
25 5. Selection of security professionals. The Legislative Council shall select, through
26 a competitive bidding process, licensed and qualified security professionals to install,
27 monitor and maintain home security systems pursuant to this section and in accordance
28 with the policies and procedures established by the Legislative Council.
29 6. Legislator Residential Security Fund. The Legislator Residential Security Fund
30 is established in the Legislature as a nonlapsing account in accordance with Title 5, section
31 1591, subsection 11 administered by the Legislative Council to be used for the purposes of
32 this section.
33 Sec. 5. 5 MRSA §1591, sub-§11 is enacted to read:
34 11. Legislature. Any balance remaining in the Legislator Residential Security Fund
35 program, General Fund account in the Legislature at the end of any fiscal year must be
36 carried forward for use by the program in the next fiscal year for the same purpose.
37 Sec. 6. Appropriations and allocations. The following appropriations and
38 allocations are made.

10 SUMMARY
1111 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill, which
1122 is a concept draft, and changes the title. This amendment establishes the Legislator
1133 Residential Security Program and Legislator Residential Security Fund to mitigate the risks
1144 to the physical security of sitting Legislators. The program is administered by the
1155 Executive Director of the Legislative Council. The fund must be used in accordance with
1166 policies and procedures established by the Legislative Council for the installation,
1177 monitoring and maintenance of a home security system for the residence of each sitting
1188 Legislator who requests it, subject to the availability of funds.
