17 SUMMARY
1188 This amendment is the majority report of the committee and replaces the bill. The
1199 amendment clarifies that an HIV test may be undertaken only with a patient's informed
2200 consent. The amendment also removes the requirement that a patient's authorization of
2211 disclosure of confidential HIV test results to a designated health care provider must be in
2222 writing. The amendment also provides that disclosure of information in a medical record
2233 for the purpose of seeking insurance reimbursement for HIV testing is not precluded.
