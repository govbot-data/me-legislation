9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1377, L.D. 2053, “An Act to Improve
10 Safety by Allowing the Use of Variable-message Signs on Public Service Vehicles”
11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'An Act to Improve Safety by Allowing the Use of Variable-message Signs on Certain
13 Vehicles'
14 Amend the bill by inserting after the title and before the enacting clause the following:
15 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
16 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
17 Whereas, vehicles that assist with traffic incidents and improve roadway safety that
18 are equipped with variable-message signs mounted on the vehicle are prepared to begin
19 using variable-message signs immediately; and
20 Whereas, a delay in allowing vehicles that assist with traffic incidents and improve
21 roadway safety to use variable-message signs could allow for a greater number of traffic-
22 related injuries to occur; and
23 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
24 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
25 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
26 therefore,'
27 Amend the bill by striking out everything after the enacting clause and inserting the
28 following:
29 'Sec. 1. 29-A MRSA §2054, sub-§1, ¶K is enacted to read:
30 K. “Safety service patrol vehicle” means a vehicle, equipped with a variable-message
31 sign, used or contracted by the department or the Maine Turnpike Authority to assist
32 with traffic incidents and improve roadway safety.
33 Sec. 2. 29-A MRSA §2054, sub-§1, ¶L is enacted to read:

COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1377, L.D. 2053
1 L. "Variable-message sign" means a traffic control device on which a message can be
2 displayed and changed manually, electronically, mechanically, electromechanically or
3 by other means to provide information about traffic congestion, traffic accidents,
4 maintenance operations, adverse weather conditions, roadway conditions or organized
5 events or other necessary roadway safety information.
6 Sec. 3. 29-A MRSA §2054, sub-§12 is enacted to read:
7 12. Variable-message signs. A variable-message sign may be used on a wrecker,
8 police vehicle, fire department vehicle, safety service patrol vehicle or highway
9 maintenance vehicle in the manner prescribed in this subsection. The variable-message sign
10 may not exceed the width of the vehicle on which it is mounted and may be displayed only
11 facing traffic behind the vehicle. The operator of a vehicle under this subsection may use a
12 variable-message sign as follows.
13 A. The operator of a wrecker may use a variable-message sign on that vehicle while it
14 is stationary and engaged in repair, loading or unloading a vehicle or performing a
15 vehicle recovery. A variable-message sign mounted on a wrecker must display amber
16 lights that create right or left arrows or the words “slow down” or “move over.”
17 B. The operator of a police vehicle, fire department vehicle, safety service patrol
18 vehicle or highway maintenance vehicle may use a variable-message sign on that
19 vehicle to provide information about traffic congestion, traffic accidents, maintenance
20 operations, adverse weather conditions, roadway conditions or organized events or
21 other necessary roadway safety information. The variable-message sign must display
22 right or left arrows or the words “slow down,” “move over,” “accident ahead,” “crash
23 ahead” or other similar language. Other messages not described in this paragraph may
24 be displayed on a variable-message sign if the additional messages are established by
25 rule and in line with standards in accordance with the latest edition of the Manual on
26 Uniform Traffic Control Devices published by the United States Department of
27 Transportation, Federal Highway Administration.
28 The Commissioner of Public Safety or the commissioner’s designee may adopt rules
29 as necessary for the purpose of implementing this paragraph. Rules adopted pursuant
30 to this paragraph are routine technical rules as defined in Title 5, chapter 375,
31 subchapter 2-A.
32 Emergency clause. In view of the emergency cited in the preamble, this legislation
33 takes effect when approved.'
34 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
35 number to read consecutively.

1 3. It requires a variable-message sign on a wrecker, police vehicle, fire department

17 maintenance vehicle to right or left arrows or the words “slow down,” “move over,”
18 “accident ahead,” “crash ahead” or other similar language approved by rule.
