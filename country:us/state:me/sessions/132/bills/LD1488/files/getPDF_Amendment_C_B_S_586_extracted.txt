10 SUMMARY
1111 This amendment removes the provision in the bill allowing the simultaneous use of
1122 manufacturing-related equipment, supplies and facilities for the manufacturing of cannabis
1133 products and hemp products. It also provides that the use of cannabis concentrate in the
1144 creation of an edible cannabis product is not considered further processing, manufacturing
1155 or alternation for the purpose of additional testing, except for testing for
1166 tetrahydrocannabinol potency, homogeneity and cannabinoid profiles to ensure correct
1177 labeling. The amendment adds an emergency preamble and emergency clause to the bill.
