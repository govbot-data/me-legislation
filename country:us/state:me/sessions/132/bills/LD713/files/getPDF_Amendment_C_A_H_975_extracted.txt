10 SUMMARY
1111 This amendment changes the title and replaces the bill, which is a concept draft. The
1122 amendment provides that a data center that begins operations on or after August 1, 2026 is
1133 excluded from the Dirigo business incentives program and that property located in a data
1144 center that begins operations or after August 1, 2026 does not qualify as eligible business
1155 equipment for the purposes of the business equipment tax exemption. The amendment
1166 requires the Department of Economic and Community Development to study potential
1177 financial incentives that a data center may benefit from based on current state laws. The
1188 department must submit a report based on the study to the joint standing committee of the
1199 Legislature having jurisdiction over taxation matters no later than November 4, 2026.
