21 SUMMARY
2222 This amendment keeps the emergency preamble and clause and replaces the rest of the
2233 bill. It makes the following changes and additions to applicable law:
24 1. It clarifies that an accessory dwelling unit is required to have fire suppression
25 sprinklers when the unit is located within or attached to a mixed-use or nonresidential
26 building or when sprinkler protection is otherwise required.
27 2. It amends the law governing rate of growth ordinances to specify that the limitations
28 on those ordinances apply only to ordinances that affect designated growth areas and also
29 changes some of the limitations applicable to ordinances affecting designated growth areas.
30 3. It clarifies that for areas outside a growth area a municipality may enact rate of
31 growth ordinances.
32 4. It adds a requirement under the provision of law governing affordable housing
33 density that to be eligible for additional height allowance, an affordable housing
34 development must be approved by the Office of the State Fire Marshal or a registered
35 municipality under the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 25, section 2448-A and not a
36 municipal fire official or designee.
37 5. It restricts the additional height allowance under the law regulating affordable
38 housing density to a total building height of 55 feet.
39 6. It replaces the phrase "public, special district or other comparable sewer system"
40 found in current law governing affordable housing density, accessory dwelling units and

14 provisions and replacing them with the chapter 385 versions, modified to remove the
15 requirement that an affordable housing development must comply with the minimum lot

17 10. It changes the minimum lot size and density limits in certain areas in a municipality.
18 11. It clarifies that planning board approval is not required for accessory dwelling units
19 or solely because a project proposes to establish 4 or fewer dwelling units within a single
20 structure on a lot.
21 12. It limits when an accessory dwelling unit in a residential use area must be allowed
22 to situations in which the accessory dwelling unit is located on the same lot as a single-
23 family dwelling or a 2-unit or 3-unit residential structure.
24 13. It subjects a family child care provider or small child care facility and not all child
25 care facilities located in an area zoned for residential purposes to the same zoning
26 requirements as a single-family dwelling unit.
27 14. It delays until July 1, 2027 the application of this legislation and of Public Law
28 2025, chapters 288, 374 and 385. The provisions of this legislation that amend the
29 application dates of Public Law 2025, chapters 288, 374 and 385 apply retroactively to
30 September 24, 2025.
