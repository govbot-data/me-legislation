25 SUMMARY
2266 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill and
2277 does the following.
28 1. It prohibits the owner of a manufactured housing community from raising the lot
29 rent more than once per calendar year.
30 2. It clarifies that if an owner of a manufactured housing community requires, requests
31 or obtains a nondisclosure agreement or similar agreement from the owner of manufactured
32 housing in the process of a mediation between the community owner and housing owner,
33 the community owner is acting in bad faith under the law.
34 3. It changes the minimum lot size required for a new manufactured housing
35 community or the expansion of an existing manufactured housing community.
36 4. It amends the existing provision of law that allows the Office of the Secretary of
37 State to cancel a certificate of title for manufactured housing or a tiny home to allow, upon
38 request, for cancellation of title for manufactured housing or a tiny home that is
39 permanently affixed to real property, whether or not the real property is owned by the
40 homeowner, and for conversion of the manufactured housing or tiny home to residential
41 real property.
