15 Sec. IIII-1. 34-A MRSA §1208-B, sub-§3, as enacted by PL 2021, c. 263, §2, is

18 further amended to read:
19 8. Visitation. A reasonable opportunity to visit with relatives and friends, in
20 accordance with departmental policies and institutional procedures, except that the
21 department may restrict or prohibit visits when the restriction or prohibition is necessary
22 for the security of the institution. Departmental policies and institutional procedures must
23 provide to a person in a correctional facility or detention facility opportunities and
24 conditions for visits with the child of the person that provide time together in settings that
25 allow for as positive a parent-child interaction as practicably can be achieved while
26 protecting the emotional and physical well-being of the child, as long as such visits are not
27 prohibited by court order, prohibited by a department policy due to the child's being a
28 victim of the person, contrary to the wishes of the child's other parent or guardian or
29 inconsistent with the security of the institution; and
30 Sec. IIII-3. 34-A MRSA §3031, sub-§9, as amended by PL 2021, c. 263, §4, is
31 further amended to read:
32 9. Menstrual products. Comprehensive access to menstrual products, including, but
33 not limited to, sanitary pads and tampons, provided and available at all times and without
34 inconvenience or charge to a person who menstruates who resides in a correctional or
35 detention facility; and.
36 Sec. IIII-4. 34-A MRSA §3031, sub-§10, as enacted by PL 2021, c. 263, §5, is
37 repealed.'
