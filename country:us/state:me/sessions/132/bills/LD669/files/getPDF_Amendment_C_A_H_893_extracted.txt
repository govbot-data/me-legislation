3 SUMMARY
44 This amendment replaces the bill, which is a concept draft, and changes the title. The
55 amendment adds Department of Transportation employees who die while performing
66 official duties to the list of eligible persons whose families may receive a death benefit
77 payment. The amendment removes the requirement in current law that the Department of
88 Administrative and Financial Services adopt rules to calculate the annual percentage
99 increase in the death benefit. The amendment requires the State to pay a death benefit to
1100 the families of Department of Transportation employees James Brown and Dwayne
1111 Campbell, who died January 13, 2026.
