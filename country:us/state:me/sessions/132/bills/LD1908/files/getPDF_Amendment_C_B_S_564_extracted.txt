16 RIGHT TO REPAIR PERSONAL DIGITAL ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT
17 §1500-PP. Right to repair
18 1. Definitions. As used in this section, unless the context otherwise indicates, the
19 following terms have the following meanings.
20 A. "Authorized repair provider" means:
21 (1) An original equipment manufacturer that offers the services of diagnosis,
22 maintenance or repair of its own personal digital electronic equipment and that
23 does not have an arrangement described in subparagraph (2) with an unaffiliated
24 individual; or
25 (2) An individual that is unaffiliated with an original equipment manufacturer and
26 that has an arrangement with the original equipment manufacturer under which the
27 original equipment manufacturer grants to the individual a license to use a trade
28 name, service mark or related characteristics for the purposes of offering the
29 services of diagnosis, maintenance or repair of personal digital electronic
30 equipment under the name of or on behalf of the original equipment manufacturer.
31 B. "Diagnosis" means the process of identifying the issue or issues causing personal
32 digital electronic equipment to not be in full working order.

1 C. "Documentation" means, with respect to personal digital electronic equipment, a
2 manual, diagram, reporting output, service code description or similar kind of
3 information required for effecting the services of diagnosis, maintenance or repair of
4 equipment manufactured or sold by an original equipment manufacturer to facilitate
5 the diagnosis, maintenance or repair of the equipment.
6 D. "Fair and reasonable terms" means, with respect to documentation, parts and tools
7 required for the diagnosis, maintenance and repair of personal digital electronic
8 equipment, that such documentation, parts and tools are made available by the original
9 equipment manufacturer at commercially reasonable costs and on commercially
10 reasonable terms that are at least as favorable as the most favorable costs and terms
11 under which the manufacturer offers the documentation, parts and tools to an
12 authorized repair provider, accounting for any discount, rebate or financial incentive
13 offered to the authorized repair provider by the manufacturer, and that:
14 (1) With respect to documentation required for repair, such documentation is made
15 available by the original equipment manufacturer:
16 (a) On terms that do not require the recipient of the documentation to be or to
17 become an authorized repair provider; and
18 (b) At no charge, except that, when the documentation is requested in physical
19 printed form, the manufacturer may require payment for the reasonable actual
20 costs of preparing and sending the printed documentation;
21 (2) With respect to tools required for repair, such tools are made available by the
22 original equipment manufacturer:
23 (a) On terms that do not require the recipient of the tool to be or to become an
24 authorized repair provider;
25 (b) Without any impediments to use; and
26 (c) At no charge for use or operation of the tool, except that when the tool is
27 requested in physical form, the manufacturer may require payment for the
28 reasonable, actual costs of procuring, preparing and sending the tool; and
29 (3) With respect to parts required for repair, such parts are made available by the
30 original equipment manufacturer either directly or through an authorized
31 distributor or authorized repair provider on terms that do not require the recipient
32 of the part to be or to become an authorized repair provider.
33 E. "Independent repair provider" means an individual or business operating in the State
34 that is not an authorized repair provider and that is not affiliated with an authorized
35 repair provider and that is engaged in diagnosing, maintaining and repairing personal
36 digital electronic equipment.
37 F. "Maintenance" means, with respect to personal digital electronic equipment, any
38 action necessary to keep currently working personal digital electronic equipment in full
39 working order.
40 G. "Modification" or "modifying" means, with respect to personal digital electronic
41 equipment, any alteration to the equipment that is not maintenance and not repair.

1 H. "Original equipment manufacturer" or "manufacturer" means a business engaged in
2 the selling or leasing of or in otherwise supplying new personal digital electronic
3 equipment manufactured by or on behalf of the business.
4 I. "Owner" means an individual or business that owns or leases personal digital
5 electronic equipment purchased or used in the State.
6 J. "Part" means, with respect to personal digital electronic equipment, any replacement
7 part or assembly of parts, either new or used, or their equivalents, for maintaining or
8 repairing personal digital electronic equipment manufactured or sold by the
9 manufacturer. "Part" does not include printed circuit board assemblies that may allow
10 device cloning in violation of 18 United States Code, Section 1029 or other applicable
11 law.
12 K. "Personal digital electronic equipment" or "equipment" means a hardware product
13 that:
14 (1) Is a computer, cellular telephone or tablet;
15 (2) Is sold at:
16 (a) Retail to an individual for personal, household, family or home office use;
17 or
18 (b) Retail or wholesale to an educational institution for educational purposes;
19 (3) Has a wholesale price to a retailer, or to others outside of direct retail sale, of
20 not less than $50; and
21 (4) Depends in whole or in part on digital electronics embedded in or attached to
22 the product for the product to function.
23 L. "Repair" means, with respect to personal digital electronic equipment, any action
24 necessary to restore the equipment to full working order. "Repair" does not include
25 post-sale modifications that alter the originally intended functioning of the personal
26 digital electronic equipment.
27 M. "Tool" means, with respect to personal digital electronic equipment, a software
28 program, hardware implement or other apparatus, or its equivalent, used for
29 diagnosing, maintaining or repairing personal digital electronic equipment
30 manufactured or sold by the manufacturer, including software or other mechanisms
31 that provide, program or pair a new part, calibrate functionality or perform any other
32 function required to repair the original part.
33 N. "Trade secret" means anything tangible or intangible or electronically stored or kept
34 that constitutes, represents, evidences or records intellectual property, including secret
35 or confidentially held designs, processes, procedures, formulas, inventions or
36 improvements or secret or confidentially held scientific, technical, merchandising,
37 production, financial, business or management information, or other intellectual
38 property that falls within the meaning of a trade secret under 18 United States Code,
39 Section 1839(3).

9 3. Additional limitations and exclusions. Notwithstanding any provision of this
10 section to the contrary:
11 A. An original equipment manufacturer is not required by this section to:
12 (1) Provide or make available documentation, parts or tools to an independent
13 repair provider or owner if:
14 (a) The part or tool is no longer available to the original equipment
15 manufacturer; or
16 (b) The documentation or tool is used by the original equipment manufacturer
17 only to perform no-cost diagnostic services virtually through telephone,
18 Internet, chat function, e-mail or similar means that do not involve the
19 manufacturer's physically handling the owner's equipment, unless the
20 manufacturer also makes that documentation or tool available to individuals
21 unaffiliated with the manufacturer;
22 (2) Divulge any trade secret;
23 (3) License any intellectual property, including copyrights or patents, to any
24 independent repair provider or owner;
25 (4) Make available to an independent repair provider or owner documentation,
26 parts or tools that would disable, reset or override electronic security locks or other
27 security-related measures or functions or disable or override antitheft security
28 measures set by the owner of the equipment without the owner's authorization.
29 This subparagraph may not be construed to relieve the original equipment
30 manufacturer’s obligation to make available to an independent repair provider or
31 owner such documentation, parts or tools if the owner provides authorization;
32 (5) Provide documentation, parts or tools for any personal digital electronic
33 equipment if reconditioning or repair of the equipment is prohibited by law,
34 regulation or building or electrical code;
35 (6) Provide documentation or tools used exclusively by the original equipment
36 manufacturer for diagnosis, maintenance or repairs completed by machines that
37 operate on several personal digital electronic equipment products simultaneously
38 or otherwise for purposes of large-scale efficiency as long as the manufacturer
39 makes available to independent repair providers and owners sufficient alternative
40 documentation and tools to diagnose, maintain or repair the equipment; or
41 (7) Warrant any repairs provided by independent repair providers or owners;

30 SUMMARY
3311 This amendment, which is the minority report of the committee, replaces the bill and
3322 changes the title. The amendment establishes a requirement that, beginning January 1,
3333 2028, an original equipment manufacturer of personal digital electronic equipment, which
3344 includes, but is not limited to, laptops, computers, cellular telephones and tablets, sold or
3355 in use in the State on or after January 1, 2028 must make available on fair and reasonable
3366 terms to any independent repair provider or owner of the equipment documentation, parts
3377 or tools required for the diagnosis, maintenance or repair of the equipment.
