18 SUMMARY
1199 This amendment replaces the bill. The amendment requires that, beginning with health
2200 plans issued or renewed on or after January 1, 2027, health insurance carriers reimburse
2211 nontransporting emergency medical service providers at 160% of the Medicare rate for
2222 covered services.
23 The amendment also provides that care that is provided at the scene of an emergency
24 medical services event by an ambulance service or nontransporting emergency medical
25 service is reimbursable care regardless of whether a patient is transported to another
26 facility, including the administration of overdose-reversing medications that do not result
27 in patient transport to a facility. The amendment specifies that a carrier is not required to
28 reimburse twice for the same service when both an ambulance service provider and a
29 nontransporting emergency medical service provider respond to the same request for
30 emergency services.
31 The amendment applies to health plans issued on or after January 1, 2027.
