11 Amend the bill by striking out the title and substituting the following:
12 'An Act to Update Juvenile Justice System Reporting Requirements and to Direct the
13 Department of Corrections and the Department of Health and Human Services to
14 Study Modifying the Long Creek Youth Development Center'

17 'Sec. 1. 34-A MRSA §7009, as enacted by PL 2023, c. 425, §4, is amended to read:
18 §7009. Publication of data
19 By February 15, 2024 Beginning August 15, 2026, each month the department shall
20 develop and publish on its publicly accessible website data regarding the number of
21 juveniles involved in the juvenile justice system. The data must be updated monthly and
22 include information regarding the numbers of juveniles referred, diverted, detained, placed
23 on probation, ordered to serve a period of confinement and committed to the department.
24 The department shall deidentify the data and remove any potential personal identifying
25 information of the juveniles. The data must be organized by region of the State and broken
26 down by age, gender and race.
27 Sec. 2. Department of Corrections and Department of Health and Human
28 Services to convene working group. The Department of Corrections and the
29 Department of Health and Human Services shall jointly convene a working group to
30 examine the current programming and use of physical facilities at the Long Creek Youth
31 Development Center, referred to in this section as "the center." The working group shall
32 provide recommendations for modifying the use of land and facilities at the center and
33 reinvesting corrections funds currently designated for youth incarceration into a continuum
34 of community-based services over the next 5 years.

19 voting member of the working group.
20 2. The working group shall:
21 A. Study efforts of other states and municipalities in other states that have modified
22 their juvenile detention centers to determine the best practices for modifying the center;
23 B. Examine the current status of the land and facilities at the center to determine
24 opportunities for modifying the land and facilities, including renovating existing
25 buildings or constructing additional facilities;
26 C. Consider alternate uses for the land and facilities at the center, including but not
27 limited to a crisis receiving center, an assessment of youth in crisis center, a youth
28 emergency shelter, youth transitional housing and a community resource center;
29 D. Examine the current use of the center’s budget and staffing and explore
30 opportunities for alternative uses of financial and human resources for services,
31 including, but not limited to, housing, behavioral health, education, substance use
32 disorder prevention and treatment, wraparound case management and diversion;
33 E. Explore existing community-based services provided to youth and work with
34 community-based organizations to determine potential programming that could take
35 place at the center; and
36 F. Examine and recommend best practices for maintaining and operating a secure and
37 safe facility for juvenile clients.

7 SUMMARY
88 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, replaces the bill and
99 changes the title. The amendment directs the Department of Corrections and the
1100 Department of Health and Human Services to jointly convene a working group to examine
1111 the current programming and use of physical facilities at the Long Creek Youth
1122 Development Center. The working group must provide recommendations for modifying
1133 the use of land and facilities at the center and reinvesting corrections funds currently
1144 designated for youth incarceration into a continuum of community-based services over the
1155 next 5 years. The working group is required to submit a report to the joint standing
1166 committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over criminal justice and public safety
1177 matters that includes the working group's findings and recommendations, including any
1188 proposed legislation related to the working group's duties. The joint standing committee
1199 may report out legislation to the Second Regular Session of the 133rd Legislature. The
2200 amendment also requires the Department of Corrections to update juvenile justice data on
2211 its publicly accessible website monthly starting August 15, 2026.
