SENATE AMENDMENT “ ” to COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “A” to S.P. 874, L.D. 2155
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment changes the title and replaces the provision of Committee
33 Amendment "A" that temporarily suspended the ability of the Bureau of Alcoholic
44 Beverages and Lottery Operations, within the Department of Administrative and Financial
55 Services, to suspend, revoke or refuse to renew a manufacturing facility license or an on-
66 premises retail license issued to a manufacturing facility licensee that was issued prior to
77 May 23, 2025 solely on the basis that the person holding a majority ownership interest in
88 the licensed manufacturing facility was determined by the bureau after the license was
99 initially issued to hold a majority ownership interest in more than the allowable number of
1100 on-premises retail licenses. Instead, this amendment permanently prohibits the bureau
1111 from suspending, revoking or refusing to renew those licenses and applies the prohibition
1122 to those licenses that were issued prior to December 21, 2025. The amendment also
1133 prohibits the sale, transfer or other conveyance of those licenses.
