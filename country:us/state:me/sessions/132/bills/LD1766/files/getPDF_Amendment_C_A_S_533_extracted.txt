10 Probate Judges into the Maine Judicial Branch”
11 Amend the bill in Part A by inserting after section 1 the following:
12 'Sec. A-2. Transition. The Supreme Judicial Court and the Chief Justice of the
13 Supreme Judicial Court shall oversee the process described in this Act for transitioning
14 from a system of elected county Probate Court Judges to a system of appointed state
15 Probate Court Judges, including by issuing any necessary administrative orders or adopting
16 or amending any applicable procedural or ethical rules.
17 Sec. A-3. Report. By January 15, 2028, the Supreme Judicial Court shall submit a
18 report to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction over judiciary
19 matters containing:
20 1. Information on progress made in planning for the establishment of a Probate Court
21 with full-time state judges on January 1, 2029, including but not limited to plans for
22 integration of the new judicial positions in the judicial branch, proposed use of existing
23 state or county facilities for probate proceedings and proposed amendments to applicable
24 procedural or ethical rules governing Probate Court proceedings and Probate Court Judges;
25 and
26 2. An evaluation of the advisability and feasibility of transferring the functions and
27 staff of the county registries of probate into the judicial branch in a manner that preserves
28 the efficient administration of justice and the ability of members of the public to pursue
29 informal probate proceedings without an attorney, including by preserving public access to
30 registry staff on a regular basis within each of the 16 counties of the State; preserving the
31 ability of registry staff to assist members of the public in drafting applications, petitions or
32 sworn statements in informal probate proceedings as described in the Maine Revised
33 Statutes, Title 18-C, section 1-510, subsection 2; and preserving the quasi-judicial authority
34 of registry staff in informal probate proceedings pursuant to the Maine Uniform Probate
35 Code.'

1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, delays by 2 years the
33 proposed transition from part-time, elected county Probate Judges to full-time, appointed
44 state Probate Judges. Under the amendment, on January 1, 2029, the first cohort of 4 full-
55 time state Probate Judges appointed by the Governor and confirmed by the Legislature will
66 begin presiding over probate proceedings in York, Kennebec, Somerset and Aroostook
77 counties. On January 1, 2031, the state Probate Court will operate statewide, presided over
88 by 9 full-time state Probate Judges appointed by the Governor and confirmed by the
99 Legislature, one of whom will be assigned by the Chief Judge of the Probate Court to serve
1100 as the primary judge in each court region of the State. The amendment also recognizes the
1111 authority of the Supreme Judicial Court and the Chief Justice of the Supreme Judicial Court
1122 to oversee the transition from part-time, elected county Probate Judges to full-time,
1133 appointed state Probate Judges, including by issuing any necessary administrative orders
1144 or adopting or amending any applicable procedural or ethical rules.
15 The amendment clarifies that during the transition period, in the counties no longer
16 served by an elected county Probate Judge, the statutory supervisory duties of the county
17 Probate Judge must be assumed by a state Probate Judge assigned by the Chief Judge of
18 the Probate Court to preside over probate proceedings in that county. Beginning January
19 1, 2031, these statutory supervisory duties will be assumed in each county by the Probate
20 Judge assigned by the Chief Judge of the Probate Court to serve as the primary judge in
21 that region of the State.
22 The amendment also incorporates an appropriations and allocations section and makes
23 technical changes to the provisions of the bill affected by the enactment of Public Law
24 2025, chapter 511.
