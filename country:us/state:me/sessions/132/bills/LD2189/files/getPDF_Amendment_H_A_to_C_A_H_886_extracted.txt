11 Commission to Evaluate the Scope of Regulatory Review and Oversight over Health Care
12 Transactions That Impact the Delivery of Health Care Services in the State”
13 Amend the amendment by inserting after the title the following:
14 'Amend the bill by inserting after the title and before the enacting clause the following:
15 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
16 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
17 Whereas, the notification required by this legislation is needed as soon as possible to
18 give affected residents adequate time to prepare for closures of labor and delivery units and
19 make other plans; and
20 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
21 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
22 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
23 therefore,' '
24 Amend the amendment by inserting after section 1 the following:
25 'Amend the bill by adding before the summary the following:
26 'Emergency clause. In view of the emergency cited in the preamble, this legislation
27 takes effect when approved.' '
28 Amend the amendment by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or
29 section number to read consecutively.
