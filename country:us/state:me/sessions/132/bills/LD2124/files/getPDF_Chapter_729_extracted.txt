H.P. 1439 - L.D. 2124
An Act to Support Emergency Shelter Funding Using Revenue from the Real
Estate Transfer Tax
Be it enacted by the People of the State of Maine as follows:
Sec. 1. 36 MRSA §4641-B, sub-§4-B, ¶E-4 is enacted to read:
E-4. Notwithstanding paragraphs E-1 and E-2, the State Controller shall first credit
$1,012,617 in fiscal year 2026-27 of the revenues available to the Maine State Housing
Authority under paragraph E-2, subparagraph (2), division (c) for the Housing
Production Fund established in Title 30-A, section 4773 to the Maine State Housing
Authority, which shall deposit the funds in the authority's shelter operating subsidy
program to support emergency homeless shelters in the State.
Sec. 2. Appropriations and allocations. The following appropriations and
allocations are made.
HOUSING AUTHORITY, MAINE STATE
Housing Production Fund N557
Initiative: Reduces allocations to reflect a one-time decrease in revenue from shifting 2%
of real estate transfer tax revenue from the Housing Production Fund program to the Maine
State Housing Authority to be used for the shelter operating subsidy program.
