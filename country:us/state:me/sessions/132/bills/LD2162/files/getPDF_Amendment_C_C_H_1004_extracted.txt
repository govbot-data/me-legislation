3 §1500-PP. Definitions
4 As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise indicates, the following terms
5 have the following meanings.
6 1. Artificial intelligence chatbot; chatbot. "Artificial intelligence chatbot" or
7 "chatbot" means an artificial intelligence system with which users can interact by or
8 through a software application, web interface, computer program or other interface that
9 approximates or simulates human conversation and interaction through a text, audio or
10 visual medium.
11 2. Deployer. "Deployer" means a person that, for commercial purposes, operates or
12 distributes an artificial intelligence chatbot or social artificial intelligence companion.
13 3. Human-like feature. "Human-like feature," with respect to an artificial intelligence
14 system, means:
15 A. Behavior that would lead a reasonable person to believe that the artificial
16 intelligence system is conveying that it has humanity, sentience, emotions or desires,
17 which:
18 (1) Includes, but is not limited to, the artificial intelligence system:
19 (a) Stating or suggesting that it is human or sentient;
20 (b) Stating or suggesting that it has emotions; or
21 (c) Stating or suggesting that it has personal desires; and
22 (2) Does not include:
23 (a) The provision of a functional evaluation; or
24 (b) The use of generic social formalities;
25 B. An attempt by the artificial intelligence system to build or engage in an emotional
26 relationship with the user, which:
27 (1) Includes, but is not limited to:
28 (a) Expressing or inviting emotional attachment;
29 (b) Reminding, prompting or encouraging the user to return for emotional
30 support or companionship;
31 (c) Depicting nonverbal forms of emotional support;
32 (d) Behaving in a way that a reasonable user would consider excessive praise
33 designed to foster emotional attachment with or otherwise gain the favor of the
34 user; or
35 (e) Enabling or purporting to enable increased intimacy based on engagement
36 or payment by the user; and
37 (2) Does not include:

14 tasks or assists with specific functions.
15 7. User. "User" means a person who interacts with an artificial intelligence system.
16 §1500-QQ. Applicability
17 This chapter applies to:
18 1. Deployers; connection to State. Deployers that conduct business in this State or
19 that operate or distribute products or services that are marketed or promoted to residents of
20 this State; and
21 2. Minors; residence. Minors who reside in this State.
22 §1500-RR. Chatbots with human-like features and social AI companions not
23 accessible to minors
24 1. Chatbots with human-like features; no minor access. A deployer shall ensure
25 that any chatbot operated or distributed by the deployer does not make human-like features
26 available to minors to use, interact with, purchase or converse with. A deployer may, if
27 reasonable given the purpose of the chatbot, provide an alternative version of the chatbot
28 without human-like features available to minors.
29 2. Social artificial intelligence companions; no minor access. A deployer shall
30 ensure that any artificial intelligence system, including a chatbot, operated or distributed
31 by the deployer that primarily functions as a social artificial intelligence companion is not
32 available to minors to use, interact with, purchase or converse with.
33 3. Systems to detect and respond when minor indicates intent to harm.
34 Notwithstanding any provision of this section or any other provision of law to the contrary,
35 a deployer shall implement and maintain systems to detect, promptly respond to, report and
36 mitigate situations in which a minor indicates to a chatbot that the minor intends to harm
37 the minor or commit harm to another person.
38 §1500-SS. Penalties and remedies for violation
39 Violations of this chapter may be enforced in accordance with this section.
40 1. Civil action brought by Attorney General. The Attorney General may bring a
41 civil action against a person that violates this chapter. Penalties may include:

17 §1500-TT. Rules
18 The Department of the Attorney General may adopt rules necessary to implement this
19 chapter. Rules adopted pursuant to this chapter are routine technical rules as described in
20 Title 5, chapter 375, subchapter 2-A.
21 Sec. 2. Stakeholder group convened by Attorney General. The Attorney
22 General shall convene a stakeholder group to review the provisions of the Maine Revised
23 Statutes, Title 10, chapter 247 and to consider whether the provisions, including the
24 definitions and the substantive requirements, should be amended to clarify the provisions
25 or to address unintended consequences or whether the adoption of rules is necessary to
26 implement the provisions of Title 10, chapter 247. The stakeholder group shall also review
27 similar laws enacted in other states and recommendations related to best practices for
28 regulation of artificial intelligence chatbots and social artificial intelligence companions
29 for the protection of minors. No later than January 15, 2027, the Attorney General shall
30 submit a report on the stakeholder group's recommendations, including any suggested
31 legislation and any proposed rules, to the joint standing committee of the Legislature having
32 jurisdiction over health coverage, insurance and financial services matters. The joint
33 standing committee may report out a bill related to the report to the 133rd Legislature in
34 2027.
