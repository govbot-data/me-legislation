13 'Sec. 6. Maine Health Data Organization and Department of Health and
14 Human Services to develop care gap plan. The Maine Health Data Organization,
15 referred to in this section as "the organization," and the Department of Health and Human
16 Services, referred to in this section as "the department," shall develop a plan to measure the
17 State's care gap. For the purposes of this section, "care gap" means the difference between
18 the home and community-based services individuals are authorized to receive and the
19 services that they actually received. The organization and the department shall do the
20 following:
21 1. Work with the department's appropriate vendors to create a supplemental file to
22 report on the care gap for services provided under the sections of MaineCare policy
23 provided under rule Chapter 101: MaineCare Benefits Manual, Chapter II, Sections 12, 17,
24 18, 19, 20, 21, 28, 29, 40, 65, 92 and 96. This file must include the authorization data
25 elements needed for the organization to link this data with the claims and eligibility data
26 that the vendor currently submits to the organization on behalf of the Office of MaineCare
27 Services within the department. The organization must have access to the authorization,
28 claims and eligibility data necessary to calculate the care gap. Using the existing data
29 governance framework, the organization shall securely integrate multiple data streams,
30 manage and store the data and develop dashboards to display key data points over time;
31 2. Work with the department's appropriate vendors to create a uniform reporting system
32 for submitting key data elements to report on the care gap for services provided under the
33 department's Office of Aging and Disability Services, Part 1 rule Chapter 5, Office of
34 Aging and Disability Services Policy Manual, Sections 61, 63, 68 and 69. These data
35 elements that may need modification to allow for data collection include, but are not limited

38 based services. The Department of Health and Human Services shall increase

43 maintain parity with similar MaineCare programs.

5 SUMMARY
66 This amendment removes all sections of the bill except for the section that adds 2
77 members to the Essential Support Workforce Advisory Committee.
8 The amendment applies a one-time cost-of-living adjustment of 3.07% for direct care
9 services provided under certain sections of the MaineCare Benefits Manual and state-
10 funded home and community-based services. The adjustment is applied beginning January
11 1, 2027.
12 The amendment replaces the requirement for the Maine Health Data Organization to
13 develop a methodology to develop a care gap report with a requirement for the Maine
14 Health Data Organization and the department to work with the department's vendors to
15 allow for the Maine Health Data Organization to develop a plan to measure the care gap in
16 essential support worker services paid by the MaineCare program or a similar state-funded
17 program. "Care gap" is defined as the difference between the services individuals are
18 authorized to receive and the services that they actually received. This follows the
19 recommendations from the report submitted pursuant to Resolve 2025, chapter 113. The
20 amendment also requires the joint standing committee of the Legislature having jurisdiction
21 over health and human services matters to determine which existing reports required by
22 statute that are submitted by the department to the committee could be amended to include
23 the ongoing care gap funding requirements.
