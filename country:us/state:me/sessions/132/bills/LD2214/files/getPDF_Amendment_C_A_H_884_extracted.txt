9 COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1493, L.D. 2214, “An Act to Confirm
10 and Finalize the Boundary Between the Town of Kittery and the Town of York”
11 Amend the bill by inserting after the title and before the enacting clause the following:
12 'Emergency preamble. Whereas, acts and resolves of the Legislature do not
13 become effective until 90 days after adjournment unless enacted as emergencies; and
14 Whereas, the communities of Kittery and York have experienced a history of border
15 disputes and disagreement over the municipal boundary line between the 2 towns; and
16 Whereas, the 2 towns have reached a mutually agreed-upon description of their
17 common boundary in an amicable manner; and
18 Whereas, it is in the public interest to immediately acknowledge the amicable
19 settlement of the boundary line dispute between the 2 towns as mutually agreed upon; and
20 Whereas, in the judgment of the Legislature, these facts create an emergency within
21 the meaning of the Constitution of Maine and require the following legislation as
22 immediately necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety; now,
23 therefore,'
24 Amend the bill by adding before the summary the following:
25 'Emergency clause. In view of the emergency cited in the preamble, this legislation
26 takes effect when approved.'
27 Amend the bill by relettering or renumbering any nonconsecutive Part letter or section
28 number to read consecutively.
29 SUMMARY
3300 This amendment amends the bill by adding an emergency preamble and emergency
3311 clause.
