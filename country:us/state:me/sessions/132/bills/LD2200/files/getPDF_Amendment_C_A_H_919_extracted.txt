5 SUMMARY
66 This amendment, which is the majority report of the committee, changes the title and
77 replaces the bill. The amendment requires that any noncompete agreement between an
88 employer and a health care practitioner that is enforceable must recognize an individual's
99 right to choose the individual's own health care practitioner.
10 Under current law, the terms of noncompete agreements between employers and
11 allopathic physicians or osteopathic physicians may take effect at any time, unlike the terms
12 of noncompete agreements for other types of employees, which may not go into effect for
13 a certain period of time. The amendment replaces the language referring to physicians in
14 current law with language referring to all types of health care practitioners.
